Marksmanship Hunter DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/08/01
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Marksmanship Hunter DPS Best in Slot Hub
Pre-Raid Best In Slot Gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 3
From Phase 1 to Phase 3, nothing really changes in terms of what is your best pre raid setup. Some new options do pop up, such as engineering goggles, but the Beastlord set is just too strong and will still dominate. Phase 2 crafted belts and Season 2 PvP gear will take over previous slots, but outside of that, not too much is different for the Marksmanship Hunter.

If you are a returning hunter from Vanilla Anniversary, technically sets from Naxxramas will be Pre-Raid BiS; but if not, the idea of newer players going back to Level 60 content isn't a reality; we have focused more on TBCC items versus the former. There are a few pieces of gear and gems you can change around based on your own personal situation. Primalstrike is better than Ebon Netherscale; however, there are Non-leatherworking options that are perfectly viable and even better than Ebon Netherscale.

For technical true Pre-Raid BiS, the Hunter would still use three specific pieces of the Cryptsalker Tier 3 pieces from Naxxramas, being the Bracers, Boots, Belt, and Ring. If you already have these 3 pieces, you can build around this with the other best options. If not, it is truly not a huge deal, but can save some time and money for those who do already have it!

With Primalstrike, you are fairly limited on sockets, so making use of the green gems Sundered Chrysoprase Sundered Chrysoprase and Jagged Chrysoprase Jagged Chrysoprase are the easiest ways to activate your meta gem Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond.

The only other important piece of information to take into consideration is your melee weapons. Mooncleaver Mooncleaver is the BiS melee weapon if you plan to melee weave. It is advised that you use a cheaper alternative weapon from dungeons/heroics and practice and see if you enjoy melee weaving and can do it properly before spending a ton of gold on leveling Blacksmithing and making Mooncleaver. If you decide that you do not want to melee weave during TBCC, your best option is to dual wield Claw of the Watcher Claw of the Watcher and Stormreaver Warblades Stormreaver Warblades and use Adamantite Weightstone Adamantite Weightstone on both.
Should Marksmanship Hunter DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
For hunters, deciding between either The Aldor or The Scryers is not that big of a deal at the end of the day. However, the former should be the faction you choose to truly min-max your character, and it will affect your Pre-BiS gearing for Phase 1. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance is ever so slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from the latter.

Besides the shoulder enchants, one of your Pre-Raid BiS rings will come from The Aldor, so that should be your indicator to go with them.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Marksmanship Hunter , take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Quivers / Ammo Pouches for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 3
Hunters are unique in that they have to use one of their bag slots specifically for their ammunition. If you use a Bow / Crossbow, then you will use a Quiver. If using a Gun, then you'll use an Ammo Pouch. Below, you can use the togglers to see which specific ones you need and can obtain in TBC Classic.

In Phase 3, we finally get a new quiver, the long-awaited Quiver of a Thousand Feathers will now be available, providing an additional 6 ammo slots.

All quiver stats share the same 15% ranged attack speed; the only difference is the slot size.

Marksmanship Hunter DPS Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/02/06
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Marksmanship Hunter DPS Best in Slot Guides
Best In Slot Gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 1
During Phase 1, Hunters have quite a few options for gear sets, depending on how hardcore or casual you want to play. In the gear planner below, the set is "Absolute" BiS and optimized for maximum DPS potential. There are a few pieces of gear and gems you can change around based on your own personal situation. The biggest obstacle that most people will run into is items that drop from World Bosses. Luckily, the alternatives do not affect the rest of the gearsets.

The only other important piece of information to take into consideration is your melee weapons. Mooncleaver Mooncleaver is the BiS melee weapon if you plan to melee weave. It is advised that you use a cheaper alternative weapon from dungeons/heroics and practice and see if you enjoy melee weaving and can do it properly before spending a ton of gold on leveling Blacksmithing and crafting it. If you decide that you do not want to melee weave during TBCC, your best option is to go with the Duel-Wield options given near the end of the article.

Marksmanship Hunters looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Hunter DPS Gems & Enchants

Should Marksmanship Hunter DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
For hunters, deciding between either The Aldor or The Scryers is not that big of a deal at the end of the day. However, the former should be the faction you choose to truly min-max your character. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance is ever so slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from The Scryers. This difference is so marginal that you really won't ever notice the dps difference in raids.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Survival Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Quivers / Ammo Pouches for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 1
Hunters are unique in that they have to use one of their bag slots specifically for their ammunition. If you use a Bow / Crossbow, then you will use a Quiver. If using a Gun, then you'll use an Ammo Pouch. Below, you can use the togglers to see which specific ones you need and can obtain in TBC Classic.

Quivers

Quivers in TBCC are limited in phase 1. If you obtained Ancient Sinew Wrapped Lamina in Classic, then you will continue to use it until later phases when larger quantity quivers are released. If you did not obtain this, then you can purchase Clefthoof Hide Quiver or Worg Hide Quiver depending on your faction.

All quiver stats share the same 15% ranged attack speed; the only difference is the slot size.

By Veramos
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.

Best In Slot Gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 2
Just like in Phase 1, Hunters have quite a few options to play around with. For certain gear slots, such as bracers/boots/legs/rings, multiple items are extremely close to each other. In the gear planner shown below, the "Absolute BiS" will be shown, and alternatives to each slot will be presented in the gear slots. In Phase 2, world boss loot is no longer BiS, however, Scaled Greaves of the Marksman are still extremely strong so if you managed to get a pair in Phase 1 you could use these the entire phase just fine while letting other hunters get Leggings of Murderous Intent or Void Reaver Greaves. You will, however, notice that, unfortunately, almost all of the "absolute BiS" items come from the final boss Lady Vashj and Kael'thas Sunstrider. Luckily, there are very close alternatives to many gear slots.

In Phase 2, there is a slight variety in our "BiS" setup, with the only differences being the ring and one gem in the belt slot. The change all depends on whether you have a Boomkin available every week to provide Improved Faerie Fire. Should you have that, then you will not gem for Hit at all. If you don't, you'll opt into Ring of Lethality and opt into 1 Hit gem on your best chest and belt options this phase.

In Phase 2, we move away from Beast Lord Armor and into Rift Stalker Armor. However, in order to make this move, you MUST have all 4 pieces of Tier 5; until that time, you will continue to wear your 4-set Beast Lord. Just like in Phase 1, an important thing to consider is your melee weapons. You'll see in our weapons section what the options there are for Melee Weaving versus those who want to opt out of that playstyle. TLDR, you'll continue to use a Two-Hand weapon for the former, and dual-wield for the latter.

Aldor vs. Scryer for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 2
In Phase 2, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 2. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Marksmanship Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Quivers / Ammo Pouches for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 2
Hunters are unique in that they have to use one of their bag slots specifically for their ammunition. If you use a Bow / Crossbow, then you will use a Quiver. If using a Gun, then you'll use an Ammo Pouch. Below, you can use the togglers to see which specific ones you need and can obtain in TBC Classic.

Quivers

Quivers in TBCC are limited in Phase 2. If you obtained Ancient Sinew Wrapped Lamina in Classic, then you will continue to use it until later phases when larger quantity quivers are released. If you did not obtain this, then you can purchase Clefthoof Hide Quiver or Worg Hide Quiver depending on your faction.

All quiver stats share the same 15% ranged attack speed; the only difference is the slot size.

Burning Crusade Classic Marksmanship Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/07/27
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for BMarksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 3
In Phase 3, we now have access to the Armor Penetration and Haste stats on gear. While there isn't enough static Haste on gear yet in Phase 3 to make it worthwhile aiming for, Armor Penetration will provide a significant boost in overall dps for Hunters. Going forward, there will be two gearsets you will want to aim for; One for 6200 armor bosses and one for 7700 armor bosses. The only difference in gearsets for Phase 3 will be your melee weapons loadout, and the 6200 set assumes you can get a 1850 rating in Arenas for the Vengeful Gladiator's Left Ripper Vengeful Gladiator's Left Ripper.

Three highly contested items that can change your set are Cursed Vision of Sargeras Cursed Vision of Sargeras, Madness of the Betrayer Madness of the Betrayer, Stormrage Signet Ring Stormrage Signet Ring for all physical dps in Phase 3. Expecting all three items to be achievable by the vast majority of players. The first-mentioned should be your highest priority in obtaining first-overall items. It can be used immediately without having to adjust any other gear slots, and you can break 4p Tier 5 with it and still have a dps increase. From there, you can replace your legs with Rift Stalker Leggings Rift Stalker Leggings if you are not already using them to further increase your DPS gain.

Just like in previous phases, an important thing to consider is your melee weapons. Hit will be an extremely difficult stat to maintain early on in Phase 3 until you can obtain certain pieces of gear, so using Halberd of Desolation Halberd of Desolation early will be very beneficial. While you don't want to use it long-term as the DPS loss is quite significant, it won't be contested by any other classes, so don't pass up on it if it drops. Once you have the supporting gear to drop Halberd of Desolation Halberd of Desolation, you should transition into Boundless Agony Boundless Agony and Blade of Infamy Blade of Infamy.

It should be noted that for your 6% set, this is a true BiS setup, and just like for everyone, achieving true BiS can seem highly unlikely. Aldor vs. Scryer for Hunter DPS in Phase 3
In Phase 3, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 3. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Marksmanship Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Marksmanship Hunter in TBC Classic Phase 3
Cursed Vision of Sargeras is absolute BiS and should be your biggest and immediate priority heading into Phase 3. While this item will be highly contested by basically every other physical dps class, there are multiple reasons why you can argue taking this over other classes first. The first being hit cap, Hunters lose the vast majority of their hit rating when dropping Tier 5 gear for Tier 6, so this item is one critical piece to maintaining cap.

The second benefit to this item is that the DPS gain is so significant over Rift Stalker Helm that you can break 4p Tier 5 for it and only have 3/5 T5 and still be ahead.

If you are unfortunate and not prioritized this item first over other classes in your guild, I highly suggest you do arena games for points and acquire Vengeful Gladiator's Chain Helm. While it doesn't provide stats anywhere near as good as the former, it does provide some additional hit rating compared to the other options of Phase 3, which will ease the DPS loss over other options available.

Gronnstalker's Helmet will be best for Survival Hunters, so you should never take this over them under any circumstances.

Best in Slot Melee Weapons for Marksmanship Hunter in TBC Classic Phase 3
Dual-Wield Weapons
Boundless Agony and Blade of Infamy are the best dual-wield weapons inPhase 3. The former should be a free pickup for Marksmanship Hunters, while the latter will be a little more contested. Tracker's Blade is an alternative off-hand that can also be used to help temporarily with Hit Rating.

If you were lucky enough to acquire Phoenix weapons in Phase 2 from Tempest Keep, then they'll be good alternatives until then.

One surprising fact is that if you gem Claw of the Watcher with two Delicate Crimson Spinel Delicate Crimson Spinel and re-enchant it with Enchant Weapon - Greater Agility, then it also becomes quite good even into Phase 3, provided you still use Adamantite Weightstone Adamantite Weightstone with it. Doing this early on, however, will not be really feasible, as epic gems will be in quite low supply and very expensive if you try to buy them yourself.

Arena weapons are incredibly more accessible in TBC Classic Anniversary, as the rating requirement has been lowered to 1700, and gaining rating in 5s isn't too challenging. Burning Crusade Classic Marksmanship Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/02/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for marksmanship Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for marksmanship Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 4
In Phase 4, there technically isn't a whole lot that changes for Marksmanship Hunters. Dagger of Bad Mojo Dagger of Bad Mojo, Signet of Primal Wrath Signet of Primal Wrath, and Berserker's Call Berserker's Call are the 3 big items that you will want to aim for in Zul'Aman. Now in an absolute BiS situation, using these pieces of gear requires a bit of gear swapping, which includes Choker of Endless Nightmares Choker of Endless Nightmares and Shadowmoon Destroyer's Drape Shadowmoon Destroyer's Drape. These two pieces of gear will still be highly contested by classes such as Warriors and Enhancement Shamans.

If you were prioritized Stormrage Signet Ring Stormrage Signet Ring, then there are alternative gearsets utilizing it that are only 3-4~ dps behind absolute BiS, and it is perfectly fine to keep using it.

Other than that, it'll be very similar to our Phase 3 BiS sets.

Marksmanship Hunters looking to gem and enchant their gear should refer to our guide below:

Aldor vs. Scryer for Hunter DPS in Phase 4
In Phase 4, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 4. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have. If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Marksmanship Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Marksmanship Hunter in TBC Classic Phase 4
Cursed Vision of Sargeras is still absolute BiS in Phase 4 and should still be one of your biggest and immediate priorities. This item will still be highly contested by basically every other physical dps class; however, there are multiple reasons why you can argue taking this over other classes first. The first being hit cap, Hunters lose the vast majority of their hit rating when dropping Tier 5 gear for Tier 6, so this item is one critical piece to maintaining cap.

The second benefit to this item is that the DPS gain is so significant over Rift Stalker Helm that you can break 4p Tier 5 for it and only have 3/5 T5 and still be ahead.

If you are unfortunate and not prioritized this item first over other classes in your guild, I highly suggest you do arena games for points and acquire Vengeful Gladiator's Chain Helm. While it doesn't provide stats anywhere near as good as the former, it does provide some additional hit rating compared to the other options of Phase 3, which will ease the DPS loss over other options available.

Gronnstalker's Helmet will be best for Survival Hunters, so you should never take this over them under any circumstances.

Burning Crusade Classic Marksmanship Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/02/18
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Marksmanship Hunter DPS in Phase 5
Phase 5 is finally here, and with that, it brings the legendary and infamous Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury. While it was previously thought to be worse than Golden Bow of Quel'Thalas Golden Bow of Quel'Thalas, that couldn't be further from the truth. Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury is actually the single biggest item upgrade you can receive as a hunter in TBC, and the dps gain ranges anywhere from 200-285dps depending on your current gear.

Moving onto the rest of the gear in Phase 5, nearly every piece of gear from Phase 4 will be replaced, except Gronnstalker's Spaulders Gronnstalker's Spaulders and Dragonspine Trophy Dragonspine Trophy. However, Dragonspine Trophy will be replaced if, and only if, you obtain both Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury and Blackened Naaru Sliver Blackened Naaru Sliver. Keep in mind that a lot of gear will be contested this phase, and achieving full BiS is extremely unrealistic. Good alternatives to take and not pass on will be pointed out in the respective gear slots, so make sure you read through everything fully.

Unfortunately, a lot of the best in slot pieces come from Kil'jaeden, and there will be a lot of competition for a lot of gear from other classes, so you will have to do your best to work with alternatives where possible.

Marksmanship Hunters looking to gem and enchant their gear should refer to our guide below:
