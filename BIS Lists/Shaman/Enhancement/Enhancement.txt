Enhancement Shaman DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/08/01
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Enhancement Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot Hub
Pre-Raid Best In Slot Gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 3
This guide gets an update as we get into Phase 3. As we potentially anticipate loot changes in Heroic dungeons in this Phase, this guide will also get updated to accommodate that once we get confirmation on that.

For now, the main updates you'll see will include PvP gear from Season 2, crafted gear from Serpentshrine Cavern, and Engineering Goggles for those who opt into that profession. For Enhancement Shamans especially, the Arena weapons will be huge, as it'll be incredibly easy to get two of these weapons, and will be the biggest increase of power a fresh level 70 could get.
Besides that, gear such as the Leatherworking Leatherworking crafted gear from Phase 1 and Badge of Justice Badge of Justice gear will still remain on this list. Because the Anniversary Servers have increased loot drop rates in Phase 1 and 2 (potentially 3 as well), when considering your pre-raid gear, prioritize the items that will not be replaced easily by overall BiS gear. Look at the gear that offers Hit and Expertise Rating, and aim for getting weapons ASAP.

Aldor vs. Scryer for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Pre-Raid
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Scryers offer a decent pre-raid leg option with Retainer's Leggings Retainer's Leggings.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Enhancement Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Pre-Raid Best in Slot Weapons and Offhands for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 3
When considering your weapons, you should aim for weapon synching, which is ideally done with two equal speed weapons so that they stay synched throughout the fight after the initial effort. This list will show you your available options for matching weapon speed in your main and offhand.

Duel-Wielding two Merciless Gladiator's Cleavers is the best option for Orcs before going into raids; for non-Orcs, it'll be the Cleaver in the off-hand, and Merciless Gladiator's Right Ripper in the main. These are the Season 2 weapons, which will be heavily discounted once Phase 3/Season 3 starts. Technically, Season 3 will be the best "Pre-Raid" option, along with being Phase 3 BiS options, but we'll be listing Season 2 due to it being quick to grab before entering raids and requiring no rating. Remember that the Anniversary Edition of TBCC makes Arena gear much more accessible; so we srongly encourage you to continue to PvP for your Season 3 weapons as well!

Dragonmaw or The Bladefist are also great, if PvPing is completely not an option. The latter can be paired with one Harvester of Souls, matching a 2.6 weapon speed. The former, however, only has one weapon to pair it with, and it's a level 61 world drop item, Fist of Reckoning. With Arena gear becoming much more accessible, we actually suggest not opting into Blacksmithing and avoiding this crafted mace, especially with Arena weapons being extremely accessible now. You can get 2.7 off-hands from raids, but the options are much more sparse than 2.6 weapons.

Note: If a weapon does not specify whether it's for the main-hand (MH) or off-hand (OH), or has x2, then it is good for both and can be duel-wielded.

Enhancement Shaman DPS Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/05/11
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Enhancement Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot Guides
Best In Slot Gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 1
In Phase 1, there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. Ideally, you will want to use the Tier 4 Head and Shoulder pieces, alongside the best off-set gear available. Using other options listed below is also fine, as there are multiple pieces with very similar stats, but try to get at least 2 pieces of Tier 4 for the set bonus. If you can, try to also do PvP for the gladiator weapons, as they are leaps and bounds ahead of any other option for the off-hand.

For gems, Meta sockets are unique to head slot pieces and accept meta gems (instead of regular gems). Only a single meta socket may exist in any given piece, and many pieces come without a meta socket at all. Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond is the best meta gem, and scales well with the rest of your gear, as it acts as a multiplier on your damage output.

Enhancement Shaman looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Enhancement Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Enhancement Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Scryers offer decent legs with Retainer's Leggings Retainer's Leggings, but isn't a determining factor worth going Scryer for.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Enhancement Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/07/14
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Enhancement Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot HubEnhancement Shaman DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot
Best In Slot Gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 2
In Phase 2, there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. Ideally, you will want to use four pieces of Tier 5 for the powerful 4-piece set bonus alongside the best off-set gear available. This is the biggest upgrade to power along with weapons that the Enhancement Shaman will get in this phase. Using other options listed below is okay, as there are individual pieces with similar stats, but the 4-set bonus, Improved Flurry, is worth chasing. All races of Enhancement Shaman can benefit a lot from getting the pvp weapons, but Orcs will benefit the most with duelwielding two Merciless Gladiator's Cleaver Merciless Gladiator's Cleaver, which are also some of the best weapons available. It is also worth noting that Phase 1's Arena weapons, like Gladiator's Cleaver Gladiator's Cleaver, will now be available without a rating requirement and for discounted Arena points, which makes it much more accessible in general.

Enhancement Shaman looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Enhancement Shaman Gems & Enchants

Should Enhancement Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Enhancement Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Weapons for Enhancement Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 2
You'll notice that there are several Blacksmithing items listed here. Currently, it is NOT one of the top recommended professions to prioritize for Enhancement Shaman. However, should you still choose to pick this profession, these weapons are going to be some of our best options.

Due to Flurry having a 0.5-second cooldown on being consumed, having weapons of matching speed is very important to minimize attack desynching and maximize your Flurry uptime. Most of the weapons we list below have either 2.6 or 2.7 speed, and weapons with the same speed are meant to be used together, whenever possible.

For Orc Enhancement Shamans, using Wicked Edge of the Planes alongside a 2.7 OH or double Merciless Gladiator's Cleaver is ideal, assuming you decided to opt into Blacksmithing, while non-Orcs should use Talon of the Phoenix and Rod of the Sun King, instead. You should favor using the axes listed in the rest of the section as Orc as well, to benefit from Axe Specialization. A strong alternative to these weapons, especially for Orcs, is Netherbane, which can be dual-wielded.

Both of the Karazhan main-hand only weapons, The Decapitator / Fool's Bane are also still strong and can combo with any of the 2.6 speed off-hands, such as The Harvester of Souls.

If you did pick blacksmithing and opted to be a Hammersmith, can use Dragonstrike from Blacksmithing Blacksmithing for main hand and Fist of Reckoning for off-hand, which is recommended for its 2.7 speed, which combos well with the crafted weapons' 2.7 speed.

Burning Crusade Classic Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/08/01
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Enhancement Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 3
Unfortunately (or fortunately, depending on how you see it), Enhancement Shaman Shaman Tier 6 set is not very good, unlike that of most classes and other Shaman specializations. On the positive side, this means you will be able to ignore the general rush for Tier 6 gear, but it also means that the drops you need are more subject to randomness than Tier tokens.

Hold on to your Tier 5 4-piece bonus for as long as possible, as it provides a large amount of DPS. Overall, you should look to swap it out when you have 2 to 3 Phase 3 replacement pieces, rather than breaking it immediately. As with every phase, doing Arena PvP is still very valuable for the Season 3 gladiator weapons, but using double Syphon of the Nathrezim Syphon of the Nathrezim for their very slow speed is your best PvE option should you not PvP. However, we will continue to encourage players to take part in Arenas in TBC Anniversary, as the requirement rating for weapons was lowered to 1700, and this is very achievable!

Meta sockets are unique to head slot pieces and accept meta gems (instead of regular gems). Only a single meta socket may exist in any given piece, and many pieces come without a meta socket at all. Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond is the best meta gem, and scales well with the rest of your gear, as it acts as a multiplier on your damage output. Should Enhancement Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

Best in Slot Weapons for Enhancement Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 3
Due to Flurry having a 0.5-second cooldown on being consumed, having weapons of matching speed is very important in order to minimize attack desynching and maximize your Flurry uptime. Most of the weapons we list below have either 2.6 or 2.7 speed, and weapons with the same speed are meant to be used together, whenever possible. Notably, Syphon of the Nathrezim can be dual-wielded and has 2.8 speed, making using two of them the best non-Orc combo available in Phase 3, but far from the only option.

For Orc Orcs, Vengeful Gladiator's Cleaver is the best weapon available, and you would ideally use two of them, but using the old Merciless Gladiator's Cleaver or any 2.6 speed off-hand is also a fine option until two can be bought. Talon of the Phoenix and Rod of the Sun King are still a very strong combo, as they were in Tier 5. Remember to favor using the axes listed in the rest of the section if you are Orc, in order to benefit from Axe Specialization.

It is important to note that the Season 2 Gladiator weapons can now be purchased without rating requirements and for discounted Arena Points, making them much easier and more reliable to get. Blacksmithing weapons are still strong, but players with them will finally be able to reliably get upgrades in the current tier.

Burning Crusade Classic Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/27
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Enhancement Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 4
Unfortunately, Enhancement Shaman Shaman Tier 6 set is not very good, unlike that of most classes and other Shaman specializations. On the positive side, this means you will be able to ignore the general rush for Tier 6 gear, but it also means that the drops you need are more subject to randomness than Tier tokens.

Hold on to your Tier 5 4-piece bonus for as long as possible, as it provides a large amount of DPS. Overall, you should look to swap it out when you have 2 to 3 best-in-slot replacement pieces, rather than breaking it immediately.

Doing Arena PvP is still very valuable for the Season 3 gladiator weapons, which are the best in slot options if you have them, but using double Syphon of the Nathrezim Syphon of the Nathrezim for their very slow speed is also a fine option, which is close in power. Phase 4 brings many gear options that are very similar to the absolute best in slot pieces, and you can take any of them and stay within 2 to 10 DPS of the best option, making it more of a cosmetic and / or RNG gear drop choice.

Meta sockets are unique to head slot pieces and accept meta gems (instead of regular gems). Only a single meta socket may exist in any given piece, and many pieces come without a meta socket at all.

Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond is the best meta gem, and scales well with the rest of your gear, as it acts as a multiplier on your damage output.

Should Enhancement Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Enhancement Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 4
Cursed Vision of Sargeras is the best helmet available by far, with impressive stats and a sleek look to go with them. Since you will be passing on all Tier 6 set pieces, this will be your main chase item alongside your weapons. You can also use Forest Prowler's Helm or Skyshatter Cover as temporary alternatives, but note that the Tier 6 helmet has much weaker throughput stats and the Tier 6 set bonuses are pretty weak for Enhancement Shaman Shamans.

Cataclysm Helm is still a great helmet, sporting great stats and a meta socket. Surestrike Goggles v2.0 are also fine, but do not contribute towards a set bonus. Alternatively, you can use Cyclone Helm, which is a strong helm for a physical damage party due to the Tier 4 2-piece set bonus.

Best in Slot Weapons for Enhancement Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 4
Due to Flurry having a 0.5-second cooldown on being consumed, having weapons of matching speed is very important in order to minimize attack desynching and maximize your Flurry uptime. Most of the weapons we list below have either 2.6 or 2.7 speed, and weapons with the same speed are meant to be used together, whenever possible. Notably, Syphon of the Nathrezim can be dual-wielded and has 2.8 speed, making using two of them the best non-Orc combo available in Phase 4, but far from the only option.

For Orc Orcs, Vengeful Gladiator's Cleaver is the best weapon available, and you would ideally use two of them, but using the old Merciless Gladiator's Cleaver or any 2.6 speed off-hand is also a fine option until two can be bought. Talon of the Phoenix and Rod of the Sun King are still a very strong combo, as they were in Tier 5. Remember to favor using the axes listed in the rest of the section if you are Orc, in order to benefit from Axe Specialization.

It is important to note that the Season 2 Gladiator weapons can now be purchased without rating requirements and for discounted Arena Points, making them much easier and more reliable to get. Blacksmithing weapons are still strong, but players with them will finally be able to reliably get upgrades in the current tier.

Burning Crusade Classic Enhancement Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/27
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Enhancement Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Enhancement Shaman DPS in Phase 5
Unfortunately, Enhancement Shaman Shaman Tier 6 set is not very good, outside of the three pieces you can get from Sunwell Plateau, which makes skipping the 4-piece bonus a strong option. Regardless, using Skyshatter Pauldrons Skyshatter Pauldrons in order to get the 4-set in a much easier way than having to loot Eredar Twins' shoulders is also a very competitive option.

Doing Arena PvP is very valuable for the gladiator weapons, which are among the best in slot options, and even the much easier to get old season weapons are very competitive, so make sure to prioritize farming them if you still need weapons! Phase 5 brings multiple gear options that are close to the absolute best in slot pieces, so feel free to diversify your gear according to what drops in your raids.

Meta sockets are unique to head slot pieces and accept meta gems (instead of regular gems). Only a single meta socket may exist in any given piece, and many pieces come without a meta socket at all.

Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond is the best meta gem, and scales well with the rest of your gear, as it acts as a multiplier on your damage output.

Enhancement Shaman looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Enhancement Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Enhancement Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

Due to Flurry having a 0.5-second cooldown on being consumed, having weapons of matching speed is very important in order to minimize attack desynching and maximize your Flurry uptime. Most of the weapons we list below have either 2.6 or 2.7 speed, and weapons with the same speed are meant to be used together, whenever possible.

Hand of the Deceiver and Mounting Vengeance are the ultimate weapons in Phase 5, having both the highest stats and two sockets each.

Dual Brutal Gladiator's Cleaver or Vengeful Gladiator's Cleaver are very potent, and one of the best alternatives, as is dual-wielding Syphon of the Nathrezim.

It is important to note that the Season 3 Gladiator weapons can now be purchased without rating requirements and for discounted Arena Points, making them much easier and more reliable to get.
