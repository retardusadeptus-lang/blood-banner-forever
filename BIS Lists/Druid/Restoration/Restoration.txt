Restoration Druid Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/01/24
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Restoration Druid Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Restoration Druid Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot Hub
Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Druid Healing in Pre-Raid
Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Tailoring as a Profession or not. The Primal Mooncloth set is extremely good, but requires Tailoring. Not only is Primal Mooncloth pre-raid BiS, but it can also be considered Phase 1 BiS as well. The Whitemend set is Pre-Raid BiS, but can be very expensive and possibly replaced with raid gear.

Many items can be considered BiS for each slot, depending on whether you play an aggressive Healing Power style or a Mana considerate style that relies more on stats like Spirit and MP5. The sections below offer detailed information for each slot.

Restoration Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Druid Healer Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
Restoration Druids favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant, Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith, from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative, Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Druids tend to have tailoring, the Aldors also offer the ability to make your own Golden Spellthread Golden Spellthread with the BoP recipe Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Restoration Druid in TBC Classic Pre-Raid
Whitemend Hood is extremely powerful and often the go-to right off the bat, as you can obtain this early. Even without the tailoring set bonus, it is best for pre-raid. However, the tailoring gear sets are quite expensive to craft or buy. Gladiator's Kodohide Helm and Cowl of Naaru Blessings are close alternatives.

Best in Slot Weapons for Restoration Druid in TBC Classic Pre-Raid
It is generally better to use a 1-handed weapon paired with an offhand instead of a 2-handed weapon.

While Gladiator's Salvation is the best pre-raid option, it is hard to obtain via PvP. Hand of Eternity is a great BoE pre-raid option available through buying or crafting via Blacksmithing. However, there are better options in raid, and the gold investment might not be worth it. Shockwave Truncheon is a cheaper alternative and still very solid. Keep in mind that Heroic dungeons might be very challenging early on.

Restoration Druid Healing Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/03/24
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Restoration Druid Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Restoration Druid Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Druid HEALING in Phase 1
Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Tailoring as a Profession or not. The Primal Mooncloth set is extremely good, but requires you to have Tailoring as a profession. Restoration Druids who are not Tailors will need to select alternate pieces from the recommended lists below.

Restoration Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Druid Healers Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
Restoration Druids favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Druids tend to have tailoring, the Aldors also offer the ability to make your own Golden Spellthread Golden Spellthread with the BoP recipe Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Restoration Druid Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Druid Healing to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot HubRestoration Druid Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Druid Healing in Phase 2
Tailoring and Leatherworking played a big role in gear during Phase 1. There is more freedom in Phase 2 to choose other professions since you can get competitive or better gear from raids.

Many items can be competitive for each slot, depending on whether you play an aggressive Healing Power style or a Mana considerate style that relies more on stats like Spirit and MP5.

Restoration Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Druid Healers Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Restoration Druids favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Druids tend to have tailoring, the Aldors also offer the ability to make your own Golden Spellthread Golden Spellthread with the BoP recipe Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/07/16
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Restoration Druid Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Druid Healing to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Druid Healing in Phase 3
In Phase 3, we finally get access to haste items. For a Resto Druid, this means you can lower your global cooldown enough and squeeze in another Lifebloom. In order to get 5 in a cycle, you need at least 113 haste, but realistically, with any server lag, you will need to reach 125 or more. Since many classes and specs will seek haste, a lot of haste gear can be highly contentious.

The Tier 6 set bonuses for Resto Druid are quite lacking. You'll want to pick up some of the tier pieces purely for the raw stats, but expect to be lower priority than other specs that get more benefit from their bonuses.

Many items can be competitive for each slot, depending on whether you play an aggressive healing power style or a mana-considerate style that relies more on stats like Spirit and MP5.

Some items have identical or close to identical alternatives like Angelista's Sash Angelista's Sash and Belt of Primal Majesty Belt of Primal Majesty. Technically, the former is 1 point of Intellect better, but the leather item is more realistic to get. Swiftheal Wraps Swiftheal Wraps and Bracers of Renewed Life Bracers of Renewed Life are another example where the items are close enough that you should aim to get whichever is easier for you and your raid team. Should Restoration Druid Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Restoration Druids favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Druids tend to have tailoring, the Aldors also offer the ability to make your own Golden Spellthread Golden Spellthread with the BoP recipe Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Restoration Druid Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Druid Healing to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Druid Healing in Phase 4
Phase 4 brings Zul'Aman as well as many new items on the Badge of Justice vendor. While this doesn't change most of what is considered BiS, it does add lots of diversity and options for alternative or catch-up gear. One of the only big changes to the BiS setup will be getting Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy from Zul'Aman.

Haste and healing power are still the most important stats for Resto Druid. Phase 4 offers many items that can aid in getting enough haste for the five global rotation and loosen the need to have either Tailoring or Leatherworking. To get the five global rotation, you'll need to get ~125 haste on your gear due to normal server latency. It can be useful to have a set of gear with less than 125 haste but high on healing power and still use the four global rotation, especially on fights where there may only be one tank actively taking damage at a time.

The Tier 6 set bonuses for Resto Druid are quite bad. You'll still want to pick up some of the tier items (Helm, Shoulders, and Chest) due to their raw stats, but expect to be a lower priority when receiving tier than other classes and specializations who gain more benefit from their bonuses.

Many items can be competitive for each slot, depending on whether you play an aggressive healing power style or a mana-conservative style that relies more on stats like Spirit and MP5. Keep in mind you only need enough mana management stats, like MP5 or Spirit, to avoid going out of mana during a pull, and you only need enough Stamina to survive an encounter. Otherwise, you should aim to get healing power and haste.

Some items have identical or close to identical alternatives like Angelista's Sash Angelista's Sash and Belt of Primal Majesty Belt of Primal Majesty. Swiftheal Wraps Swiftheal Wraps and Bracers of Renewed Life Bracers of Renewed Life are another example where the items are close enough that you should aim to get whichever is easier for you and your raid team. Or, if you have enough haste and want more healing power, you can use either Rejuvenating Bracers Rejuvenating Bracers or Bracers of Martyrdom Bracers of Martyrdom. The sections below offer more detailed information for each slot.

Should Restoration Druid Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Restoration Druids favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Druids tend to have tailoring, the Aldors also offer the ability to make your own Golden Spellthread Golden Spellthread with the BoP recipe Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Restoration Druid Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/02/22
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Restoration Druid Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Druid Healing to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Druid Healing in Phase 5
Phase 5 is the final phase of TBC and brings a lot of new gearing options. There is new raid gear from Sunwell Plateau, new Badge of Justice gear, Season 4 PvP gear, new profession gear, and new gems. In addition to the new raid gear, there is the transmute vendor Yrma who will take specific raid pieces, along with a Sunmote Sunmote, and turn them into a different item.

While there isn't a new tier set, there are additional slots for Tier 6. Bracers, belts, and boots are now available to complete the Thunderheart Raiment set. This doesn't mean much to Resto Druids who didn't really care about the set before, but these additional tier items also happen to be the best options for those slots and are worth picking up.

Professions play a more important role in Phase 5. Jewelcrafting, Tailoring, Leatherworking, and Alchemy all offer some strong gearing options. Since you can only have two professions at a time, this means you'll have to make decisions on what gear to aim for and if you want to change professions.

Haste and healing power are still the most important stats for Resto Druid. With many more options for increased Haste in Phase 5, there are some gearing considerations to be made. To get the five global rotation, you'll need to get ~118 haste on your gear due to normal server latency. Once this threshold is met, haste is still a high-value stat until the optimal threshold of ~247. Beyond that value, HoT effects lose some HPS efficiency.

Should Restoration Druid Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Restoration Druids favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Druids tend to have tailoring, the Aldors also offer the ability to make your own Golden Spellthread Golden Spellthread with the BoP recipe Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.
