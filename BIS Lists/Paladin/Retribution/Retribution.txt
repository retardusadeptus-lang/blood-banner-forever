Retribution Paladin DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By terryn
Updated: 2026/02/15
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Retribution Paladin DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Retribution Paladin DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Retribution Paladin DPS Best in Slot Hub
Pre-Raid Best In Slot Gear for Retribution Paladin DPS in Phase 1
Even moreso than any other melee DPS class, gearing for Retribution Paladin is heavily reliant on our overall Hit & Expertise rating, our two most important stats. This is because when we miss or dodge an attack, none of our seals will occur, absolutely neutering our damage. This makes Hit & Expertise worth about three times as much as it would be for any other melee class, due to how seals need to survive multiple chances to miss/dodge during a seal twist.

You want to get as close to the 9% total Hit cap and 6.5% total Expertise cap overall as you can, given your raid comp.

    Precision - This will provide 3% Hit chance from having 3/3 talent points.
    Improved Faerie Fire - This is provided by a Balance Druid, or sometimes a Resto Druid, and provides 3% Hit chance against debuffed enemies.
    Heroic Presence - If you have a Draenei Warrior/Paladin/Hunter in your party, this will provide an extra 1% Hit chance.
    Gear - The rest of your Hit chance will come from your gear.

By taking all of these factors into consideration, we will need:

    6% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision.
    3% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision and Improved Faerie Fire.
    2% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision, Improved Faerie Fire and Heroic Presence.

We will assume that you have all of this except for the Draenei buff, and therefore need 3% Hit from your gear.

Expertise is less easy to acquire and needs to come solely from your gear, but is just as important. Generally anytime you see high level melee DPS gear with Expertise on it, you want that item very badly and will most likely use it for quite a long time. Expertise is not that easy to come by as a stat, especially early in TBC, therefore we need to take it where we can.
Gemming as a Retribution Paladin
In regards to gemming correctly, there are going to be some cases where we use appropriate color sockets, with the primary focus to fulfill the 2 Yellow and 2 Blue requirement for our best Meta gem: Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond. However, we will otherwise generally gem pure Red +Strength gems like Bold Living Ruby Bold Living Ruby, unless we get more total Strength by taking the socket bonus. There is very little nuance to the gemming strategy.

Retribution Paladin Gems & Enchants
Aldor or Scryers for Retribution Paladin DPS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
The answer is that it doesn't really matter that much, but if you really want to minmax, you should choose Aldor which has a very slightly better shoulder enchant, and will be the more relevant option in the final Sunwell Plateau phase as well due to providing a better proc if you use the Shattered Sun Pendant of Might Shattered Sun Pendant of Might necklace, though it will not be best-in-slot. The Aldor shoulder enchant offers +10 attack power, whereas the Scryers shoulder enchant offers +5 crit, which may be more preferable for arena but is less preferable for PvE. The difference is minimal.

There is also a pre-raid ring that is only available to Aldor via a quest - Overseer's Signet Overseer's Signet - whereas the Scryers quest provides a tanking neck instead: Thalodien's Charm Thalodien's Charm. Neither item is very important long-term.

I would also argue that Scryers have the much better-looking tabard, and often have to pay less for the materials used to exchange for shoulder enchants.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Retribution Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Pre-Raid Best in Slot Trinkets for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 1
Bloodlust Brooch and Abacus of Violent Odds are essentially tied for the best active on-use trinket to pair with DST, and you can use either one based on your preference. Before you acquire DST, you will equip both of them; but since they are both active trinkets, you will need to stagger their use - using Brooch first with cooldowns like Avenging Wrath and Haste Potion within the first 10 seconds of Bloodlust, and Abacus last after your haste potion has dropped. This is the most effective way to use these two together and overlap them with cooldowns and Bloodlust. The Bloodlust Brooch trinket should be one of your first few purchases with Badge of Justice Badge of Justice.

Scrolls of Blinding Light is a trinket that is incredible as a substitute for Abacus if the fight if short enough, due to having a long duration and providing spell haste which shortens the length of the global cooldown for Seal spells. If the fight is short enough that you'll only get one trinket use of either trinket (sub 2-minutes), it will generally be worth using Scrolls over Abacus or Brooch. If you are able to get a second trinket use out of Brooch or Abacus at the 2-minute mark, they will usually perform better.

We will generally try to avoid using more than one active on-use trinket as they all share a short cooldown and cannot be stacked. That being said, there are still occasions where you may use two active on-use trinkets together, such as using Abacus of Violent Odds and Bloodlust Brooch together early in Phase 1 until you get Dragonspine Trophy.

Pre-Raid Best in Slot Weapons for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 1
The best weapon is a combination of weapon speed, weapon DPS, and stats. Generally, the slower the weapon is, the better it is (compared to weapons of similar item level) as it increases our proc chance for Seal of Command and therefore successful seal twists. Due to racial passives, Humans will generally want Swords or Maces more, both due to the extra Expertise they will get from using them; though this is not always strictly the case.

Lionheart Champion is the best single PvE weapon in a vacuum, locked behind being a Master Swordsmith. If you are going to be playing lots of Arena, you may instead choose to go Master Hammersmith and craft Deep Thunder, in which case that will be your best weapon in a vacuum.

However, as noted in the Chest slot section, some players may choose instead to forego both Blacksmithing weapons and go Armorsmithing for the Bulwark of Kings and its powerful 30-minute cooldown, which, when the cooldown is ready and it is combined with a powerful non-BS weapon, will provide more damage output than the Blacksmithing weapons. When the Armorsmithing cooldown is not ready, you will underperform a bit compared to the Blacksmithing weapons. It is a trade-off; higher peak DPS every 30-minutes, lower average DPS outside of that.

Additionally, Twinblade of the Phoenix in Phase 2 is strong enough to rival the upgraded Phase 2 Blacksmithing weapons like Lionheart Executioner; meaning that if you can acquire it, Twinblade will make the weaponsmithing profession specializations irrelevant for PvE damage - at which point you'd be much better off with the Armorsmithing chestpiece. You can even continue using the Armorsmithing chestpiece in Phases 3 + 4 by equipping it when the cooldown is ready and taking it off when it isn't.

Additionally, many high-rated Arena players choose to use World Breaker in Phase 2 over the iconic Stormherald, so Hammersmithing may also lack the longevity that you'd expect from such a weapon.

You can switch between Blacksmithing specializations for the cost of 100 gold. This means you can also craft the Weaponsmithing weapons and the Armorsmithing chest and switch between them as you like -- such as crafting Lionheart Champion and then eventually switching to Armorsmithing if you get Twinblade in Phase 2, which will be a common strategy.

Outside of the weaponsmithing weapons, your best non-BS weapon is going to be the Season 1 Gladiator's Greatsword until you are able to start getting raid weapons.

Singing Crystal Axe is a really solid weapon due to the +400 haste proc, though it falls behind the BiS weapons due to lower item level and faster 3.5 speed.

This guide will list the recommended gear for Retribution Paladin DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Retribution Paladin DPS in Phase 1
Even moreso than any other melee DPS class, gearing for Retribution Paladin is heavily reliant on our overall Hit & Expertise rating, our two most important stats. This is because when we miss or dodge an attack, none of our seals will occur, absolutely neutering our damage. This makes Hit & Expertise worth about three times as much as it would be for any other melee class, due to how seals need to survive multiple chances to miss/dodge during a seal twist.

You want to get as close to the 9% total Hit cap and 6.5% total Expertise cap overall as you can, given your raid comp.

    Precision - This will provide 3% Hit chance from having 3/3 talent points.
    Improved Faerie Fire - This is provided by a Balance Druid, or sometimes a Resto Druid, and provides 3% Hit chance against debuffed enemies.
    Heroic Presence - If you have a Draenei Warrior/Paladin/Hunter in your party, this will provide an extra 1% Hit chance.
    Gear - The rest of your Hit chance will come from your gear.

By taking all of these factors into consideration, we will need:

    6% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision.
    3% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision and Improved Faerie Fire.
    2% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision, Improved Faerie Fire and Heroic Presence.

We will assume that you have all of this except for the Draenei buff, and therefore need 3% Hit from your gear.

Expertise is less easy to acquire and needs to come solely from your gear or racial passive, but it is just as important. Generally, anytime you see high-level melee DPS gear with Expertise on it, you want that item very badly and will most likely use it for quite a long time. Expertise is not that easy to come by as a stat, especially early in TBC, and we need to take it where we can.
Gemming as a Retribution Paladin
In regards to gemming correctly, there are going to be some cases where we use appropriate color sockets, with the primary focus to fulfill the 2 Yellow and 2 Blue requirement for our best Meta gem: Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond. However, we will otherwise generally gem pure Red +Strength gems like Bold Living Ruby Bold Living Ruby, unless we get more total Strength by taking the socket bonus. There is very little nuance to the gemming strategy.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 1
Dragonspine Trophy, commonly referred to as "DST", is your primary best-in-slot trinket and will be used well into the final raid, Sunwell Plateau, as a non-Human, or for the entire expansion as a Human. This is your best trinket, no questions, providing a massive damage boost to seal twisting Retribution Paladins that rivals the damage gain of the very best DPS classes. Do not let anyone tell you that you are a bad candidate for receiving this trinket; and if you are skilled enough to seal twist well during the haste proc, it is one of the most important items you can acquire during this phase.

Additionally, other classes have new alternative options with every new phase such as Tsunami Talisman, Warp-Spring Coil or Madness of the Betrayer, causing them to gain less and less from DST as the phases progress; that is simply not the case for Retribution Paladin, who is always stuck using Abacus of Violent Odds without DST and cannot use any of the alternatives, causing us to gain more and more DPS from this trinket as the phases progress.

If people tell you that this DST trinket is not that good for Retribution, or is way better for some other class, they are simply wrong. That being said, this trinket provides a massive, random haste rating increase that will require you to adjust quickly in your seal twisting rotation - it takes practice and skill to use this trinket properly.

Bloodlust Brooch and Abacus of Violent Odds are essentially tied for the best active on-use trinket to pair with DST, and you can use either one based on your preference. Before you acquire DST, you will equip both of them; but since they are both active trinkets, you will need to stagger their use - using Brooch first with cooldowns like Avenging Wrath and Haste Potion within the first 10 seconds of Bloodlust, and Abacus last after your haste potion has dropped. This is the most effective way to use these two together and overlap them with cooldowns and Bloodlust. The Bloodlust Brooch trinket should be one of your first few purchases with Badge of Justice Badge of Justice.

Scrolls of Blinding Light is a trinket that is incredible as a substitute for Abacus if the fight is short enough, due to having a long duration, but it has a longer cooldown as well. This means that on longer fights where you would get two or more uses of Abacus or Brooch, they will win out; but on shorter fights where you will only have one trinket usage, Scrolls may outperform them. The other great thing about Scrolls is that it and provides spell haste as well, which shortens the length of the global cooldown for Seal spells and allows us to twist more easily at faster melee swing speeds, which is incredibly potent during the initial Bloodlust window.

If the fight is short enough that you'll only get one trinket use out of either trinket (sub 2-minutes), it will generally be worth using Scrolls over Abacus. If you are ables to get a second trinket use out of Abacus at the 2-minute mark, it will usually perform better.

We will generally try to avoid using more than one active on-use trinket as they all share a short cooldown and cannot be stacked. That being said, there are still occasions where you may use two active on-use trinkets together, such as Abacus of Violent Odds and Bloodlust Brooch together early in Phase 1 until you get Dragonspine Trophy.

Mark of the Champion is a decent passive trinket option for Undead/Demon bosses if you have access to it. Kiss of the Spider and Slayer's Crest from Naxxramas also continue to be decent trinket options as a fresh 70 if you have them.

Best in Slot Weapons for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 1
The best weapon is a combination of weapon speed, weapon DPS, and stats. Generally, the slower the weapon is, the better it is (compared to weapons of similar item level) as it increases our proc chance for Seal of Command and therefore successful seal twists. Due to racial passives, Humans will generally want Swords or Maces more, both due to the extra Expertise they will get from using them, though this is not always strictly the case.

Lionheart Champion is the best single PvE weapon in a vacuum, locked behind being a Master Swordsmith. If you are going to be playing lots of Arena, you may instead choose to go Master Hammersmith and craft Deep Thunder, in which case that will be your best weapon in a vacuum.

However, as noted in the Chest slot section, some players may choose instead to forego both Blacksmithing weapons and go Armorsmithing for the Bulwark of Kings and its powerful 30-minute cooldown, which, when the cooldown is ready and it is combined with a powerful non-BS weapon like Ethereum Nexus-Reaver or Gorehowl, will provide more damage output than the Blacksmithing weapons. When the Armorsmithing cooldown is not ready, you will underperform a bit compared to the Blacksmithing weapons. It is a trade-off; higher peak DPS every 30-minutes, lower average DPS outside of that.

Additionally, Twinblade of the Phoenix in Phase 2 is strong enough to rival the upgraded Phase 2 Blacksmithing weapons like Lionheart Executioner; meaning that if you can acquire it, Twinblade will make the weaponsmithing professions specializations irrelevant for PvE damage - at which point you'd be much better off with the Armorsmithing chestpiece. You can even continue using the Armorsmithing chestpiece in Phases 3 + 4 by equipping it when the cooldown is ready and taking it off it isn't ready.

Additionally, many high-rated Arena players choose to use World Breaker in Season 2 Arena over the iconic Stormherald, so Hammersmithing may also lack the longevity that you'd expect from such a weapon.

You can switch between Blacksmithing specializations for the cost of 100 gold. This means you can also craft the Weaponsmithing weapons and the Armorsmithing chest and switch between them as you like -- such as crafting Lionheart Champion and then eventually switching to Armorsmithing if you get Twinblade in Phase 2, which will be a common strategy.

Ethereum Nexus-Reaver is the best non-Blacksmithing weapon if you have access to World Bosses and their drops. Hammer of the Naaru is roughly equivalent for Humans, but falls slightly behind Gorehowl for everyone else. Gorehowl is the next-best option for non-Humans.

Retribution Paladin DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic

Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep: The Eye, Serpentshrine Cavern, Karazhan, Gruul's Lair and Magtheridon's Lair for Retribution Paladin DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Retribution Paladin DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Retribution Paladin DPS in Phase 2
Even moreso than any other melee DPS class, gearing for Retribution Paladin is heavily reliant on our overall Hit & Expertise rating, our two most important stats. This is because when we miss or dodge an attack, none of our seals will occur, absolutely neutering our damage. This makes Hit & Expertise worth about three times as much as it would be for any other melee class, due to how seals need to survive multiple chances to miss/dodge during a seal twist.

You want to get as close to the 9% total Hit cap and 6.5% total Expertise cap overall as you can, given your raid comp.

    Precision - This will provide 3% Hit chance from having 3/3 talent points.
    Improved Faerie Fire - This is provided by a Balance Druid, or sometimes a Resto Druid, and provides 3% Hit chance against debuffed enemies.
    Heroic Presence - If you have a Draenei Warrior/Paladin/Hunter in your party, this will provide an extra 1% Hit chance.
    Gear - The rest of your Hit chance will come from your gear.

By taking all of these factors into consideration, we will need:

    6% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision.
    3% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision and Improved Faerie Fire.
    2% hit chance from gear with 3/3 Precision, Improved Faerie Fire and Heroic Presence.

We will assume that you have all of this except for the Draenei buff, and therefore need 3% Hit from your gear.

Expertise is less easy to acquire and needs to come solely from your gear or racial passive, but it is just as important. Generally, anytime you see high-level melee DPS gear with Expertise on it, you want that item very badly and will most likely use it for quite a long time. Expertise is not that easy to come by as a stat, especially early in TBC, and we need to take it where we can.
Gemming as a Retribution Paladin
In regards to gemming correctly, there are going to be some cases where we use appropriate color sockets, with the primary focus to fulfill the 2 Yellow and 2 Blue requirement for our best Meta gem: Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond. However, we will otherwise generally gem pure Red +Strength gems like Bold Living Ruby Bold Living Ruby, unless we get more total Strength by taking the socket bonus. There is very little nuance to the gemming strategy.

Aldor or Scryers for Retribution Paladin DPS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
The answer is that it doesn't really matter that much, but if you really want to minmax, you should choose Aldor which has a very slightly better shoulder enchant, and will be the more relevant option in the final Sunwell Plateau phase as well due to providing a better proc if you use the Shattered Sun Pendant of Might Shattered Sun Pendant of Might necklace, though it will not be best-in-slot. The Aldor shoulder enchant offers +10 attack power, whereas the Scryers shoulder enchant offers +5 crit, which may be more preferable for arena but is less preferable for PvE. The difference is minimal.

There is also a pre-raid ring that is only available to Aldor via a quest - Overseer's Signet Overseer's Signet - whereas the Scryers quest provides a tanking neck instead: Thalodien's Charm Thalodien's Charm. Neither item is very important long-term.

I would also argue that Scryers have the much better-looking tabard, and often have to pay less for the materials used to exchange for shoulder enchants.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Retribution Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Chest Armor for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 2
The best chestpiece is the crafted Armorsmithing chest, the Bulwark of Ancient Kings, when combined with Twinblade of the Phoenix.

Just like the Swordsmithing and Hammersmithing weapons, this chestpiece requires Nether Vortex Nether Vortex to craft; though it only requires two of them instead of the five vortexes the weapons require, making it easier to craft early on in the phase. The reason the Armorsmithing chest is so good is because of the on-use effect that effectively provides you with a third trinket effect, that is notably powerful on short fights; but has a long 30-minute cooldown.

The other reason it is so good is because in Phase 2 there is a weapon -- Twinblade of the Phoenix -- that is roughly equal to the crafted Swordsmithing weapon Lionheart Executioner. Since those two weapons are approximately equal, it stands to reason that Twinblade + Bulwark of Ancient Kings is better than Lionheart alone.

However, for Armorsmithing to compete with Lionheart Executioner, you will need to actually acquire Twinblade first.

The two alternatives to the Armorsmithing chest are the Tier 5 Crystalforge Breastplate and the Krakken-Heart Breastplate, both of which are roughly equal to each other in performance and compete against the Armorsmithing chest when it doesn't have the 30-minute cooldown ready. Just like last phase, our tier set bonuses are pretty worthless.

Best in Slot Waist Armor for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 2
The belt slot has now become an Expertise slot as well, with the addition of the Belt of One-Hundred Deaths; commonly referred to as "100D".

This is an incredibly powerful and high-demand melee DPS item that everybody wants, though it will provide you with more DPS than any other melee DPS class due to the insanely high value we place on Expertise as a stat, making Retribution Paladin a fantastic candidate to receive one of the first belts that drop. This belt will be used all the way until you acquire the Tier 6.5 belt in Sunwell Plateau during Phase 5, and has no remotely close competition. Best in Slot Trinkets for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 2
Dragonspine Trophy, commonly referred to as "DST", is your primary best-in-slot trinket and will be used well into the final raid, Sunwell Plateau, as a non-Human, or for the entire expansion as a Human. This is your best trinket, no questions, providing a massive damage boost to seal twisting Retribution Paladins that rivals the damage gain of the very best DPS classes. Do not let anyone tell you that you are a bad candidate for receiving this trinket; and if you are skilled enough to seal twist well during the haste proc, it is one of the most important items you can acquire during this phase.

Additionally, other classes have new alternative options with every new phase such as Tsunami Talisman, Warp-Spring Coil or Madness of the Betrayer, causing them to gain less and less from DST as the phases progress; that is simply not the case for Retribution Paladin, who is always stuck using Abacus of Violent Odds without DST and cannot use any of the alternatives, causing us to gain more and more DPS from this trinket as the phases progress.

If people tell you that this DST trinket is not that good for Retribution, or is way better for some other class, they are simply wrong. That being said, this trinket provides a massive, random haste rating increase that will require you to adjust quickly in your seal twisting rotation - it takes practice and skill to use this trinket properly.

The other trinket slot should generally be an active on-use trinket, which can be either Abacus of Violent Odds, Bloodlust Brooch, or Scrolls of Blinding Light; though most people will generally favor using Bloodlust Brooch due to the ease of use and the ease of obtaining it.

Scrolls of Blinding Light is slightly better than Abacus during the first use due to having a longer duration, but it has a longer cooldown as well. This means that on longer fights where you would get two or more uses of Abacus or Brooch, they will win out; but on shorter fights where you will only have one trinket usage, Scrolls will outperform them. The other great thing about Scrolls is that it provides spell haste as well, which reduces the global cooldown on our seal spells and allows us to twist more easily at faster melee swing speeds, which is incredibly potent during the initial Bloodlust window.

A good rule of thumb is that the fight should be 1-minute in length or shorter in order to use Scrolls, but it depends on other variables as well, such as having DST or not.

The problem with Hourglass of the Unraveller and Tsunami Talisman is that their uptime is just simply not great for us, and therefore neither can beat Abacus.

Best in Slot Weapons for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 2
The two best weapons are the crafted Swordsmithing weapon Lionheart Executioner and the Twinblade of the Phoenix from Kael'thas Sunstrider. While they are roughly equal, if you consider the fact that Twinblade allows you to drop weaponsmithing professions to go Armorsmithing, then the combination of Twinblade + Armorsmithing is wins out over Lionheart alone. However, for Armorsmithing to compete against Weaponsmithing, you first need to acquire a Twinblade.

The two crafted weaponsmithing weapons each require five Nether Vortex Nether Vortex to craft, obtained from Tier 5 trash mobs and raid bosses.

If you went Hammersmithing for PvP reasons, then Stormherald will fall roughly ~2% DPS behind the two swords, but is still a great option. The slow 3.8 speed of Stormherald helps to make up for the places where it is otherwise lacking, and brings it much closer to the swords.

World Breaker is both a great option for PvE and PvP alike, as the critical strike proc can actually apply to multiple swings or seal hits when they occur as part of a single seal twist. Paired with the slow 3.7 speed of the weapon, this makes it incredibly bursty, which is heavily favored in arena combat -- notably potent as Reckoning build, where you will almost always dump the proc into a seal twist. If you never took the opportunity to craft Stormherald, then this is going to be one of your best options for a PvP weapon, on top of being a strong PvE weapon for those without a Twinblade or LHE; holding it back in PvE is the lower weapon damage, and the fact that in the PvE rotation the proc can be eaten by Crusader Strike or Judgement instead of a seal twist.

Burning Crusade Classic Retribution Paladin DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By terryn
Updated: 2026/02/11
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Retribution Paladin DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Retribution Paladin DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Retribution Paladin DPS in Phase 3
As with any other DPS class, gearing for Retribution Paladin is heavily reliant on your overall Hit rating. You want to get as close to 9% hit overall with your raid comp. However, the Paladin Talent Precision, a Draenei's passive racial group buff Heroic Presence, and a Balance Druid's Improved Faerie Fire can lower your required Hit from gear to 4%. Most of the "Best" options provided in this guide are made assuming the first factor only: 6% Hit from gear with only 3/3 in our Precision talent, as this is the standard Retribution Spec option, and not all raids will have Balance Druid. With that in mind, if you have higher Hit from other pieces, some pieces will devalue because they are putting you over the Hit cap and are not as effective.

In regards to gemming correctly, there are going to be some cases where we use appropriate color sockets, with the primary focus to fulfill the 2 Yellow and Blue requirements for our best Meta gem: Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond. However, we will typically always gem pure Strength, unless the bonus for gemming correctly awards additional Attack Power or Strength correctly. There is a little more nuance to Ret Paladin gemming versus some other classes.

Retribution Paladins looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Retribution Paladin Gems & Enchants
Should Protection Paladin DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
With Tier 6 / Phase 3, there is an almost negligible difference between Aldor and Scryer. This decision does not matter until Phase 5 (Sunwell Plateau). I have Aldor listed as that is what you will do eventually in those later phases, but for now, the overall effect doesn't matter.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Retribution Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 3
For Tier 6, Cursed Vision of Sargerad is your absolute BIS helm. The level of hit allows you to basically disregard Hit on all other slots and ditch the hit helm enchant for the Lower City one, which gives you more overall Attack Power and gives you a better mana pool. However, due to the competition to get it, you may be inclined to look into something else. Forest Prowler's Helm is going to be your #2, providing an absurd amount of Critical Strike Rating, some raw Attack Power, but less overall when compared to CVOS, and will have considerably less competition over CVOS. After that its Helm of the Illidari Shatterer > Lightbringer War-Helm. The latter will pull ahead if you're running a lot of other hit pieces, but those sets will provide overall much less damage, which is why the tier helm isn't even recommended for acquiring 2p t6. After that, your old Furious Gizmatic Goggles is your 5th bis, so basically aim to upgrade this into your preferred tier helm.

Burning Crusade Classic Retribution Paladin DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By terryn
Updated: 2026/02/28
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Retribution Paladin DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Retribution Paladin DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Retribution Paladin DPS in Phase 4
As with any other DPS class, gearing for Retribution Paladin is heavily reliant on your overall Hit rating. You want to get as close to 9% hit overall with your raid comp. However, the Paladin Talent Precision, a Draenei's passive racial group buff Heroic Presence, and a Balance Druid's Improved Faerie Fire can lower your required Hit from gear to 4%. Most of the "Best" options provided in this guide are made assuming the first factor only: 6% Hit from gear with only 3/3 in our Precision talent, as this is the standard Retribution Spec option, and not all raids will have Balance Druid. With that in mind, if you have higher Hit from other pieces, some pieces will devalue because they are putting you over the Hit cap and are not as effective.

Tier 6 has two set bonuses:
2 Piece: Mana Regen Proc
4 Piece: Improved Hammer of Wrath

The 2-piece is proving to be very strong, a great boost to mana, allowing for more use of Consecration, keeping mana higher during trash so you don't have to drink as much, etc. The 4-piece is a total trap until Wrath of the Lich King pre-patch. Hammer of Wrath is a dps loss to use in almost all situations, and increasing its damage by 10% still doesn't do anything. When you want to run the 2p, however, you are locked into one of two paths. Running chest: Lightbringer Breastplate Lightbringer Breastplate and legs: Lightbringer Greaves Lightbringer Greaves or running chest: Lightbringer Breastplate Lightbringer Breastplate and shoulders: Lightbringer Shoulderbraces Lightbringer Shoulderbraces. Sims have shown that running the Chest/Shoulders combo when Shoulderpads of the Stranger Shoulderpads of the Stranger provides only 2 expertise value instead of 3 (remember expertise rating provides value in discrete increments rather than single increments), whereas chest / pants is better when Shoulderpads of the Stranger Shoulderpads of the Stranger provides 3 expertise. A note is that running Chest/Shoulders allows you to continue running Bow-stitched Leggings Bow-stitched Leggings, giving you a bigger mana pool, letting you run more consecration, which is kind of the point of running 2p t6. Either scenario will really mess with your hit values, so you will need to add some off-gemming to whichever piece you're using to make sure you're at hit cap.

Overall, there is a measured damage decrease running these two-tier sets (more information to come as the ret simulation matures, but currently about 20-30 dps), but your major advantage will bethat the added mana pool will increase your sustain through an instance.

In regards to gemming correctly, there are going to be some cases where we use appropriate color sockets, with the primary focus to fulfill the 2 Yellow and Blue requirements for our best Meta gem: Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond. However, we will typically always gem pure Strength, unless the bonus for gemming awards additional Attack Power or Strength correctly. There is a little more nuance to Ret Paladin gemming versus some other classes.

Retribution Paladins looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Retribution Paladin Gems & Enchants
Should Protection Paladin DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
With ZA / Phase 4, there is an almost negligible difference between Aldor and Scryer. This decision does not matter until Phase 5 (Sunwell Plateau). I have Aldor listed as that is what you will do eventually in those later phases, but for now, the overall effect doesn't matter.
Best in Slot Head Armor for Retribution Paladin in TBC Classic Phase 4
For Tier 6, Cursed Vision of Sargerad is your absolute BIS helm. The level of hit allows you to basically disregard Hit on all other slots and ditch the hit helm enchant for the Lower City one, which gives you more overall Attack Power and gives you a better mana pool. However, due to the competition to get it, you may be inclined to look into something else. Forest Prowler's Helm is going to be your #2, providing an absurd amount of Critical Strike Rating, some raw Attack Power, but less overall when compared to CVOS, and will have considerably less competition over CVOS. After that its Helm of the Illidari Shatterer > Lightbringer War-Helm. The latter will pull ahead if you're running a lot of other hit pieces, but those sets will provide overall much less damage, which is why the tier helm isn't even recommended for acquiring 2p t6. After that, your old Furious Gizmatic Goggles is your 5th bis, so basically aim to upgrade this into your preferred tier helm.

Burning Crusade Classic Retribution Paladin DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By terryn
Updated: 2026/02/28
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Retribution Paladin DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Retribution Paladin DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Retribution Paladin DPS in Phase 5
With BIS, you are primarily going to be aiming for a massive haste set. We want to full send haste outside of one or two items that are just absurdly good in the other stats. There are some simulations that show a full Armor Penetration set is actually approximately equal to the haste set; the pieces are so highly contested that it is not actually going to be recommended in this guide. If you're interested in more information, please check out Surv's video on the matter here

As with any other DPS class, gearing for Retribution Paladin is heavily reliant on your overall Hit rating. You want to get as close to 9% hit overall with your raid comp. However, the Paladin Talent Precision, a Draenei's passive racial group buff Heroic Presence, and a Balance Druid's Improved Faerie Fire can lower your required Hit from gear to 4%. Most of the "Best" options provided in this guide are made assuming the first factor only: 6% Hit from gear with only 3/3 in our Precision talent, as this is the standard Retribution Spec option, and not all raids will have Balance Druid. With that in mind, if you have higher Hit from other pieces, some pieces will devalue because they are putting you over the Hit cap and are not as effective.

Tier 6 has two set bonuses:
2 Piece: Mana Regen Proc
4 Piece: Improved Hammer of Wrath

The 2-piece is proving to be very strong, a great boost to mana, allowing for more use of Consecration, keeping mana higher during trash so you don't have to drink as much, etc. The 4-piece is a total trap until Wrath of the Lich King pre-patch. Hammer of Wrath is a dps loss to use in almost all situations, and increasing its damage by 10% still doesn't do anything. When you want to run the 2p, however, you are locked into one of two paths. Running chest: Lightbringer Breastplate Lightbringer Breastplate and legs: Lightbringer Greaves Lightbringer Greaves or running chest: Lightbringer Breastplate Lightbringer Breastplate and shoulders: Lightbringer Shoulderbraces Lightbringer Shoulderbraces. Sims have shown that running the Chest/Shoulders combo when Shoulderpads of the Stranger Shoulderpads of the Stranger provides only 2 expertise value instead of 3 (remember expertise rating provides value in discrete increments rather than single increments), whereas chest / pants is better when Shoulderpads of the Stranger Shoulderpads of the Stranger provides 3 expertise. A note is that running Chest/Shoulders allows you to continue running Bow-stitched Leggings Bow-stitched Leggings, giving you a bigger mana pool, letting you run more consecration, which is kind of the point of running 2p t6. Either scenario will really mess with your hit values, so you will need to add some off-gemming to whichever piece you're using to make sure you're at hit cap.

Overall, there is a measured damage decrease running these two-tier sets (more information to come as the ret simulation matures, but currently about 20-30 dps), but your major advantage will bethat the added mana pool will increase your sustain through an instance.

In regards to gemming correctly, there are going to be some cases where we use appropriate color sockets, with the primary focus to fulfill the 2 Yellow and Blue requirements for our best Meta gem: Relentless Earthstorm Diamond Relentless Earthstorm Diamond. However, we will typically always gem pure Strength, unless the bonus for gemming awards additional Attack Power or Strength correctly. There is a little more nuance to Ret Paladin gemming versus some other classes.
