In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Arcane Mage DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Arcane Mage DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Arcane Mage DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best-In-Slot Gear for Arcane Mage DPS in Pre-Raid
There are a ton of different ways to gear an Arcane Mage, and they all depend on the gear you have available, as well as some personal preferences. Between all the different powerful rings, trinkets, necks, and even a few armor items here and there, you can make adjustments and still do some of the best damage possible.

Arcane Mages looking to add enchants and gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Arcane Mage Gems & Enchants
Should Arcane Mage DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
The Aldor vs Scryer choice for Mages is not super important. The key thing to keep in mind is the enchants, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline and Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. They are very, very similar, which overall makes the choice not super impactful—we're talking 0.x% in favor of Aldor.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Arcane Mages, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best-in-Slot Trinkets for Arcane Mages in TBC Classic Pre-Raid
The two trinkets recommended here are both easy to obtain and the best options aside from the Karazhan The Lightning Capacitor trinket, making them both last a very long time, even once you start raiding. Making most of the alternative trinkets somewhat irrelevant, with the exception of two.

The Mind Quickening Gem, although not best in slot, is by far the best AoE trinket, given it can be used on demand and swapped in and out between trash packs. Additionally, the Mark of the Champion can perform as well as the best in slot options, but requires the enemy to be either undead or a demon - making it quite limited for very little gain even on those.

Overall, if you're trying to maximize your damage, it's recommended to gather multiple trinkets and switch them around on different fights.

Best-in-Slot Weapons for Arcane Mages in TBC Classic Pre-Raid
One-handed weapons paired with a solid off-hand are the go-to choice for Mages, especially due to the fact that the best in slot off-hand is so easy to obtain through badges of justice. Simply put, they give more of the useful stats, like Spell Power and Crit, than staves. As such, you should be aiming to stick with a one-hand/off-hand duo as often as possible.

When it comes to one-handed weapons overall, I recommend using the Stormcaller or Blade of the Archmage (faction dependent) since it performs the best out of the farmable options, and is eventually guaranteed via reputation. The Eternium Runed Blade performs very slightly better, but can cost a lot of gold, which is better spent on the main Spellfire set, enchants, or consumables.

Specifically against Demon enemies such as Magtheridon or Prince Malchezaar, the Illidari-Bane Mageblade is great to have and use, at least until you replace both of your weapons by the phase 1 best options.

Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Arcane Mage DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Arcane Mage DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Arcane Mage DPS Best in Slot Guides

Best In Slot Gear for Arcane Mage DPS in Phase 1
There are a ton of different ways to gear an Arcane Mage, and they all depend on the gear you have available, as well as some personal preferences. Between all the other powerful Rings, Trinkets, Necks, and even a few armor items here and there, you can make adjustments and still do some of the best damage possible.

Arcane Mages looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Arcane Mage Gems & Enchants
Should Arcane Mage DPS choose Aldor or Scyer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
The Aldor vs Scryer choice for Mages is not super important. The key thing to keep in mind is the enchants, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline and Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. They are very similar, which overall makes the choice not particularly impactful - we're talking 0.1% here in favor of Aldor.

The Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem trinket that Scryer allows you to buy gets brought up a lot related to this topic, but because this trinket is essentially useless due to the Hit being unnecessary and there being better trinkets to get before raid makes this irrelevant for your choice.

All in all, I'd either recommend Aldor if you care about a marginal amount of DPS, or whichever has the cheapest reputation turn-in items.

By Foolipe
Updated: 2026/05/12
Foolipe
Offline on Twitch

Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Arcane Mage DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Arcane Mage DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Arcane Mage DPS Best in Slot HubArcane Mage DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Arcane Mage DPS in Phase 2
There are a ton of different ways to gear an Arcane Mage and they all depend on the gear you have available, as well as some personal preferences. Between all the different powerful Rings, Trinkets, Necks, and even a few armor items here and there, you can make adjustments and still do some of the best damage possible.

Arcane Mages looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Arcane Mage Gems & Enchants
Should Arcane Mage DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
The Aldor vs Scryer choice for Mages is not super important. The key thing to keep in mind is the enchants, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline and Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. They are very similar, which overall makes the choice not particularly impactful - we're talking 0.1% here in favor of Aldor.

The Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem trinket that Scryer allows you to buy gets brought up a lot related to this topic, but because this trinket is essentially useless due to the Hit being unnecessary and there being better trinkets to get before raid makes this irrelevant for your choice.

All in all, I'd either recommend Aldor if you care about a marginal amount of DPS, or whichever has the cheapest reputation turn-in items.

The only exception to that is the Destruction Holo-gogs, which don't drop from the raids, but instead can be crafted with engineering, these perform similarly to tier 4 headpiece, making it a solid early gearing option to players wanting to get caught up quickly.

Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Arcane Mage DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Arcane Mage DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Arcane Mage DPS in Phase 3
Approaching how to gear your Arcane Mage can be challenging, as there are a lot of options and every stat is valuable in its own way, especially with spell haste now being added into the mix, which is an incredibly strong stat but has its own value that can fluctuate based on the rest of your gear.

Depending on your goals, your gear set can look entirely different. While some players might focus on their boss performance, others pivot their attention towards overall damage and build their set with AoE as its central point. An important thing to remember is that even though an AoE set might outperform every other option for its intended goal, it also underperforms when it comes to bosses. Because of this, small gear swaps between specific situations have a great impact.

Should Arcane Mage DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
The Aldor vs Scryer choice for Mages is not super important. The main difference, particularly at this stage of TBC, to keep in mind is the enchants, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline and Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. They're extremely close when it comes to output, but to summarize, you should make your decision based on what you focus on - Aldor pushes ahead by about 2-3 DPS in single target, while Scryer outperforms by a similar amount in AoE scenarios.

Meanwhile, Scryer does offer better items, most notably Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which is a strong hit trinket. However, this is not as important for Arcane due to a lower hit requirement, and it will be replaced once you are geared.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Arcane Mages, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Arcane Mage DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Arcane Mage DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Arcane Mage DPS in Phase 4
Approaching how to gear your Arcane Mage can be challenging, as there are a lot of options and every stat is valuable in its own way, especially with the recent inclusion of spell haste, which is now being reinforced with Zul'Aman gear and some new items purchasable using Badge of Justice Badge of Justice.

It's important to remind ourselves that we don't always have every piece of gear that we want, be it because our guild can't clear a specific boss, or because we're low priority for big items. With this in mind, don't get discouraged, as options are plenty and Arcane Mage is pretty forgiving with what you can get outside of a few pieces that are nowadays fairly easy to get.

First, I want to touch on how important the Tirisfal Regalia is; it has two amazing set bonuses - the first one provides a flat 20% increase to the damage of your Arcane Blast, and the second gives a bonus 70 spell power when you crit, which will have very high uptime in single-target and basically 100% uptime on AoE encounters.

Secondly, I want to emphasize that spell haste is amazingly strong and one of our most valuable stats, but don't tunnel vision on it, as sometimes other items without it are just as good or better, depending on how much they provide. Each stat is valuable in its own way, and that value constantly shifts as you change your gear around.

One of a mage's main roles in a raid is overall performance, which means your damage throughout the entire raid, instead of focusing on just bosses. This is a big factor related to your value towards a group since a full raid consists mostly of trash packs, and the bosses are only a small part of it. Maybe that's not your thing, and that's okay as well. If it is your thing, one of the most significant changes you can make to your play style is trinket-swapping; several trinkets will allow for new heights in your overall damage. Here are some of them and some quick notes:

    Mind Quickening Gem Mind Quickening Gem continues to be the most powerful AoE trinket, even though it's now eight phases old.
    The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan has a similar use to Mind Quickening Gem but is equipped with a lower cooldown, allowing for more uses.
    Ashtongue Talisman of Insight Ashtongue Talisman of Insight is the best static trinket in your collection and should almost always be equipped in AoE situations since it'll have 100% uptime in AoE.
    Serpent-Coil Braid Serpent-Coil Braid is worth using when your Mana Emerald Mana Emerald is available, as it then provides 225 spell power and has crit built into it. This is one of the best trinkets as long as the pack you're fighting is below the AoE cap.
    Hex Shrunken Head Hex Shrunken Head is great if you're under the AoE cap as it's basically a better Icon of the Silver Crescent Icon of the Silver Crescent
    The Lightning Capacitor The Lightning Capacitor has its niche spot for situations where you're AoE capped (usually 14+ targets), which allows you to add damage above that point through its Lightning Bolts.
    Sextant of Unstable Currents Sextant of Unstable Currents isn't especially best at anything, but it's consistent at what it does, it provides 40 spell crit and has a really good 190 spellpower proc.

Should Arcane Mage DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
The Aldor vs Scryer choice for Mages is not super important. The main difference, particularly at this stage of TBC, to keep in mind is the enchants, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline and Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. They're extremely close when it comes to output, but to summarize, you should make your decision based on what you focus on - Aldor pushes ahead by about 2-3 DPS in single target, while Scryers outperforms by a similar amount in AoE scenarios.

Meanwhile, Scryers does offer better items, most notably Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which is a strong hit trinket. However, this is not as important for Arcane due to a lower hit requirement, and it will be replaced once you are geared.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Arcane Mages, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Arcane Mage DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Arcane Mage DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Arcane Mage DPS in Phase 5
Approaching how to gear your Arcane Mage can be challenging, as there are a lot of options and every stat is valuable in its own way, especially with the wide range of encounter lengths in Sunwell Plateau, where the strength of stats can vary a fair bit. I built this guide with the most realistic setup that'll perform the best in both long and short fights outside of a couple of pieces of loot, which will be explained further in the item rankings.

With Sunwell Plateau's release, we get a raid that is quite unlike previous ones, since it does not have much trash within it. Combined with the fact that our single-target BiS set already contains so much spell haste to begin with, giving up spell crit or spell power in favor of more spell haste just isn't as worthwhile anymore.

One thing that's still very worth thinking about is trinket swapping, as rotating through a few select trinkets can have a massive impact on your performance regardless of the raid's content. Here are the ones that are still within reason to use and why:

    Mind Quickening Gem Mind Quickening Gem continues to be the most powerful AoE trinket, but shouldn't be combined with other haste abilities and actives.
    The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan has a similar use to Mind Quickening Gem but is equipped with a lower cooldown, allowing for more uses.
    Ashtongue Talisman of Insight Ashtongue Talisman of Insight is the best static trinket in your collection and should almost always be equipped in AoE situations since it'll have 100% uptime in AoE.
    Shifting Naaru Sliver Shifting Naaru Sliver is now available, and it provides the biggest spellpower boost in TBC, along with some spell haste.
    Serpent-Coil Braid Serpent-Coil Braid is worth using when your Mana Emerald Mana Emerald is available, as it then provides 225 spell power and has crit built into it. This is one of the best trinkets as long as the pack you're fighting is below the AoE cap.
    Hex Shrunken Head Hex Shrunken Head is great if you're under the AoE cap as it's basically a better Icon of the Silver Crescent Icon of the Silver Crescent
    The Lightning Capacitor The Lightning Capacitor has its niche spot for situations where you're AoE capped (usually 14+ targets), which allows you to add damage above that point through its Lightning Bolts.
    Sextant of Unstable Currents Sextant of Unstable Currents isn't especially best at anything, but it's consistent at what it does, it provides 40 spell crit and has a really good 190 spellpower proc.

Should Arcane Mage DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
The Aldor vs Scryer choice for Mages is not super important. The main difference, particularly at this stage of TBC, to keep in mind is the enchants, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline and Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. They're extremely close when it comes to output, but to summarize, you should make your decision based on what you focus on - Aldor pushes ahead by about 2-3 DPS in single target, while Scryers outperforms by a similar amount in AoE scenarios.

Meanwhile, Scryers does offer better items, most notably Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which is a strong hit trinket. However, this is not as important for Arcane due to a lower hit requirement, and it will be replaced once you are geared.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Arcane Mages, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Amulet of Unfettered Magics is best in slot mostly for Horde players, specifically if using the Shifting Naaru Sliver Shifting Naaru Sliver and Hex Shrunken Head Hex Shrunken Head combo, as you are likely to need the hit rating. If you're still spell hit capped through other items before you get your entire best in slot set, then Brooch of Nature's Mercy is still better regardless.

Brooch of Nature's Mercy is better in any case where you're hit capped. Alliance players have an easier gearing path here and can just entirely skip trying to get Amulet of Unfettered Magics. Another good example is if you're using The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan combined with one of the former two trinkets for your set as Horde.

Eye of the Night and Chain of the Twilight Owl are both still worth noting as they provide group-wide buffs which have greater impact than the single target performance of other necks. These can and should be coordinated with your raid or group for high uptime during end-game content.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Arcane Mage in TBC Classic Phase 5
In Phase 5, there are three different best trinket setups, depending on faction and fight duration:

    Shifting Naaru Sliver and Hex Shrunken Head is great for longer fights, since the Sliver's cooldown matches up with that of your other big cooldowns like Icy Veins and Arcane Power. This setup is best when you're spell hit capped, meaning it's great for Alliance players, or in the case of using different spell hit items elsewhere, such as Amulet of Unfettered Magics Amulet of Unfettered Magics, also good for Horde players.
    Shifting Naaru Sliver and The Skull of Gul'dan is a great trinket setup for Horde players due to the extra spell hit - it also enables you to use Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy as your best in slot neck instead.
    The Skull of Gul'dan and Hex Shrunken Head performs similarly to the previous combination, it has the same advantages towards gearing for Horde players due to the spell hit allowing for different items in other slots. This setup is slightly harder to play but easier to obtain, as both trinkets were already in-game before Phase 5.

Other Trinket Recommendations

There are plenty of other trinkets that are still useful in niche cases or until you acquire the best trinket setups:

    Ashtongue Talisman of Insight can be combined with Sextant of Unstable Currents for good synergy - this combo has some of the highest performance potential in extremely short fights.
    The Lightning Capacitor continues to be one of the best trinkets if capped in AoE situations and still a great early trinket that can be used temporarily.
    Icon of the Silver Crescent and Dark Iron Smoking Pipe are straight downgrades of Hex Shrunken Head, but they're still useful early trinkets to use in higher-end raids until you get better.
