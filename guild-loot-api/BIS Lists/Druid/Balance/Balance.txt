Balance Druid DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/01/29
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Balance Druid DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Balance Druid DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Balance Druid DPS in Pre-Raid
Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Tailoring as a Profession or not. The Spellfire set is extremely good, but requires Tailoring. Balance Druids who are not Tailors will need to select alternate pieces from the recommended lists below. A lot of this set remains BiS even through Phase 1 raids.

Balance Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Balance Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Balance Druid DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
Choosing either Aldor or Scryer for Balance is fine and really comes down to a few options that can sway you in one direction. Regarding Phase 1 BiS gear, choosing The Scryers gives access to Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which can help reach the cap for hit rating. If you are Aldor, you can swap out this trinket, but you will have to make up for the hit rating elsewhere, such as with gems. You want to aim for 12% hit rating since you can get 4% from talents in the Balance tree. You can potentially get away with less depending on your party setup, but it's generally a good idea to have a set of gear that reaches 12% on your own.

The Aldor offer a better shoulder enchant with Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, slightly edging out Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. On the other hand, Scryer Tailors are also able to pick up and make their own Runic Spellthread Runic Spellthread, although the finished product can be bought and sold on the Auction House. In the long run, taking future phases into account, the Shattered Sun Offensive trinket proc off of Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is better if you are aligned with The Aldor.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Balance Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Balance Druid DPS Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/02/01
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Balance Druid DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Balance Druid DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot Guides

Best In Slot Gear for Balance Druid DPS in Phase 1
Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Tailoring as a Profession or not. The Spellfire set is extremely good, but requires Tailoring. Balance Druids who are not Tailors will need to select alternate pieces from the recommended lists below.

Balance Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Balance Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Balance Druid DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
Choosing either Aldor or Scryer for Balance is fine and really comes down to a few options that can sway you in one direction. Regarding Phase 1 BiS gear, choosing The Scryers gives access to Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which can help reach the cap for hit rating. If you are Aldor, you can swap out this trinket, but you will have to make up for the hit rating elsewhere, such as with gems. You want to aim for 12% hit rating since you can get 4% from talents in the Balance tree. You can potentially get away with less depending on your party setup, but it's generally a good idea to have a set of gear that reaches 12% on your own.

The Aldor offer a better shoulder enchant with Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, slightly edging out Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. On the other hand, Scryer Tailors are also able to pick up and make their own Runic Spellthread Runic Spellthread, although the finished product can be bought and sold on the Auction House. In the long run, taking future phases into account, the Shattered Sun Offensive trinket proc off of Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is better if you are aligned with The Aldor.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Balance Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

I am a Moonkin Main with full Spellfire and Spellstrike set and I think it was a trap.

Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Balance Druid DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Balance Druid DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot HubBalance Druid DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Balance Druid DPS in Phase 2
During Phase 1, gear was more dependent on whether you chose to have Tailoring or Leatherworking. In Phase 2, there is more freedom to choose other professions, but this means you'll have to compete with more raid gear.

Balance Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Balance Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Balance Druid DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Choosing either Aldor or Scryer for Balance is fine and really comes down to a few options that can sway you in one direction. Regarding Phase 2 BiS gear, choosing The Scryers gives access to Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which can help reach the cap for hit rating. If you are Aldor, you can swap out this trinket, but you will have to make up for the hit rating elsewhere, such as with gems. You want to aim for 12% hit rating since you can get 4% from talents in the Balance tree. You can potentially get away with less depending on your party setup, but it's generally a good idea to have a set of gear that reaches 12% on your own.

The Aldor offer a better shoulder enchant with Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, slightly edging out Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. On the other hand, Scryer Tailors are also able to pick up and make their own Runic Spellthread Runic Spellthread, although the finished product can be bought and sold on the Auction House. In the long run, taking future phases into account, the Shattered Sun Offensive trinket proc off of Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is better if you are aligned with The Aldor.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Balance Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/07/31
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Balance Druid DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Balance Druid DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Should Balance Druid DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Choosing either Aldor or Scryer for Balance is fine and really comes down to a few options that can sway you in one direction. Regarding Phase 2 BiS gear, choosing The Scryers gives access to Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which can help reach the cap for hit rating. If you are Aldor, you can swap out this trinket, but you will have to make up for the hit rating elsewhere, such as with gems. You want to aim for 12% hit rating since you can get 4% from talents in the Balance tree. You can potentially get away with less depending on your party setup, but it's generally a good idea to have a set of gear that reaches 12% on your own.

The Aldor offer a better shoulder enchant with Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, slightly edging out Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. On the other hand, Scryer Tailors are also able to pick up and make their own Runic Spellthread Runic Spellthread, although the finished product can be bought and sold on the Auction House. In the long run, taking future phases into account, the Shattered Sun Offensive trinket proc off of Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is better if you are aligned with The Aldor.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Balance Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Balance Druid DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Balance Druid DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Balance Druid DPS in Phase 4
Phase 4 brings Zul'Aman as well as upgrades to the Badge of Justice vendor. The BiS items remain mostly the same with the exception of Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy and Hex Shrunken Head Hex Shrunken Head from Zul'Aman. There is also a new competitive ring option Mana Attuned Band Mana Attuned Band which has both haste and hit although this ring is only better if you need the hit rating or can't get your hands on two Ring of Ancient Knowledge Ring of Ancient Knowledge.

The Tier 6 set bonuses for Balance are quite good and should be sought after. Until you can get the full 4-set of Tier 6, it is still worth wearing the 4-set from Tier 5.

Balance Druids looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Balance Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Balance Druid DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Choosing either Aldor or Scryer for Balance is fine and really comes down to a few options that can sway you in one direction. Regarding Phase 2 BiS gear, choosing The Scryers gives access to Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which can help reach the cap for hit rating. If you are Aldor, you can swap out this trinket, but you will have to make up for the hit rating elsewhere, such as with gems. You want to aim for 12% hit rating since you can get 4% from talents in the Balance tree. You can potentially get away with less depending on your party setup, but it's generally a good idea to have a set of gear that reaches 12% on your own.

The Aldor offer a better shoulder enchant with Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, slightly edging out Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. On the other hand, Scryer Tailors are also able to pick up and make their own Runic Spellthread Runic Spellthread, although the finished product can be bought and sold on the Auction House. In the long run, taking future phases into account, the Shattered Sun Offensive trinket proc off of Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is better if you are aligned with The Aldor.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Balance Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Balance Druid DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Kyxie
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Balance Druid DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Balance Druid DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Balance Druid DPS in Phase 5
Phase 5 is the final phase of TBC and brings a lot of new gearing options. There is new raid gear from Sunwell Plateau, new Badge of Justice gear, Season 4 PvP gear, new profession gear, and new gems. In addition to the new raid gear, there is the transmute vendor Yrma who will take specific raid pieces, along with a Sunmote Sunmote, and turn them into a different item.

While there isn't a new tier set, there are additional slots for Tier 6. Bracers, belts, and boots are now available to complete the Thunderheart Regalia set. This opens a lot of gearing options, and you'll definitely want to pick up these new pieces. With these additional items, you can equip both 4-set Tier 6 and 4-set Tier 5, but this is not as strong as picking up 4-set Tier 6 along with powerful off-pieces from Sunwell Plateau.

Professions play a more important role in Phase 5. Jewelcrafting, Tailoring, and Leatherworking all offer some strong gearing options. Since you can only have two professions at a time, this means you'll have to make decisions on what gear to aim for and if you want to change professions. Jewelcrafting and Enchanting are really strong profession choices for personal player power, but Leatherworking and Tailoring are also solid and can be situationally beneficial and possibly better for the raid team, especially Leatherworking for the drums.

Balance Druid Gems & Enchants
Should Balance Druid DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Choosing either Aldor or Scryer for Balance is fine and really comes down to a few options that can sway you in one direction. Regarding Phase 2 BiS gear, choosing The Scryers gives access to Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, which can help reach the cap for hit rating. If you are Aldor, you can swap out this trinket, but you will have to make up for the hit rating elsewhere, such as with gems. You want to aim for 12% hit rating since you can get 4% from talents in the Balance tree. You can potentially get away with less depending on your party setup, but it's generally a good idea to have a set of gear that reaches 12% on your own.

The Aldor offer a better shoulder enchant with Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, slightly edging out Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. On the other hand, Scryer Tailors are also able to pick up and make their own Runic Spellthread Runic Spellthread, although the finished product can be bought and sold on the Auction House. Finally available in Phase 5, the Shattered Sun Offensive proc of Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is better if you are aligned with The Aldor; however, this neck is not BiS, just a strong option while gearing.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Balance Druids, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.
