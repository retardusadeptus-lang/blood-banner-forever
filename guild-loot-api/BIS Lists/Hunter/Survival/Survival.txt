Survival Hunter DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/08/01
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Survival Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Survival Hunter DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Survival Hunter DPS Best in Slot Hub
Pre-Raid Best In Slot Gear for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 3
From Phase 1 to Phase 3, nothing really changes in terms of what your best pre-raid setup is. Some new options do pop up, such as engineering goggles, but the Beastlord set is just too strong and will still dominate. Phase 2 crafted belts and Season 2 PvP gear will take over previous slots, but outside of that, not too much is different for the Survival Hunter. In a TLDR: Agility and Critical Strike Rating are king.

During pre-raid, Hunters have quite a few options for gear sets, depending on how hardcore or casual you want to play. For Survival Hunters, there is a lot less hit rating you need to worry about due to the talent Surefooted.

Survival Hunters looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Should Survival Hunter DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
For hunters, deciding between either The Aldor or The Scryers is not that big of a deal at the end of the day. However, The Aldor should be the faction you choose to truly min-max your character. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance is ever so slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from The Scryers. This difference is so minor and only a few dps difference between the few, so for the average player, you really won't ever notice the dps difference in raids.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Survival Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Weapons for Survival Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 3
Hunters can opt into either using Duel Wield Weapons or a Two-Hand Weapon for additional stats outside of their ranged weapon. Opting into the latter means you'll want to do additional damage with "Melee Weaving", where you will deal melee damage from our Two-Hand Weapon in between your auto-shots. We'll be going over all the available options for the Survival Hunter below.
Duel Wield Weapons

Quivers / Ammo Pouches for Survival Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 3
Hunters are unique in that they have to use one of their bag slots specifically for their ammunition. If you use a Bow / Crossbow, then you will use a Quiver. If using a Gun, then you'll use an Ammo Pouch. Below, you can use the togglers to see which specific ones you need and can obtain in TBC Classic.

In Phase 3, we finally get a new quiver; the long-awaited Quiver of a Thousand Feathers will now be available, providing an additional 6 ammo slots.

All quiver stats share the same 15% ranged attack speed; the only difference is the slot size.

Survival Hunter DPS Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/02/01
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Survival Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Survival Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Survival Hunter DPS Best in Slot Guides
Best In Slot Gear for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 1
During Phase 1, Hunters have quite a few options for gear sets, depending on how hardcore or casual you want to play. For survival hunters, there is a lot less hit rating you need to worry about due to the talent Surefooted. 3 specific items drop from the Karazhan basement random boss, which have an extremely low drop chance of rolling the "of Agility" attribute and therefore are extremely difficult to obtain. If your guild agrees to spend the time each week going for the belt/bracers/boots for you, then by all means, try and get them.

One major difference compared to beast mastery hunters is that you want Legacy over Mooncleaver. Even without melee weaving, Legacy will provide your raid with a small benefit over using fist weapons Claw of the Watcher Claw of the Watcher and Stormreaver Warblades Stormreaver Warblades with Adamantite Weightstone Adamantite Weightstone on both.

Survival Hunters looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Hunter DPS Gems & Enchants

Should Survival Hunter DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
For hunters, deciding between either The Aldor or The Scryers is not that big of a deal at the end of the day. However, the Aldor should be the faction you choose to truly min-max your character. The shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance is ever so slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from The Scryers. This difference is so minor and only a few DPS difference between the two, so for the average player, you really won't notice the DPS difference in raids.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Survival Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Quivers / Ammo Pouches for Survival Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 1
Hunters are unique in that they have to use one of their bag slots specifically for their ammunition. If you use a Bow / Crossbow, then you will use a Quiver. If using a Gun, then you'll use an Ammo Pouch. Below, you can use the togglers to see which specific ones you need and can obtain in TBC Classic.

Quivers

Quivers in TBCC are limited in phase 1. If you obtained Ancient Sinew Wrapped Lamina in Classic, then you will continue to use it until later phases when larger quantity quivers are released. If you did not obtain this, then you can purchase Clefthoof Hide Quiver or Worg Hide Quiver depending on your faction.

All quiver stats share the same 15% ranged attack speed; the only difference is the slot size.

Survival Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Survival Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Survival Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.

Best In Slot Gear for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 2
In Phase 2, you will notice that the Survival gearset is almost completely identical to Beast Mastery. This is because the gear in Phase 2 that Beast Mastery Hunters also use just naturally has a high amount of Agility. The biggest difference in gearing for Survival is that you will use Netherbane instead of Talon of the Phoenix for your main hand, and your T5 offset piece is also different, as well as your gems will primarily center around all Agility gems.

For melee weaving in Phase 2 as Survival, you want Twinblade of the Phoenix over Bloodmoon for the additional 24 Agility that it would provide.

Survival Hunters looking to gem and enchant their BiS gear should refer to our guide below:

Survival Hunter DPS Gems & Enchants

Aldor vs. Scryer for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 2
In Phase 2, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 2. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Survival Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Quivers / Ammo Pouches for Survival Hunter DPS in TBC Classic Phase 2
Hunters are unique in that they have to use one of their bag slots specifically for their ammunition. If you use a Bow / Crossbow, then you will use a Quiver. If using a Gun, then you'll use an Ammo Pouch. Below, you can use the togglers to see which specific ones you need and can obtain in TBC Classic.

Quivers

Quivers in TBCC are limited in Phase 2. If you obtained Ancient Sinew Wrapped Lamina in Classic, then you will continue to use it until later phases when larger quantity quivers are released. If you did not obtain this, then you can purchase Clefthoof Hide Quiver or Worg Hide Quiver depending on your faction.

All quiver stats share the same 15% ranged attack speed; the only difference is the slot size.

Burning Crusade Classic Survival Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/07/27
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Survival Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Survival Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 3
In Phase 3, Survival Hunters have it quite a bit easier than Beast Mastery for a few main reasons. The biggest being that you will not need or want two of the highest contested items: Cursed Vision of Sargeras and Stormrage Signet Ring. The second advantage is that only requiring 3% hit makes gearing up and transitioning into Tier 6 gear much easier.

Halberd of Desolation by itself can cover over half of the required Hit Rating early on. This is a very good early option to pick up while you work on acquiring two Blade of Infamy. Madness of the Betrayer is a significant gain for Survival Hunters to acquire early on, as it will be the item that allows you to drop Halberd of Desolation.

Unlike Beast Mastery Hunters, you won't need to look to PvP for weapons, as taking double Blades of Infamy will award the most Agility. However, should you want to be able to swap to Beast Mastery easier, you can look to get the weapons so you don't have to compete for Boundless Agony . As with Beast Mastery Hunters, your best option for breaking 4p Tier 5 early is using the Gronnstalker's Gloves and Gronnstalker's Spaulders.

Aldor vs. Scryer for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 3
In Phase 3, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 3. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Survival Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Survival Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/02/13
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Survival Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Survival Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 4
In Phase 4, Survival Hunters have it quite a bit easier than Beast Mastery again. The lower requirement for hit cap makes gearing much simpler. You do, however, have two ways of going about gearing, and both will be shown below in the gear planner. The first and recommended gearset balances both personal dps and raid-wide dps gain through Expose Weakness while the second setup focuses primarily on personal dps.

Halberd of Desolation Halberd of Desolation by itself can cover over half of the required hit rating early on. This is a very good early option to pick up while you work on acquiring two Dagger of Bad Mojo Dagger of Bad Mojo. Madness of the Betrayer Madness of the Betrayer is a significant gain for Survival Hunters to acquire early on, as it will be the item that allows you to drop Halberd of Desolation Halberd of Desolation. It will also be a big ticket item, still depending on which gearing route you choose to go.

As with Beast Mastery Hunters, your best option for breaking 4p Tier 5 early is using the Gronnstalker's Gloves Gronnstalker's Gloves and Gronnstalker's Spaulders Gronnstalker's Spaulders.

Survival Hunters looking to gem and enchant their BiS gear should refer to our guide below:

Survival Hunter DPS Gems & Enchants

Aldor vs. Scryer for Hunter DPS in Phase 4
In Phase 4, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 4. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Survival Hunters, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Survival Hunter DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Veramos
Updated: 2026/02/22
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Survival Hunter DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Survival Hunter DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Survival Hunter DPS in Phase 5
Phase 5 is finally here, and with that, it brings the legendary and infamous Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury. While it was previously thought to be worse than Golden Bow of Quel'Thalas Golden Bow of Quel'Thalas, that couldn't be further from the truth. Thori'dal, the Stars' Fury is actually the single biggest item upgrade you can receive as a hunter in TBC, and the dps gain ranges anywhere from 200-285dps depending on your current gear.

Moving onto the rest of the gear in Phase 5, nearly every piece of gear from Phase 4 will be replaced, except Gronnstalker's Spaulders Gronnstalker's Spaulders and Dragonspine Trophy Dragonspine Trophy. One thing you will notice for Phase 5 is that Survival Hunters will gear almost the same as Beast Mastery, with the only exceptions being that some alternatives are based purely around personal DPS gain instead of boosting your Expose Weakness as much as possible.

Unfortunately, a lot of the best-in-slot pieces come from Kil'jaeden, and there will be a lot of competition for gear from other classes, so you will have to do your best to work with alternatives where possible.

Beast Mastery Hunters looking to gem and enchant their gear should refer to our guide below:

Beast Mastery Hunter DPS Gems & Enchants
Aldor vs. Scryer for Hunter DPS in Phase 5
In Phase 5, The Aldor is still the best faction choice for Hunters; choosing The Scryers is not the end of the world, as the difference is very minor. There are no new key items that we benefit from in Phase 5. The only relevant item is still the shoulder enchant Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance or Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade.

Besides the shoulder enchants, there is nothing else of relevance for hunters in terms of raid gear or enchants that we "must" have.
