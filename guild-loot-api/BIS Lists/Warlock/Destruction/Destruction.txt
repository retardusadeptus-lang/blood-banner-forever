By Zephan
Updated: 2026/08/02
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Destruction Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Destruction Warlock DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Destruction Warlock DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Destruction Warlock DPS in Pre-Raid in Phase 3
In Phase 3, a couple of additions will come to Pre-Raid BiS. The primary three changes will be Season 2 PvP gear, Engineering Goggles, and Phase 2 crafted belts. Outside of that, not much will change until Phase 4 badge gear. We'll discuss priority in Pre-Raid gear as well, as with pre-raid, you either want to get as powerful as possible before entering the raids, or you want to achieve the minimal amount of power that will get you into a raid.

Due to the differences in specialization talents, variety of stats on gear, and bonuses from other players in the party, there's no single best set of gear appropriate for all situations. Hit chance plays an enormous role in determining the gear you want to use, as missing a piece of hit gear or having extra hit from talents, gems, or buffs will result in changing other pieces of gear to maintain that hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but some important things to consider:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

Should Destruction Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Destruction Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc. That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. And Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Destruction Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Weapons for Destruction Warlock in TBC Classic Pre-Raid in Phase 3
Merciless Gladiator's Spellblade, the Season 2 PvP weapon, will be your best weapon to get in terms of power and accessibility. Previous season gear gets heavily discounted and can be obtained after a week of doing your required 10 Arena games. However, you technically can get the Season 3 weapon, Vengeful Gladiator's Spellblade Vengeful Gladiator's Spellblade, before entering raids, but it does require a 1700 rating and way more points. Although it arguably is not too challenging to achieve this rating in 5v5 Arenas, we still will put the previous season weapon on here, primarily in terms of access, But did want to note the reader of Season 3 as well.

Illidari-Bane Mageblade is a great option versus Demon targets which you'll encounter a lot in Phase 3 Raids. Besides those targets, Blade of Wizardry and Stormcaller/Blade of the Archmage are the best options. The latter will be expensive, the former will require a hefty reputation grind.

Two-handed staves provide a lot of stats, but generally not enough to outweigh the combination of competitively sourced one-hand/offhand combinations during Phase 1. Atiesh, Greatstaff of the Guardian is an outlier in that regard, however, due to the buffs it provides you and your party, increasing everyone's Spell Damage by 33. If you are one of the lucky few to have obtained the weapon in Classic, it will remain relevant until Phase 3.

Burning Crusade Classic Destruction Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 1 Gear Guide
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/04/15
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Warlock DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Warlock DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Destruction Warlock DPS in Phase 1
Due to the differences in specialization talents, variety of stats on gear, and bonuses from other players in the party, there's no single best set of gear appropriate for all situations. Hit chance plays an enormous role in determining the gear you want to use, as missing a piece of hit gear or having extra hit from talents, gems, or buffs will result in changing other pieces of gear to maintain that hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but some important things to consider:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

Should Destruction Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Destruction Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on Faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this Phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Destruction Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Destruction Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Destruction Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Destruction Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Destruction Warlock DPS Best in Slot HubDestruction Warlock DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Destruction Warlock DPS in Phase 2
Due to the differences in specialization talents, variety of stats on gear, and bonuses from other players in the party, there's no single best set of gear appropriate for all situations. Hit chance plays an enormous role in determining the gear you want to use, as missing a piece of hit gear or having extra hit from talents, gems, or buffs will result in changing other pieces of gear to maintain that hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but some important things to consider:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

Before we dive in, I should mention that when gearing in Phase 2, the four-piece set bonus of Corruptor Raiment only works on base damage. The value also drops off on multi-target fights, as you would need to Shadow Bolt multiple targets, which we can't. Also, once there is movement, you also get a lower uptime. All this combined makes it not worth getting.

Secondly, Fire Destruction Warlocks may be wondering when it is worthwhile to break Wrath of Spellfire. A simple answer to this is that if you get either Vestments of the Sea-Witch Vestments of the Sea-Witch or Belt of Blasting Belt of Blasting, it is worthwhile to break the set.

Should Destruction Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Destruction Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Destruction Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best In Slot Gear for Destruction Warlock DPS in Phase 3
When gearing, there is a multitude of different things to consider: different talent specializations, your currently equipped gear, and your raid composition all play a key part. Perhaps the biggest deciding factor of them all is Hit rating - having too much or too little Hit rating from either talents, gems, or buffs will result in you swapping in different pieces of gear or gems to reach the relevant Hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but here are some important things to note:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

The time of Malefic Raiment and wings has arrived! The biggest upgrade in this phase is the four-piece bonus of this set, which increases the damage of our Shadow Bolt and Incinerate by 6%. To put this in perspective, this is roughly equal to more than 100 Spell Power, or more than twice as good as four-piece Tier 4, or three-piece Spellfire! We will keep rocking this set until the end of times (of The Burning Crusade), although we switch up the item combination in Sunwell Plateau.

There are a lot of high-ticket caster items this phase, which we aren't the only ones aiming for. I go in-depth about which items specifically, and the possible alternatives, in the descriptions of every equipment slot. The long and short of it is that you will want to prioritize getting The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan, as that is the item that will last you the longest.

Note: In the following guide, some items are marked with a icon, indicating they are only suitable for Fire.

Should Destruction Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Destruction Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc. That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. And Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Destruction Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

By Zephan
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Destruction Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Destruction Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Destruction Warlock DPS in Phase 4
When gearing, there is a multitude of different things to consider: different talent specializations, your currently equipped gear, and your raid composition all play a key part. Perhaps the biggest deciding factor of them all is hit rating - having too much or too little hit rating from either talents, gems, or buffs will result in you swapping in different pieces of gear or gems to reach the relevant hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but here are some important things to note:

    The hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% hit.

The time of Malefic Raiment and wings has arrived! The biggest upgrade in this phase is the four-piece bonus of this set, which increases the damage of our Shadow Bolt and Incinerate by 6%. To put this in perspective, this is roughly equal to more than 100 spell power, or more than twice as good as four-piece Tier 4, or three-piece Spellfire! We will keep rocking this set until the end of times (of The Burning Crusade), although we switch up the item combination in Sunwell Plateau.

There are a lot of high-ticket caster items this phase, which we aren't the only ones eyeing. I go in-depth about which items specifically, and the possible alternatives, in the descriptions of every equipment slot. In this phase specifically, the interesting items are Hex Shrunken Head Hex Shrunken Head, Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy, Mana Attuned Band Mana Attuned Band, and Carved Witch Doctor's Stick Carved Witch Doctor's Stick.

Should Destruction Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Destruction Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, the Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while the Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Destruction Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Destruction Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Destruction Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Destruction Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Destruction Warlock DPS in Phase 5
When gearing, there is a multitude of different things to consider: different talent specializations, your currently equipped gear, and your raid composition all play a key part. Perhaps the biggest deciding factor of them all is hit rating - having too much or too little hit rating from either talents, gems, or buffs will result in you swapping in different pieces of gear or gems to reach the relevant hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but here are some important things to note:

    The hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% hit.

The time of Malefic Raiment and wings is still here! This bonus flat-out increases our Shadow Bolt and Incinerate by 6%. To put this in perspective, this is roughly equal to 100 spell power, or more than twice as good as four-piece Tier 4, or three-piece Spellfire!

In the current phase, the items we use to get out the four-piece bonus switch around slightly. Bracers of the Malefic Bracers of the Malefic, Belt of the Malefic Belt of the Malefic, and Boots of the Malefic Boots of the Malefic are the three new Tier 6 items. Not only are they great ways to get our set bonus, but they are also BIS independent of the set bonus.

This means we only need one other piece from our previous Tier 6 options. On paper, Mantle of the Malefic Mantle of the Malefic is the best option for this, but Hood of the Malefic Hood of the Malefic is also really close. The best items used in combination with either are Dark Conjuror's Collar Dark Conjuror's Collar (in combination with T6 shoulders) or Amice of the Convoker Amice of the Convoker (together with T6 helm). Alternatively, you can also opt to keep using your Leggings of the Malefic Leggings of the Malefic and make use of both Amice of the Convoker and Dark Conjuror's Collar; the benefit is not needing Leggings of Calamity Leggings of Calamity, albeit at the cost of a bit of damage.

Should Destruction Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Destruction Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only became available in Phase 5, and there are stronger alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, the Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while the Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Destruction Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.
