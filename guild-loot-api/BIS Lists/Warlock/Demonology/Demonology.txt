Burning Crusade Classic Demonology Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/08/01
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Demonology Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Demonology Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Demonology Warlock DPS in Phase 4
When gearing, there is a multitude of different things to consider: different talent specializations, your currently equipped gear, and your raid composition all play a key part. Perhaps the biggest deciding factor of them all is Hit rating - having too much or too little Hit rating from either talents, gems, or buffs will result in you swapping in different pieces of gear or gems to reach the relevant Hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but here are some important things to note:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

The time of Malefic Raiment and wings has arrived! The biggest upgrade in this phase is the four-piece bonus of this set, which increases the damage of our Shadow Bolt and Incinerate by 6%. To put this in perspective, this is roughly equal to more than 100 Spell Power, or more than twice as good as four-piece Tier 4, or three-piece Spellfire! We will keep rocking this set until the end of time (of The Burning Crusade), although we switch up the item combination in Sunwell Plateau.

Note: As Demonology, you can opt for the two-piece set bonus of Corruptor Raiment along with Void Star Talisman Void Star Talisman. Whilst theoretically it doesn't boost your damage, keeping your pet alive is of the utmost importance.

There are a lot of high-ticket caster items this phase, which we aren't the only ones aiming for. We go in-depth about which items specifically, and the possible alternatives, in the descriptions of every equipment slot. The long and short of it is that you will want to prioritize getting The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan, as that is the item that will last you the longest.

Should Demonology Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Demonology Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc. That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. And Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Demonology Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Back Armor for Demonology Warlock in TBC Classic Phase 4
Our new cloaks will all be coming from Black Temple. Nethervoid Cloak is technically the best value cloak of the new three. However, the issue is that it has a lot of Hit rating. Which you are unlikely to make use of due to the rest of your gear being high on Hit already. You might be able to make more use out of the Hit when starting to transition into Sunwell Plateau gear, as this gear is more Haste heavy instead of Hit heavy. Another benefit is that early on during Phase 4, when you do not have a lot of Hit from your other pieces yet, you are more likely to make full use of the Hit on the back. It also has the advantage of being a Trash drop, resulting in it being solo obtainable.

Shroud of the Highborne and Cloak of the Illidari Council are the other two cloaks. Both of these are about equally good. Shroud of the Highborne technically has a minor edge in value (roughly 4 Spell Power worth in BiS), but it is a better option for healers. it also is something all caster DPS will seek out due to offering Haste, making it one of the most competative items in Phase 4. However, once all healers have the cloak, it should be fair game.

Best in Slot Ring Jewelry for Demonology Warlock in TBC Classic Phase 4
Ring of Ancient Knowledge is our new best ring. This ring is not unique, so you can equip two of them. These drop from the trash in Black Temple and can potentially be soloed depending on which bosses are already dead. By soloing this content, you can potentially get your rings faster. However, as before, every caster will want this item. So if you solely rely on your raid itself, it may take you a while before you see your second ring.

Tip: To solo trash, you will need an ID that is cleared up to Mother Shahraz. In the original TBC, you could solo bigger packs, but these have gained a speed bonus in Classic. You can still solo the single mobs, but you can only do one at a time, and need Swiftness of Zanza Swiftness of Zanza to outpace them. It takes roughly 6 to 8 minutes per kill, and you will need to reset after 3 to 4 mobs, depending on the positioning. Be warned that this is rather slow and tedious.
Here are two examples of it being done: Madarb, Offe.

Until you get two Rings of Ancient Knowledge, Ring of Captured Storms and Band of the Eternal Sage are solid choices. Band of the Eternal Sage is the final version of the ring, but even the Friendly version of the ring is already a solid option until you reach Exalted. Similar to your cloak option, the rings will be highly contested by every other caster, so you should look at your other options as well.

The Skull of Gul'dan is the biggest gain in DPS aside from Malefic Raiment four-piece bonus, especially when it comes to AoE DPS. It lasts the entire expansion and is the biggest upgrade in this phase. From all the items listed here, this should be your highest priority. Unfortunately, like your ring and cloak options, this too will be highly contested amongst every caster DPS in the game.

Your other trinket of choice is going to depend on whether you are facing Demons or Undead targets, in which case you will want to equip Mark of the Champion. Demonology Warlock DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/08/02
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Demonology Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Demonology Warlock DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Demonology Warlock DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Demonology Warlock DPS in Pre-Raid in Phase 3
In Phase 3, a couple of additions will come to Pre-Raid BiS. The primary three changes will be Season 2 PvP gear, Engineering Goggles, and Phase 2 crafted belts. Outside of that, not much will change until Phase 4 badge gear. We'll discuss priority in Pre-Raid gear as well, as with pre-raid, you either want to get as powerful as possible before entering the raids, or you want to achieve the minimal amount of power that will get you into a raid.

Due to the differences in specialization talents, variety of stats on gear, and bonuses from other players in the party, there's no single best set of gear appropriate for all situations. Hit chance plays an enormous role in determining the gear you want to use, as missing a piece of hit gear or having extra hit from talents, gems, or buffs will result in changing other pieces of gear to maintain that hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but some important things to consider:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

Should Demonology Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Demonology Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc. That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. And Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

Burning Crusade Classic Demonology Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 1 Gear Guide
By Zephan
Updated: 2025/12/30
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Demonology Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Demonology Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Demonology Warlock DPS Best in Slot Guides
Best In Slot Gear for Demonology Warlock DPS in Phase 1
Due to the differences in specialization talents, variety of stats on gear, and bonuses from other players in the party, there's no single best set of gear appropriate for all situations. Hit chance plays an enormous role in determining the gear you want to use, as missing a piece of hit gear or having extra hit from talents, gems, or buffs will result in changing other pieces of gear to maintain that hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide.

While Spell Damage and Crit are important gearing factors which may adjust your gearing, Hit is generally the most important consideration and the main determination for your gearing. As such, the gear lists below will list a variety of options based on those factors, offering competitive alternatives depending on whether you are already above or below the Hit cap. For more specific situations, the Classic Warlock Discord maintains a gear simulation spreadsheet which will help you evaluate the best combination of gear actually available to you.

In this BiS setup we'll aim to get the Four Set Bonus from our Tier 4. This Four-piece bonus is achieved by getting the Head, Chest, and Shoulders. Should Demonology Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
The choice between Aldor and Scryer for warlocks does not matter in the grand scheme of things. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc, more about this here.

That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items. this being Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet. There are better items than these available in Phase 1, so you aren't missing out by not going Scryer. What these items do, however, is offer great flexibility in your gearing choices. It allows you to swap around Hit and non-Hit pieces to achieve your ideal Hit amount in each situation.

The Aldor has the better enchant. Although still really close in value to the Scryer enchant. Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1 to 2 DPS difference compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb. This is really minor. Sapphiron drops Power of the Scourge Power of the Scourge, which is about the same in value as the Aldor enchant, DPS-wise.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on Faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and independent of the Faction chosen, there are better alternatives available, so you aren't missing out on anything. The choice is either between The Scryers, which brings early gearing flexibility, or The Aldor for a negligibly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

By Zephan
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Demonology Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Demonology Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.

Best In Slot Gear for Demonology Warlock DPS in Phase 2
Due to the differences in specialization talents, variety of stats on gear, and bonuses from other players in the party, there's no single best set of gear appropriate for all situations. Hit chance plays an enormous role in determining the gear you want to use, as missing a piece of hit gear or having extra hit from talents, gems, or buffs will result in changing other pieces of gear to maintain that hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but some important things to consider:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

Before we dive in, I should mention that when gearing in Phase 2, the four-piece set bonus of Corruptor Raiment only works on base damage. The value also drops off on multi-target fights, as you would need to Shadow Bolt multiple targets, which we can't. Also, once there is movement, you also get a lower uptime. All this combined makes it not worth getting.

As Demonology, you want the two-piece set bonus of Tier 5. While it doesn't directly boost your damage, keeping your pet alive is of the utmost importance.

Demonology Warlocks looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Demonology Warlock Gems & Enchants
Should Demonology Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Demonology Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Demonology Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Demonology Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/08/01
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Demonology Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Demonology Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Demonology Warlock DPS in Phase 3
When gearing, there is a multitude of different things to consider: different talent specializations, your currently equipped gear, and your raid composition all play a key part. Perhaps the biggest deciding factor of them all is Hit rating - having too much or too little Hit rating from either talents, gems, or buffs will result in you swapping in different pieces of gear or gems to reach the relevant Hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but here are some important things to note:

    Hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of Hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% Hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% Hit.

The time of Malefic Raiment and wings has arrived! The biggest upgrade in this phase is the four-piece bonus of this set, which increases the damage of our Shadow Bolt and Incinerate by 6%. To put this in perspective, this is roughly equal to more than 100 Spell Power, or more than twice as good as four-piece Tier 4, or three-piece Spellfire! We will keep rocking this set until the end of times (of The Burning Crusade), although we switch up the item combination in Sunwell Plateau.

As Demonology, you can opt for the two-piece set bonus of Corruptor Raiment along with Void Star Talisman Void Star Talisman. Whilst theoretically it doesn't boost your damage, keeping your pet alive is of the utmost importance.

There are a lot of high-ticket caster items this phase, which we aren't the only ones aiming for. I go in-depth about which items specifically, and the possible alternatives, in the descriptions of every equipment slot. The long and short of it is that you will want to prioritize getting The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan, as that is the item that will last you the longest.

Demonology Warlocks looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Demonology Warlock Gems & Enchants
Should Demonology Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Demonology Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

That being said, let us look at the differences that influence gearing choices. The Scryers have the better items: Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet at Exalted. Although these are quite good, there are better items available in both slots in Phase 1. They do, however, offer additional flexibility in your gearing choices as you can swap them in and out to increase/decrease your total hit as required.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only becomes available by Phase 5, and in this phase, there will be better alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, The Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while The Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Demonology Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Demonology Warlock DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Zephan
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Demonology Warlock DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Demonology Warlock DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Demonology Warlock DPS in Phase 5
When gearing, there is a multitude of different things to consider: different talent specializations, your currently equipped gear, and your raid composition all play a key part. Perhaps the biggest deciding factor of them all is hit rating - having too much or too little hit rating from either talents, gems, or buffs will result in you swapping in different pieces of gear or gems to reach the relevant hit cap. You can learn more about stat caps and priorities by visiting the Warlock Stat Priority Guide, but here are some important things to note:

    The hit cap is determined by the level of the target, with level 73 raid bosses requiring +16% chance to hit, while level 70 trash requires only +3%. You should typically gear for bosses, but you may consider it worthwhile to have a specialized setup for clearing trash.
    The amount of hit gems you use, such as Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz and Great Dawnstone Great Dawnstone.
    Draenei grant 1% hit to their group through Inspiring Presence.
    Elemental Shamans provide Totem of Wrath, which provides 3% hit.

The time of Malefic Raiment and wings is still here! This bonus flat-out increases our Shadow Bolt and Incinerate by 6%. To put this in perspective, this is roughly equal to 100 spell power, or more than twice as good as four-piece Tier 4, or three-piece Spellfire!

In the current phase, the items we use to get out the four-piece bonus switch around slightly. Bracers of the Malefic Bracers of the Malefic, Belt of the Malefic Belt of the Malefic, and Boots of the Malefic Boots of the Malefic are the three new Tier 6 items. Not only are they great ways to get our set bonus, but they are also BIS independent of the set bonus.

This means we only need one other piece from our previous Tier 6 options. On paper, Mantle of the Malefic Mantle of the Malefic is the best option for this, but Hood of the Malefic Hood of the Malefic is also really close. The best items used in combination with either are Dark Conjuror's Collar Dark Conjuror's Collar (in combination with T6 shoulders) or Amice of the Convoker Amice of the Convoker (together with T6 helm). Alternatively, you can also opt to keep using your Leggings of the Malefic Leggings of the Malefic and make use of both Amice of the Convoker and Dark Conjuror's Collar; the benefit is not needing Leggings of Calamity Leggings of Calamity, albeit at the cost of a bit of damage.

As Demonology, you can opt for the two-piece set bonus of Corruptor Raiment to greatly increase pet survivability. However, this does come at a significant DPS cost. For most encounters, this is not recommended.

The Black Book The Black Book is a trinket with a unique use, especially for Demonology Warlocks. This trinket applies a buff to your demon of choice for 30 seconds, which stays up on your pet even after equipping another trinket. Before a boss fight, you can equip this trinket, buff your pet, and swap over to another trinket before entering combat again. Demonology Warlocks can also make great use of Void Star Talisman Void Star Talisman. It greatly increases the survivability of your pet. It does come at a significant DPS loss, so if you don't need it, don't use it.

Demonology Warlocks looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Demonology Warlock Gems & Enchants
Should Demonology Warlock DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
There is not a huge difference between Aldor and Scryers for Demonology Warlocks. You should most likely base this on personal preference, such as elevator type, location, profession trainers, tabard style, etc.

The Aldor has the better enchant, but there is a very small difference between the two; Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline is about a 1-2 DPS increase compared to Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is a special neck that changes proc based on faction alignment. This neck only became available in Phase 5, and there are stronger alternatives, so this is unlikely to affect your decision.

Ultimately, the Scryers provide more early gearing flexibility, while the Aldor provide a very slightly better shoulder enchant. Whatever your choice is, it is the right one.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Demonology Warlocks, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

This trinket applies a buff to your demon of choice for 30 seconds, which stays up on your pet even after equipping another trinket.
