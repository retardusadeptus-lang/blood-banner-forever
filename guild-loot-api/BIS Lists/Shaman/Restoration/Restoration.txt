Restoration Shaman Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/07/23
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Restoration Shaman Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Restoration Shaman Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Restoration Shaman Healing Best in Slot Hub
Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Pre-Raid
Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Leatherworking as a Profession or not. The Windhawk set is extremely good, but requires Leatherworking. Not only is the Windhawk set pre-raid BiS, but it can also be considered Phase 1 BiS as well.

Many items can be considered BiS for each slot, depending on whether you play an aggressive Healing Power style or a Mana considerate style that relies more on stats like Spirit and MP5. The sections below offer detailed information for each slot.

Restoration Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Shaman Healer Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
Restoration Shamans favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant, Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith, from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative, Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Shamans tend to have Leatherworking, there are various Leatherworking recipes from both factions, but nothing noteworthy enough to influence your decision.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Restoration Shaman Healing Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/03/16
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Restoration Shaman Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Restoration Shaman Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Restoration Shaman Healing Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman HEALING in Phase 1
Phase 1 gear sets are highly dependent on whether you choose Leatherworking as a Profession. The Windhawk set is extremely good, but requires Leatherworking. This set remains relevant throughout Phase 1.

There are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. Ideally, you will want to use the Tier 4 Head and Shoulder pieces, alongside the best off-set gear available. Using other options listed below is also fine, as there are multiple pieces with very similar stats, and our Tier 4 set bonuses are not particularly strong.

Restoration Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Shaman Healers Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
Restoration Shamans favor choosing Aldor. The shoulder enchant, Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith, from the Aldor is better than the Scryer alternative, Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. There aren't any notable BoP items from either vendor. Since many Restoration Shamans tend to have Leatherworking, there are various Leatherworking recipes from both factions, but nothing noteworthy enough to influence your decision.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Restoration Shaman Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Restoration Shaman Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Shaman Healing to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Restoration Shaman Healing Best in Slot HubRestoration Shaman Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Phase 2
In Phase 2 there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. Ideally, you will want to use the Tier 5 Head and Shoulder pieces, alongside the best off-set gear available. Using other options listed below is also fine, as there are multiple pieces with very similar stats, and our Tier 5 set bonuses are not particularly strong.

Notably, you can already acquire Band of Eternity Band of Eternity if you can kill Lady Vashj and Kael'thas Sunstrider for The Vials of Eternity The Vials of Eternity. This is a very good ring, easily on par with Naaru Lightwarden's Band Naaru Lightwarden's Band.

Restoration Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Shaman Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for healing.
    Scryers offer a decent staff at Revered, Seer's Cane Seer's Cane. This is okay for starters, but almost any loot you can get at 70 is better, including cheap auction house BoEs such as The Essence Focuser The Essence Focuser + Crystal Pulse Shield Crystal Pulse Shield.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Phase 3
In Phase 3, there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots have a stand-out BiS option, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. The Tier 6 (Skyshatter Raiment) set bonuses should be obtained as quickly as possible, as they synergize together and are extremely strong. Spell haste also makes its introduction into the game, which is a strong stat that increases your throughput and ability to reactively heal.

Should Restoration Shaman Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for healing.
    Scryers offer a decent staff at Revered, Seer's Cane Seer's Cane. This is okay for starters, but almost any loot you can get at 70 is better, including cheap auction house BoEs such as The Essence Focuser The Essence Focuser + Crystal Pulse Shield Crystal Pulse Shield.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Restoration Shaman Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/15
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Restoration Shaman Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Shaman Healing to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Phase 4
In Phase 4, there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. The Tier 6 (Skyshatter Raiment) set bonuses should be obtained as quickly as possible, as they synergize together and are extremely strong. The introduction of Zul'Aman brings catch-up items for every slot, some of which are almost as good as Tier 6 gear, but if you are already decked out in Phase 3 best in slot, you will not find many upgrades.

Restoration Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Restoration Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Shaman Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for healing.
    Scryers offer a decent staff at Revered, Seer's Cane Seer's Cane. This is okay for starters, but almost any loot you can get at 70 is better, including cheap auction house BoEs such as The Essence Focuser The Essence Focuser + Crystal Pulse Shield Crystal Pulse Shield.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Restoration Shaman Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/28
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Restoration Shaman Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Restoration Shaman Healing to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Restoration Shaman Healing in Phase 5
The Tier 6 (Skyshatter Raiment) set bonuses should be obtained as quickly as possible, as they synergize together and are extremely strong. Due to the new Tier 6 pieces introduced in Sunwell Plateau having very strong stats, you will generally want to prioritize using these pieces for your tier bonuses, while using off-pieces on the head, legs, gloves, and chest.

Spell haste is a particularly strong stat that increases your throughput and ability to reactively heal. This does come at a cost to your mana pool if you are spamming heals without pause, however.

Restoration Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Restoration Shaman Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for healing.
    Scryers offer a decent staff at Revered, Seer's Cane Seer's Cane. This is okay for starters, but almost any loot you can get at 70 is better, including cheap auction house BoEs such as The Essence Focuser The Essence Focuser + Crystal Pulse Shield Crystal Pulse Shield.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Restoration Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Restoration Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

Redeemer's Alchemist Stone is the best trinket available in Phase 5, as long as you are using mana pots on cooldown, but it requires Alchemy to craft and equip. Best in Slot Weapons for Restoration Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5
Staves and other two-handed weapons generally grant less total healing than an MH+OH combo, and also lack the extra armor and block a shield provides. Thus, we would strongly recommend you avoid two-handed weapons unless the amount of stats on the weapon is way better than what you are currently using.

Hammer of Sanctification is the ultimate Phase 5 weapon, having the highest stats in every category by a landslide.

Alternatively, Archon's Gavel is also very strong, as is Crystal Spire of Karabor, whose proc has unfortunately yet to prove itself on the logs. Dark Blessing is also extremely strong, and PvP Shamans can get the new PvP weapon, Brutal Gladiator's Salvation.
