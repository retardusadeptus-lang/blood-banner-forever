Elemental Shaman DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/08/01
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Elemental Shaman DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Pre-Raid in Phase 3
This guide gets an update as we get into Phase 3. As we potentially anticipate loot changes in Heroic dungeons in this Phase, this guide will also get updated to accommodate that once we get confirmation on that.

For now, the main updates you'll see will include PvP gear from Season 2, crafted gear from Serpentshrine Cavern, and Engineering Goggles for those who opt into that profession. It should be noted that technically the Season 3 weapon is better than Season 2, but it is much easier to obtain the Season 2 weapon, and it is still incredibly powerful, which is why it's preferred in this article.

The best pre-raid gear is a mix of gear from Leatherworking, Badges of Justice, dungeons, and a couple of BoE pieces crafted by Tailors. You may want to use gems such as Shining Fire Opal Shining Fire Opal and Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz to get as close to the Spell Hit cap (12% from talents and Totem of Wrath + 4% from gear) as possible, without going over and wasting stats in the process.

Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main-Hand/Off-Hand combinations are better at level 70.
    Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high Spell Hit due to getting so much of it from talents.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Pre-Raid in Phase 3
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

Icon of the Silver Crescent and Quagmirran's Eye are your best pre-raid trinkets, with Icon of the Silver Crescent serving as an extra DPS cooldown with its on-use effect. Phase 3 of TBC Anniversary will line up with Brewfest, meaning players can get their hands on Dark Iron Smoking Pipe, which is the exact same trinket as Icon, and you can wear it WITH Icon. Making it a great trinket combination.

It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time; especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.

Best in Slot Weapons for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Pre-Raid in Phase 3
Staves and other two-handed weapons generally grant less total spell damage than an MH+OH combo, and also lack the extra armor and block a shield provides. Thus, we would strongly recommend you avoid two-handed weapons unless the amount of stats on the weapon is way better than what you are currently using.

Going into Phase 3, the Season 2 weapon, Merciless Gladiator's Gavel is your best pre-raid weapon in terms of obtaining a powerful weapon as fast as possible, but technically the Season 3 iteration, Vengeful Gladiator's Gavel Vengeful Gladiator's Gavel is obviously stronger, but will take at least two weeks to obtain once the season starts, and requires a raiting, whereas the previous season can be obtained in one. For the sake of getting into the raids faster, we'll be listing the Season 2 weapon in the list, but wanted to clarify about Season 3 as well.

Elemental Shaman DPS Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress on the first few raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Elemental Shaman DPS Best in Slot Guides

Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 1
In Phase 1, there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. Ideally, you will want to use the Tier 4 Head and Shoulder pieces, alongside the best off-set gear available. Using other options listed below is also fine, as there are multiple pieces with very similar stats, but try to get at least 2 pieces of Tier 4 for the set bonus.

You may want to use gems such as Shining Fire Opal Shining Fire Opal and Veiled Noble Topaz Veiled Noble Topaz to get as close to the Spell Hit cap (12% from talents and Totem of Wrath + 4% from gear) as possible, without going over and wasting stats in the process.

Elemental Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Elemental Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main-Hand/Off-Hand combinations are better at level 70.
    Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high Spell Hit due to getting so much of it from talents.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Shoulder Armor for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 1
Cyclone Shoulderguards are the go-to shoulders, having high spell damage and spell crit, as well as 2 sockets with a good socket color bonus. They also enable your T4 (2) Set bonus, Improved Wrath of Air Totem, which gives a massive spell damage benefit to your group!

Best in Slot Back Armor for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 1
Brute Cloak of the Ogre-Magi is a great all-rounder cloak with no spell hit, making it a great choice when trying to avoid hitting Elemental Shaman Shaman's low spell hit cap. Alternatively, Ruby Drape of the Mysticant is a strong spell hit cloak, and Ancient Spellcloak of the Highborne is very similar to the best in slot cloak, but because it drops from Doom Lord Kazzak, it can be much harder to get.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 1
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

The Lightning Capacitor and Icon of the Silver Crescent are your best trinkets, with The Lightning Capacitor benefitting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits and Icon of the Silver Crescent having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown.

Best in Slot Weapons for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 1
Staves and other two-handed weapons generally grant less total spell damage than a Main-Hand/Off-Hand combination, and also lack the extra armor and block a shield provides. Thus, we would strongly recommend that you avoid two-handed weapons unless the amount of stats on the weapon is way better than what you are currently using.

Nathrezim Mindblade is your chase weapon, as Talon of the Tempest comes from a World Boss, making it extremely difficult to get.
Rank	Item	Source

BiS	Nathrezim Mindblade Nathrezim Mindblade	Drop: Prince Malchezaar (Karazhan)
PvP	Gladiator's Gavel Gladiator's Gavel	Vendor: Arena Points (Season 1)

Elemental Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Woah
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.

Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 2
In Phase 2 there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your throughput. Ideally, you will still want to use the Tier 4 Head and Shoulder pieces for the powerful 2-piece set bonus, alongside the best off-set gear available, as Tier 5 has very weak set bonuses. Using other options listed below is also fine, as there are multiple pieces with very similar stats, but try to get at least 2 pieces of Tier 4 for the set bonus.

Elemental Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Elemental Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Shamans can pick both The Aldor or The Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offers a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but most 1h + Off-Hand combos at 70 are better.
    Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem, although you probably will not need its high Spell Hit due to getting so much of it from talents.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Feet Armor for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 2

Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 2
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

The Lightning Capacitor and Icon of the Silver Crescent are your best trinkets, with The Lightning Capacitor benefitting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits and Icon of the Silver Crescent having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown.

It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time, especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.

Best in Slot Weapons for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 2
Typically, staves generally grant less total spell damage than an MH+OH combo, and also lack the extra armor and block a shield provides, making an MH+OH generally superior if its stats are comparable to the best two-handed weapon available. However, The Nexus Key is the exception, and is the best weapon in Tier 5, having the highest amount of Intellect, critical strike, and spell damage in a single package.

Outside of this special circumstance, we'll look again to the one-hand + off-hand combo for Elemental Shamans. Merciless Gladiator's Gavel is a great weapon that has no RNG involved in its acquisition, but it can only be bought with Arena Points, which must be obtained over the course of several weeks, but can be obtained in 2 at a minimum.

Nathrezim Mindblade is still a great weapon, as is Talon of the Tempest. Gladiator's Gavel can now be obtained without rating requirements and for discounted Arena points, making it much easier to get than it used to be!

Burning Crusade Classic Elemental Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/07/26
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 3
In Phase 3, Elemental Shamans finally get strong tier set bonuses, and getting four pieces of the set should be one of your main priorities. Your physical DPS counterpart, the Enhancment Shaman, will also not look to wear any tier as their bis, eliminating some players from competition. Ideally, you will want your off-set piece to be the legs, as Leggings of Channeled Elements Leggings of Channeled Elements are the strongest off-piece available, but crafting Mantle of Nimble Thought Mantle of Nimble Thought is also a good option, especially if competition for the legs in your guild is high.

The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan is the best-in-slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents. Because of this, we'll recommend opting out of obtaining this initially, and look into out previous phase BiS trinkets.

Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main Hand / Offhand combinations are better at level 70.
    Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high spell hit due to getting so much of it from talents.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 3
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

The Skull of Gul'dan is the best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

Thus, The Lightning Capacitor and Icon of the Silver Crescent (or Dark Iron Smoking Pipe, the Brewfest caster trinket) are your best trinkets, with The Lightning Capacitor benefitting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits and Icon of the Silver Crescent having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown. Sextant of Unstable Currents is a fine alternative, but its damage contribution can be quite random.

It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time, especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.

Burning Crusade Classic Elemental Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 4
Elemental Shaman's Tier 6 bonuses are strong, and getting four pieces of the set should be one of your main priorities. Ideally, you will want your off-set piece to be the legs, as Leggings of Channeled Elements Leggings of Channeled Elements are the strongest off-piece available, and you can also simply get Pantaloons of Arcane Annihilation Pantaloons of Arcane Annihilation for badges as a reliable alternative.

The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan is the best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

Elemental Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Elemental Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main Hand / Offhand combinations are better at level 70.
    Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high spell hit due to getting so much of it from talents.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 4
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

The Skull of Gul'dan is the best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

Thus, The Lightning Capacitor and Hex Shrunken Head are your best trinkets, with The Lightning Capacitor benefiting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits and Hex Shrunken Head having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown.

It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time, especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.

Burning Crusade Classic Elemental Shaman DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Woah
Updated: 2026/02/22
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Elemental Shaman DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Elemental Shaman DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Elemental Shaman DPS in Phase 5
Elemental Shaman's Tier 6 bonuses are strong, and getting four pieces of the set should be one of your main priorities, with three of them being the new tier pieces in Sunwell Plateau. Ideally, you will want your remaining piece to be the shoulders, as it is the slot where you have the least to gain by switching to a Sunwell Plateau gear piece.

The Skull of Gul'dan The Skull of Gul'dan is a best in slot trinket for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

Elemental Shamans looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Elemental Shaman Gems & Enchants
Should Elemental Shaman DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Shamans can pick either of the Aldor or the Scryers without much penalty. The Aldor shoulder enchant is slightly better, but the difference is minuscule from the Scryers enchant when considering your full gear stat bonuses. In general, it is best to pick based on which faction is cheaper to buy reputation materials for, as Aldor, for example, can often be extremely expensive due to having the favored shoulder enchant for most specializations.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for DPS.
    Aldor also offers a decent staff at Revered, Auchenai Staff Auchenai Staff. This is okay for starters, but Main Hand / Offhand combinations are better at level 70.
    Scryers offers one of the best rings, Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, at Exalted, and a powerful trinket, Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem at Revered, although you probably will not need its high spell hit due to getting so much of it from talents.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Elemental Shamans, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Elemental Shaman in TBC Classic Phase 5
In TBC, most trinkets offer passive bonuses on top of their unique, cooldown-bound powers. This makes many trinkets useful for their passive benefits alone, but there are still many powerful on-use effects to consider when picking a trinket.

The Skull of Gul'dan is one of the best in slot trinkets for Elemental Shamans, as it is for every caster, but it is generally a small priority upgrade due to its high spell hit, which easily ends up wasted due to Elemental's multiple spell hit talents.

Thus, Shifting Naaru Sliver, The Lightning Capacitor, and Hex Shrunken Head are generally your best trinkets, with the Sliver sporting both high stats and an extremely powerful on use effect, Capacitor benefitting from your frequent spell crits and overload crits, and Shrunken Head having high spell damage, which you can further power up as a DPS cooldown.

It should also be noted that Classic-era trinkets, such as Mark of the Champion and Neltharion's Tear, are still extremely powerful in TBC and can be used for a long time, especially Mark of the Champion, which stays your best trinket when fighting Demons and Undead.
