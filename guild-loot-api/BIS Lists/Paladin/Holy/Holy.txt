Holy Paladin Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/01/27
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Holy Paladin Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Holy Paladin Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Holy Paladin Healing Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Holy Paladin Healing Pre-Raid
There are three different ways to approach your pre-raid best in slot (BiS) gear. You can either use no profession-required gear, you can use the tailoring items, or you can use the leatherworking items, all based on your needs. It is important to note the cost of using the crafted gear, especially relative to the longevity of the gear itself. While they are very strong for the pre-raid gear, they quickly lose potency in Phase 1 and are nearly completely replaceable in Phase 2. Another important thing to consider is the lower survivability of wearing cloth or leather gear compared to plate, which is much better itemized in TBC Classic and offers no competition amongst the other class healers. Finally, Holy Paladins have a unique gear path compared to other healers throughout TBC. Intellect and Spell Crit grant a lot more power due to the playstyle and talents available to Holy Paladins, and should be considered when looking at upgrades.

Holy Paladin Gems & Enchants
Should Holy Paladin Healers Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
The quick answer is that this is entirely preference-based, but the difference is rather small and will not make or break end-game content. Choosing either faction will ultimately be fine in the long run, so do not be dismayed if you went with your least preferred option.

The Aldor shoulder enchant is Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith compared to The Scryers getting Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. The difference here is +11 healing power gained while losing 2 mana per 5. While the Greater Inscription of Faith is superior from an overall Healing, it's not a large enough margin to definitively say The Aldor is better and worth changing from The Scryers if you have already gone that route. Alternatively, The Scyers offers the better enchant from a Spell Crit perspective, with Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb compared to The Aldors Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline.

The last point of contention is which proc of the Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration (available once Exalted with Shattered Sun Offensive) is superior for Holy paladins. This is a bit of future planning, as these necklace procs won't be available until the last content phase for TBC Classic, Phase 5.

In conclusion, The Aldor offers the most benefit from a healing perspective, with the better healing shoulder enchant and necklace proc in phase 5 compared to The Scryers.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Holy Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Holy Paladin Healing Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/01
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Holy Paladin Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Holy Paladin Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Holy Paladin Healing Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Holy Paladin Healer in Phase 1
There are different ways to approach gearing for the Holy Paladin in Phase 1 of TBCC. World Bosses have a lot of very strong pieces for the Paladin Healer, but will be considerably challenging to obtain, considering these bosses are non-instanced and will be heavily contested. There are also strong pieces through specific professions with Mooncloth Tailoring and Tribal Leatherworking, but these are only usable if you are trained in these professions, as the items are bound to the player upon creation, and not tradeable.

Finally, should you not have access to these professions or World Bosses, then there's the more traditional route of gearing from Raids and Dungeons. It's important to note the differences first for healing: total Healing Power, Intellect/Mana, Spell Crit, and Mp5. Then compare the survivability stats, such as total health/Stamina and Armor. The difference in gear sets is much smaller than in the pre-raid phase and will become even smaller in Phase 2. Whichever route you choose to follow from the items listed will all be comparable, with only a marginal difference.

Holy Paladins looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Holy Paladin Gems & Enchants
Should Holy Paladin Healers Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
The quick answer is that this is entirely preference-based, but the difference is rather small and will not make or break end-game content. Choosing either faction will ultimately be fine in the long run, so do not be dismayed if you went with your least preferred option.

The Aldor shoulder enchant is Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith compared to The Scryers getting Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. The difference here is +11 healing power gained while losing 2 mana per 5. While the Greater Inscription of Faith is superior from an overall Healing, it's not a large enough margin to definitively say The Aldor is better and worth changing from The Scryers if you have already gone that route. Alternatively, The Scyers offers the better enchant from a Spell Crit perspective, with Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb compared to The Aldors Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline.

There is no gear seriously worth buying from either faction; however, there are the Pattern: Silver Spellthread Pattern: Silver Spellthread and Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread from The Aldor if you are a tailor, so that provides another nod in favor of choosing them.

The last point of contention is which proc of the Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration is superior for Holy paladins. This is a bit of future planning, as these necklace procs won't be available until the last content phase for The Burning Crusade Classic, Phase 5. The Aldor's version gives Light's Salvation, while The Scryers gives Arcane Surge. While both effects are nice bonuses, the former will typically win out due to our tank healing and mixed usage of Holy Light and Flash of Light, where Holy Light will likely see more use near the end of the expansion. The additional healing added to a max rank cast of this spell will be a lot more beneficial due to the healing coefficient bonus.

In conclusion, The Aldor offers the most benefit from a healing perspective, with the better healing shoulder enchant and necklace proc in phase 5 compared to The Scryers.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Holy Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Holy Paladin Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Holy Paladin Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Holy Paladin Healing to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.

Best In Slot Gear for Holy Paladin Healer in Phase 2
Holy Paladins looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Holy Paladin Gems & Enchants
Should Holy Paladin Healers Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
The quick, TL;DR answer is that The Aldor faction is better for PvE, but the difference is rather small and will not make or break end-game content. Choosing either faction will ultimately be fine in the long run, so do not be dismayed if you went with The Scryers instead. The Aldor shoulder enchant is Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith, compared to The Scryers getting Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. The difference here is +11 healing power gained while losing 2 mana per 5. While the Aldor variant is superior, the margin isn't large enough to definitively say the former is better and worth switching from the latter if you have already gone that route.

There is no gear seriously worth buying from either faction; however, there are the Pattern: Silver Spellthread Pattern: Silver Spellthread and Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread from The Aldor if you are a tailor, so that provides another nod in favor of choosing them.

The last point of contention is which proc of the Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration is superior for Holy paladins. This is a bit of future planning, as these necklace procs won't be available until the last content phase for The Burning Crusade Classic, Phase 5. The Aldor's version gives Light's Salvation, while The Scryers gives Arcane Surge. While both effects are nice bonuses, the former will typically win out due to our tank healing and mixed usage of Holy Light and Flash of Light, where Holy Light will likely see more use near the end of the expansion. The additional healing added to a max rank cast of this spell will be a lot more beneficial due to the healing coefficient bonus.

In conclusion, both short-term and long-term, The Aldor will edge out as the better faction to pick for Holy Paladins.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Holy Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Holy Paladin Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/19
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Holy Paladin Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Holy Paladin Healing to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Holy Paladin Healing in Phase 3
Truthfully, there isn't a strictly BiS setup as everyone has different play-styles and/or different guilds (i.e., focusing on speed clears vs progression) which require different gearing, like stacking haste and +healing gems for a quick Flash of Light spamming build, whereas others will stack more Intellect and spell crit for Holy Light sustainability. Choose the playstyle that suits you and your guild the most and go with that.

The Tier 6 Holy set bonuses are solid, with 5% increased Holy Light crit at 2-piece definitely worth using, and 5% increased Flash of Light healing at 4-piece as decent-ish. The pieces are statted nicely, too. Don't expect to be a high priority because Protection Paladin and Warlock sets are stronger, and because there are offset plate alternatives, but Holy is definitely far from the bottom.

Holy Paladin Healers looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Holy Paladin Healing Gems & Enchants
Should Holy Paladin Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
The quick, TL;DR answer is that The Aldor faction is better for PvE, but the difference is rather small and will not make or break end-game content. Choosing either faction will ultimately be fine in the long run, so do not be dismayed if you went with The Scryers instead.

The Aldor shoulder enchant is Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith compared to the Scryers getting Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. The difference here is 11 healing power gained while losing 2 MP5. While the Aldor enchant is superior, it's not a large enough margin to definitively say they are better and worth changing from the Scryers if you have already gone that route.

There is no gear worth buying from either faction; however, there are Pattern: Silver Spellthread Pattern: Silver Spellthread and Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread available from The Aldor if you are a Tailor, so that provides another nod in favor of choosing the Aldor.

The last point of contention is which proc of the Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration (buyable once Exalted with Shattered Sun Offensive) is superior for Holy paladins. This is a bit of future planning, as these necklace procs won't be available until the last content phase for The Burning Crusade Classic, Phase 5.

For the Aldor version, the proc granted is Light's Salvation, increasing +healing by 220 for 10 seconds. For the Scryers version, the proc granted is Arcane Surge, adding a direct heal for around 620 to 690, and is capable of being a critical heal around 980.

While both effects are nice bonuses, the Aldor's proc will typically win out due to our tank healing and mixed usage of Holy Light and Flash of Light, where Holy Light will likely see more use during end-game content in Sunwell Plateau. The additional +healing added to a max rank Holy Light will be a lot more beneficial due to the healing coefficient bonus. The Scryer's proc will not benefit as much, as it is a static heal amount not affected by your +healing level, and can only proc off of Paladin's casted heals, whereas it can proc off of other healers' healing over time spells, creating more value for them compared to Paladins.

In conclusion, The Aldor offers the most benefit from a better shoulder enchant, Tailoring patterns, and necklace proc in phase 5 compared to The Scryers.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Holy Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Holy Paladin Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/28
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Holy Paladin Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Holy Paladin Healing to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Holy Paladin Healing in Phase 5
Phase 5 introduces the Sunwell Plateau raid, easily the most challenging content in The Burning Crusade Classic. Similar to how the original Naxxramas raid had only a small percentage of guilds clearing it, the original Sunwell Plateau was notorious for its difficulty.

As I've said in previous phases, there is no true "one size fits all" for gearing. The gear-set below is an idea of one way to gear as a Holy Paladin, focusing on sustain over haste. Truthfully, there isn't a strictly BiS setup as everyone has different play-styles and/or different guilds (i.e., focusing on speed clears vs progression), which require different gearing, like stacking haste and +healing gems for a quick Flash of Light spamming build, whereas others will stack more intellect and spell crit for Holy Light sustainability. Choose what play-style suits you and your guild the most and go with that.

Regarding the gem slots, you can tailor these to your specific needs. The gear set below is just an example of matching the socket bonuses with an emphasis on bonus healing from teardrop cuts. Experimenting with different setups will be key in fine-tuning your gear. Also, a reminder that Quick Lionseye Quick Lionseye will become available if that is something that interests you.

Profession-specific gear is not included in this set, but if you are an alchemist, using the Redeemer's Alchemist Stone is recommended. If you are a blacksmith, using the Sunblessed Breastplate would mean less competition against your shaman for the mail chest. Jewelcrafters can make the Amulet of Flowing Life. Also, if you want to maintain the four-piece bonus of the tier 6 armor, you can use the Lightbringer Pauldrons over the Spaulders of the Thalassian Savior.

If you want to experiment with a higher haste set, you can include items such as Gauntlets of the Soothed Soul, Kilt of Spiritual Reconstruction, and Noble Judicator's Chestguard.

Holy Paladin Healers looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Holy Paladin Healing Gems & Enchants
Should Holy Paladin Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
The quick, TL;DR answer is that The Aldor faction is better for PvE, but the difference is rather small and will not make or break end-game content. Choosing either faction will ultimately be fine in the long run, so do not be dismayed if you went with The Scryers instead.

The Aldor shoulder enchant is Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith compared to the Scryers getting Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle. The difference here is 11 healing power gained while losing 2 MP5. While the Aldor enchant is superior, it's not a large enough margin to definitively say they are better and worth changing from the Scryers if you have already gone that route.

There is no gear worth buying from either faction; however, there are Pattern: Silver Spellthread Pattern: Silver Spellthread and Pattern: Golden Spellthread Pattern: Golden Spellthread available from The Aldor if you are a Tailor, so that provides another nod in favor of choosing the Aldor.

The last point of contention is which proc of the Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration (buyable once Exalted with Shattered Sun Offensive) is superior for Holy paladins. This is a bit of future planning, as these necklace procs won't be available until the last content phase for The Burning Crusade Classic, Phase 5.

For the Aldor version, the proc granted is Light's Salvation, increasing +healing by 220 for 10 seconds. For the Scryers version, the proc granted is Arcane Surge, adding a direct heal for around 620 to 690, and is capable of being a critical heal around 980.

While both effects are nice bonuses, the Aldor's proc will typically win out due to our tank healing and mixed usage of Holy Light and Flash of Light, where Holy Light will likely see more use during end-game content in Sunwell Plateau. The additional +healing added to a max rank Holy Light will be a lot more beneficial due to the healing coefficient bonus. The Scryer's proc will not benefit as much, as it is a static heal amount not affected by your +healing level, and can only proc off of Paladin's casted heals, whereas it can proc off of other healers' healing over time spells, creating more value for them compared to Paladins.

In conclusion, The Aldor offers the most benefit from a better shoulder enchant, Tailoring patterns, and necklace proc in Phase 5 compared to The Scryers.
