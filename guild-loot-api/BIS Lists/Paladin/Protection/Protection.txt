In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Protection Paladin Tank Best in Slot Hub

Pre-Raid Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1
This guide will focus on a few different schools of thought regarding Pre-Raid "BiS". Firstly, we'll look at what truly is your "best" gear you can have before you could step foot into a raid. After that, we'll look at the quickest way to be properly geared for raids. With Arena and Honor gear becoming incredibly more accessible to the average player in TBCC Anniversary, we will also be pushing more for players to acquire this gear, however, PvP gear is not a requirement, but the gear is incredibly strong, with some of it making it's way into Phase 1 BiS, and eliminating having to compete for items such as your best weapon, Gladiator's Gavel Gladiator's Gavel, against your caster DPS. If anything else, that should be your motivation to enter Arenas.

It should be noted that acquiring Arena Points will take time, and if there is a priority on what to purchase first, it is definitely your weapon, as it is your overall BiS weapon, whereas other pieces have equal PvE options.

There are a few things to keep in mind as a fresh Protection Paladin, and when reading this guide. Firstly, as mentioned above, your sets are fluid, and you are encouraged to hoard gear and apply items liberally and be creative in finding appropriate sets for your circumstances. For tanks, there are scenarios where you would want to put on more gear for Threat, and there are encounters where you'll want to gear more towards Mitigation / Survivability. Because of that, we must note that there is not "one" bis list for tanks.

When working on your tanking build, some good goals are:

    Critical strike immunity: -5.6% chance to be crit, can be reached using resilience or 490 defense, or a combination thereof.
    Crush immunity: (+102.4% Miss/Dodge/Parry/Block when Holy Shield is active.
    Enough health and/or effective hit points (EHP) to survive your given encounter.
    After all these benchmarks are completed, we can then focus on Threat.

Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1 Pre-Raid
For Protection Paladins, the main differences between the factions come in the early game with regard to quest rewards and rep vendor items that can help you gear up much faster for tanking heroic dungeons and Phase 1 raids. In terms of rep gain, The Scryers tend to be an easier faction to gain reputation with, so in that respect, Scryers are the superior option for those who want to get their shoulder enchants as fast as possible.

For the shoulder enchants, Greater Inscription of the Knight Greater Inscription of the Knight from the Scryers will edge out Greater Inscription of Warding Greater Inscription of Warding, although marginal, it still offers more towards Crit reduction and overall Avoidance. The Aldor does offer a really solid tank chest, but this can easily be replaced with other gear. And on that note, the Scryers offer two relatively strong Threat pieces with Scryer's Bloodgem Scryer's Bloodgem and Seer's Signet Seer's Signet, and offer a really strong tank necklace with Thalodien's Charm Thalodien's Charm, which you could be wearing longer than the tank chest option offered from The Aldor.

Although it's marginal, we recommend Protection Paladins opt into The Scryers for TBC Classic.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Protection Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Pre-Raid Best in Slot Trinkets for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1
The various trinkets listed below fulfill a wide variety of roles, such as survivability, threat generation, and general utility. Similarly to Rings, this is another slot where we will find ourselves consistently swapping out certain trinkets for certain encounters. And also similar to the Ring option, in a basic setting, we'd recommend one trinket for Threat and the other for Survivabiliy / Mitigation. For a "best" pairing, we would recommend Figurine of the Colossus and Icon of the Silver Crescent.

Another great threat trinket, and a reason to push your choice towards being a Scryer, is Scryer's Bloodgem. You can find this in the togglers for "other trinket recommendations", but this trinket offers a generous amount of Spell Hit, something not found easily for Protection Paladin, and also has a very similar on-use to Icon of the Silver Crescent, only offering 5 Spell Power less and lasting 5 less seconds to its superior version.

Brooch of the Immortal King, although not under the "best" options immediately below, is a battlemaster-esque trinket that requires some attention and understanding to use to its fullest extent, but since we are very short of defensive cooldowns as Protection Paladins, having a last stand effect on a trinket is powerful.
Crit/Crush Reduction + Avoidance/Mitigation Trinkets

Several other options in the drop-down menu are also powerful in their own right, as should you need more Defense in your early gearing to achieve Crit Immunity (490 Defense), then you would need to equip one of those over the two former mentioned, as unfortunately, Figurine does not provide any.

DISCLAIMER: After some discussion, I would like to state that this post reflects only my personal opinion that is based on my experience in the original TBC and on beta. In this post I am only voicing this opinion - that is "gearing for survival should take precedent, only after you need more threat and you have no problem surviving, you should gear for it at the expense of defensive stats". So take my hard stances with a grain of salt.

As others mentioned, reaching uncrushability should be your goal before going for anything else (apart from 490 def).

For gems the rule of thumb is always socket pure stamina, cases where def/stam or spelldmg/stam gems are better are very few (only if the socket bonus is worth it). Pure spelldmg gems should never be used unless you're building a pure threat gear/gear piece. They have no place in regular gear.

Helm - your choices are technically the best but I would add a disclaimer that they might not be worth the price.
Shoulders - Fanblade Pauldrons are superior, Spaulders of the Righteous are very good though. Spell dmg gem is a fail, purple dmg/stam gem can be considered here as the socket bonuses are reasonable
Back - all hail the Devilshark Cape
Chest - Jade-Skull Breastplate is the best choice to reach uncrush., Breastplate of the Righteous is the second best choice but the gems have to be 3x 12stamina, socket bonus is worth absolutely nothing.
Wrists - Bracers of the Green Fortress are bis until Void Reaver bracers (T5), Bracers of Dignity can be considered a threat option but nothing more
Hands - considering the price - Felsteel Gloves beat the Iron Tower hands down (resilience is useless after reaching def cap), Gauntlets of Dissension could be viewed as bis if in need of the extra avoidance for uncrush. I would start with Felsteel Gloves and if I get my hands on Gauntlets of Dissension I would take them, but I would probably not farm them. I would list both Gauntlets of Dissension and Felsteel as bis.
Belt - Girdle of the Immovable is bis due to the stamina difference, I wouldn't mind the Girdle of Valorous Deeds listed as second or at shared 1st place since the trade-off for some spelldmg is not that big and you get to save on gems. My personal feeling is, use whichever one you get first.
Legs - all hail the Timewarden's Leggings, but the gems gotta be 3x 12stam. As I stated above, you never gem +9 spelldmg.
Boots - Boots of the Righteous Path are the best threat piece, Boots of the Colossus are the best stamina piece, Flesh Beast's Metal Greaves and Eaglecrest Warboots are the best uncrush pieces. They all have their uses, choose what you need in your gear but as a true prebis I would go with Boots of the Colossus with 2 stamina gems (1green if you lack defense)
Neck - Necklace of the Juggernaut is the best by a small margin ahead of Strength of the Untamed and Maladaar's Blessed Chaplet, the rest are significantly worse - the list should reflect that. In my view getting Strength of the Untamed and saving badges for something else (libram should always be first) is recommended.
Rings - Elementium Band of the Sentry and Band of Impenetrable Defenses are technically the best but chances are you gotta use Andormu's Tear to reach uncrush, list should reflect that. Andormu's Tear is always the first choice while starting at lvl 70, you replace it when you're able to without losing uncrush.
Trinkets - Maybe the trinket section could be split into more tables? Stamina / Threat / Def-uncrush.
Darkmoon Cards were added in 2.1, list should reflect that for now.
Best trinket for both def and uncrush cap is Styleen's (hard to get especialy for horde paladins) but I would mention it. Brooch of the Immortal King came with 2.1.
Figurine of the Colossus is best when struggling to reach uncrush, after that it's mostly farm trinket and can be usefull in big trash pulls (farming normals for rep?).

As stamina trinket go, you won't be able to reach uncrush with them early so they are more of a T4 bis things. Darkmoon Card: Vengeance is probably the best in game trinket for paladin but added in 2.1. Early options for paladin include engi trinket for engineers, Figurine - Living Ruby Serpent for JWCs and Argussian Compass for others. Commander's Badge also came with 2.1.

If I had to list two prebis trinket, it would be: Styleen's Impeding Scarab (Adamantine Figurine) + Figurine of the Colossus.
Goblin Rocket Launcher for stamina and Icon of Silver Crescent for threat.

Weapons - Gavel of Unearthed Secrets is crap, it's too slow. Continuum Blade is your prebis weapon - no discussion here. If you want more threat then get Stormcaller but getting exalted ain't easy.

Shields - Here you pretty much can't go wrong. First I would get Shield of the Wayward Footman, then Platinum Shield of the Valorous (easily farmable). Sha'tar and badge shields are pretty much equal in my eyes so I would get the badge one unless I'm farming exalted for a healer friend.

Librams - all hail Libram of Repentance, your first 15 badges go here, get asap.

How to spend badges?
Libram of Repentance -> Azure-Shield of Coldarra (if you don't have Shat'ar exalted) -> Icon of Silver Crescent -> Necklace of the Juggernaut (maybe don't even waste badges on this - too small of an upgrade)

Just remember, get your 490 defense first, then get 102.4 combined avoidance+block+miss, then go for stamina, then avoidance, then comes the block value and extra block rating (mostly for aoe tanking). You can focus on threat (spell dmg, rest of the stats are pretty much worthless in comparison) once you have no trouble surviving. Also, spell dmg flask and oil go a long way, it's better than compromising your gear with threat pieces early on.

By Proton (995 – 3·6) on 2021/06/22 (Burning Crusade)	
	
Gavel of Unearthed Secrets Gavel of Unearthed Secrets is better than Blade of the Archmage Blade of the Archmage/Stormcaller Stormcaller for Prot paladins regardless of race.

The difference is 11 more stamina and 5 more intellect, 6 less spell crit rating, and a weapon speed difference of 0.9 seconds/swing.

Here are some conversions:
1 Stamina = 10 hp
11 Stamina = 110 hp
With talents Combat Expertise and Sacred Duty there's a 1.16 multiplier added to stamina
11 Stamina * 1.16 = 12.76 Stamina = 127.6 hp

1 Intellect = 15 total mana
5 Intellect = 75 total mana
With talent Divine Intellect there's a 1.10 multiplier added to intellect
5 Intellect = 5.5 Intellect = 82.5 total mana
This isn't a typical talent to take for prot paladins, but worth mentioning just for due diligence.

Paladins receive 1% spell critical strike chance for every 79.4 points of intellect in TBC
5 Intellect = +0.062% spell crit chance
(With talents) 5.5 Intellect = +0.069% spell crit chance
(Notice that's in percent)

At level 70, 22.1 Spell Critical Strike Rating increases your chance to land a Critical Strike with a Spell by 1%
6 spell crit rating = +0.271% chance to crit with spells

So, overall Gavel of Unearthed Secrets Gavel of Unearthed Secrets will give 127.6 extra hp, 75 total mana, and -0.209% spell crit chance.

Here's my math on that:
11 Stamina * 1.16 = 12.76 Stamina = 127.6 hp (with both talents Combat Expertise and Sacred Duty fully utilized)
5 Intellect = 75 total mana (since most prot paladins will not spec into Divine Intellect for the multiplier)
5 Intellect = +0.062% spell crit chance
6 spell crit rating = +0.271%
0.062 - 0.271= -0.209 spell crit chance

There will likely be contention over the weapon speed (2.7 vs 1.8) being a massive difference (0.9 second difference). The math on TPS gets variable when factoring in Seal of Righteousness or Seal of Vengeance proc frequency with swings because it also depends on individual paladin stats, but here's food for thought:

In dungeons, trash and even boss encounters are very brief. For a 3 minute encounter, which is comparative more to raid boss fight length, 180 seconds is 180/2.7=66.66 swings or 180/1.8=100 swings which is in line with the difference of exactly 1/3 difference in speed. But this is not what prot paladins struggle with. Prot paladins are great at established threat, but struggle with snap threat in short encounters.

There are many other sources of threat like Consecration, Holy Shield, Retribution Aura, and even Felsteel Shield Spikes that are more reliable than relying on melee swings and Seal of Righteousness or Seal of Vengeance procs to establish threat. Especially when combined with Avenging Wrath and/or trinkets.

Gavel of Unearthed Secrets Gavel of Unearthed Secrets is better in P1 for survivability, which should be the priority in P1, but just marginally worse for threat.

Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 1
Best in Slot gear for tanks should also include an asterisk that you'll also look to get as much bag space as possible, as your "best" gear may change encounter to encounter. For tanks, there are scenarios where you would want to put on more gear for Threat, and there are encounters where you'll want to gear more towards Mitigation / Survivability. Because of that, we must note that there is not "one" bis list for tanks.

When working on your tanking build, the minimum goals should be the following:

    Immunity to Critical Hits - Crit Cap/ Crit Immunity (490 Defense)
    Immunity to Crushing Blows - Crush Cap / Avoidance Cap (+102.4% Miss/Dodge/Parry/Block with Holy Shield)
    10k+ HP with full enchants/gems, unbuffed

You'll notice a very healthy amount of PvP gear on here, both from Honor Points and Arena Points. We discuss this in our Stat Priority guide, but it should be remembered how Resilience works when looking at our aforementioned goals above. Resilience, like Defense, contributes towards Crit Immunity. Defense, however, also provides Avoidance through additional Dodge/Parry/Block/Miss, whereas Resilience does not. A high amount of PvP gear would work in Heroic dungeons and trash mobs in Raids, since the gear provides high Stamina and high Spell Power, and these mobs cannot crush. It is not advisable to main tank bosses in raids in full Resilience, as you will not be Avoidance Cap doing so; however, you can fit in some PvP gear into your gearset and still be Crush cap, while having better threat from the PvP gear's high Spell Power.

Protection Paladin Tank Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Protection Paladin Tank Best in Slot HubProtection Paladin Tank Pre-Raid Best in Slot
Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 2
Going into Phase 2, we'll be looking to replace a healthy amount of our Phase 1 BiS gear. We don't actively seek Tier 5 specifically for any particular set bonus, although the 4-set bonus is powerful in its own right; we will look to upgrade some of our Tier 4 for Tier 5, as the raw stat upgrades are too powerful to ignore. With that being said, we are still looking to keep our 2-set bonus from our Tier 4, as the damage increase provided to our seals is a very strong bonus that we don't want to discard just yet.

There are an extremely powerful couple of pieces that are unfortunately locked behind Blacksmithing. Since we typically have not recommended this profession as one of the top two to pick for Protection Paladin, we have also included the next best options.

As we learned from Phase 1, although we do not want to ignore threat generation, should we find ourselves in a main tank situation for raid bosses, we always want to prioritize Survivability first. However, it should be noted that one specific encounter, being Morogrim Tidewalker in Serpentshrine Cavern, is where the Protection Paladin shines the brightest as the designated off-tank. Since we won't find ourselves tanking the boss in this encounter, typically, and focusing on the add waves, we should gear heavily more towards Spell Power and threat generation, and discard the stat goal of Avoidance Cap for this encounter. We won't mention a specific "set" to build for that encounter, but consider your gearing options and make sure you look towards having a heavy threat generation set when you make your way to that boss.

With that being said, as we mentioned in the Phase 1 BIS guide, there is no true BiS for tanking. There are various builds that are better suited for particular scenarios.

For Phase 2, many of the best pieces of gear for Protection Paladin tanks will come from Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, and Arenas.

Protection Paladins looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 2
For Protection Paladins, the stat differences between the blue shoulder enchants for each faction are pretty marginal, with Scryers Greater Inscription of the Knight Greater Inscription of the Knight barely winning out vs Aldors Greater Inscription of Warding Greater Inscription of Warding. The main differences between the factions come in the early game with regard to quest rewards and rep vendor items that can help you gear up much faster for tanking heroic dungeons and Phase 2 raids. In terms of reputation gain, The Scryers tend to be an easier faction to gain reputation with, so in that respect, Scryers are the superior option for those who want to obtain their shoulder enchants as quickly as possible.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Protection Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Protection Paladin Tank Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By WaltherLeopold
Updated: 2026/02/10
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3
Phase 3 is a period of major upgrades for Protection Paladin, with some of the best itemization the game has to offer. Lightbringer Armor offers substantial upgrades in every slot, with a significant 4set that will see our already strong AoE capabilities reach new heights, phase 3 also introduces to us very strong off set items such as Faceplate of the Impenetrable Faceplate of the Impenetrable and Praetorian's Legguards Praetorian's Legguards which can rival their tier counterparts in many respects.

These sets are specifically modeled on Archimonde due to his unique place as a phase 3 endboss, he is the hardest and one of the fastest hitters in the tier outside of Illidan. He is an imperfect example, but a very interesting one to build your gear around, because if you can tank Archimonde, you can tank every other boss in phase 3 with the same gear, save Illidan himself who follows a different and unique ruleset.

Illidan Stormrage will often cast Shear, this ability can not miss, but it can be dodged, parried and blocked. The reason this is important is because it will reduce your max HP by 60% for the duration of the debuff it applies, as such you need to have a cumulative 101.8% avoidance whenever he casts Shear so that you will never be hit by it.

Because protection paladins are reliant on layers of avoidance to reach requisite status to tank bosses, it is highly recommended that you do not immediately throw away gear as you get new pieces. Though an item may be listed on this guide as an upgrade, it may not immediately fit well into your current set. You will often have to make small changes to your sets to accommodate “upgrades” to maintain proper amounts of avoidance and crit immunity. To make this easier, we recommend an addon or weakaura that helps you track your avoidance, like Tankadin III.

Protection Paladin Gems & Enchants

Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3
For Protection Paladins, the stat differences between the blue shoulder enchants for each faction are pretty marginal, with Scryers Greater Inscription of the Knight Greater Inscription of the Knight barely winning out vs Aldors Greater Inscription of Warding Greater Inscription of Warding. The main differences between the factions come in the early game with regard to quest rewards and rep vendor items that can help you gear up much faster for tanking heroic dungeons and Phase 2 raids. In terms of reputation gain, The Scryers tend to be an easier faction to gain reputation with, so in that respect, Scryers are the superior option for those who want to obtain their shoulder enchants as quickly as possible.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Protection Paladins, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

At this point in the game, the Aldor spell power enchant Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline also offers better threat stats over the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb, giving a clear overall advantage to Aldor. Although this is not a game-breaking choice for those who went Scryer early on for their Phase 1 items.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Protection Paladin Tank in TBC Classic Phase 3
We recommend Faceplate of the Impenetrable as your go to off set piece in your head. This provides a major defensive boost for a relatively low cost out of any other headpiece in Phase 3. However, there is a caveat in gearing. Should you acquire Praetorian's Legguards Praetorian's Legguards prior to obtaining this, we recommend that you take Lightbringer Faceguard, it's a good helmet which fills most of our needs, but the lack of avoidance is fairly restrictive later down the line, should you want to swap pieces.

Best in Slot Shoulder Armor for Protection Paladin Tank in TBC Classic Phase 3
There are no other real options here; Lightbringer Shoulderguards is strictly the best item in the slot. Pauldrons of Abyssal Fury are worth looking at, but it's by no means a mainstay item for your end phase 3 set. Until then, we recommend going with Justicar Shoulderguards. Our shoulder slot is, in general, pretty underwhelming, but this will at least give you some avoidance to tide you over.

At this stage in the game, the shoulder faction enchants are becoming more obsolete in their main purpose for a Protection Paladin, namely to allow us to reach our avoidance cap goal of 102.4% combined avoidance (miss, parry, dodge, block) in order to be out of reach from crushing blows and critical strikes while holy shield is up. That being said, above certain thresholds, hard avoidance can carry a lot of weight in overall damage reduction, this is why we still give it mentions but still recommend Fortitude of the Scourge Fortitude of the Scourge as your main shoulder enchant from Sapphiron.

Worth noting that Phoenix-Wing Cloak from Al’ar has a little more avoidance than Pepe's Shroud of Pacification, so it may find itself useful in bridging gaps in your sets as you progress.

Best in Slot Ring Jewelry for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3
The 'Rings' section has been divided into two different categories in the toggler section- Mitigation and Threat- in order to provide a more organized list of recommended rings suited to a Paladin tank's gearing style. We'll provide a recommended "pairing", but as mentioned, this can change depending on the encounter.
Your best rings to pair together will be Ring of Sundered Souls with Ashyen's Gift. The former provides a healthy amount of all Survivability stats, while the latter provides great Threat.

There are drawbacks to our recommended BiS mitigation ring. Band of the Eternal Defender is a faction reward ring, meaning you should not reasonably be able to equip it and its threat counterpart at the same time aswell that as of right now, the proc effect [Band of the Eternal Defender] will override an existing Scroll of Protection V Scroll of Protection V & similar effects, removing them.

A central theme of the best rings in this phase is that they are generally loaded on spell power, loaded on spell hit or a healthy combination of both. Since we have such long cooldowns, finding good sources of hit will see a noticeable impact on your threat output. This is why our number #1, 2 & 3 Threat rings are all essentially the same item with varying strength; use any or all as you please. There is no bad choice here.

Best in Slot Librams for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 3
Libram of Repentance is the recommended Phase 3 BiS libram due to providing +5.33% Block Chance while Holy Shield is active, making it easier for tanks to attain uncrushability, which is necessary for tanking raid bosses. This libram is especially useful for maintaining immunity against Shear if main tanking Illidan Stormrage.

Phase 3 introduces a new tanking libram, Tome of the Lightbringer. This libram excels at mitigating damage when tanking large packs of mobs, for example, when tanking trash waves in Hyjal Summit or mobs in Heroic dungeons. With regards to uptime, assuming the paladin tank is casting Judgement every 10 seconds, this libram's proc will be active approximately 50% of the time.

Libram of the Eternal Rest will add 4.477 damage to your consecration ticks, it's a very low return on the cost so we don't recommend this.

Got a question. What does EHP and Threat set means? I am new to prot paladin but i love it ^^

1

	

WaltherLeopold <Guide Author> on 2022/03/21 (Burning Crusade)
EHP stands for Effective Hit/Health Points, which is effectively your total health multiplied with your total damage reduction, typically from armor but may also be extrapolated from things like damage reduction effects and resistance. A threat set is typically a set built for threat in mind. Threat is the product of your damage, with improved righteous fury talented our holy damage has a 1.94 modifier that is converted to threat. Threat is what determines wether a monster is attacking you or not while in combat with it, for a player to take aggro from another player -- that is, for the monster to swap targets -- they must be at or above 110% current monster targets threat in melee or 130% when ranged.

Burning Crusade Classic Protection Paladin Tank Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By WaltherLeopold
Updated: 2026/02/23
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 4
In contrast to phase 3, phase 4 is not a very major phase for Protection Paladins who are already established and raiding the current content; it is an incredible phase for those who want to pick up Protection Paladin and don't want to get into the potential hassle of raiding 25-man. Zul'Aman is, by design, a very fast and efficient raid, and this phase offers very good, but not quite as good as tier 6 gear.

One of the prevailing themes around phase 4 gear for Protection Paladins is that they are very similar to existing phase 3 items in their respective slots, but usually end up just slightly short. This means you may treat a lot of the items from Zul'aman like Black Temple & Mount Hyjal non-tier sidegrades, and given how powerful our tier pieces are from phase 3, it means not much is going to change on the BiS end of things, but mostly that the road to BiS may look drastically different.

This doesn't mean that Phase 4 is not an exciting time for already geared Protection Paladins. Phase 4 gear, in contrast to Phase 3, allots its stats very differently, focusing on very efficient block rating over the more prevalent and valuable dodge that's on tier 6 and Phase 3 gear. This means that phase 4 may help Protection Paladins bridge gaps between existing sets in avoidance and is also very valuable for those that focus on farming content, since the combination of Block Value and Block Rating goes hand in hand with Hobbs way of pulling.

As usual, all gear is valuable, and we strongly recommend that you do not throw out new acquisitions without mindfulness. Likewise, avoidance, especially since it is frankly unintuitive and the game does a poor job of conveying what you need to do, it very easy to make mistakes. We recommend the Weakaura Tankadin III to assist you in surveying your equipped stats.

Protection Paladin Gems & Enchants

Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 4
For Protection Paladins, the stat differences between the blue shoulder enchants for each faction are pretty marginal. We recommend that you go with Aldor in Phase 4, due to having an overall better quality of stats than Scryer for Protection Paladin needs.

Comparing the defensive stats between both shoulder enchants, Greater Inscription of Warding Greater Inscription of Warding is the superior option by a small margin due to the prevalence of dodge. It is worth noting that Greater Inscription of the Knight Greater Inscription of the Knight offers more overall avoidance in the form of Block Rating, but this is largely wasted in phase 4.

Aldor spell power enchant Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline also offers better threat stats over the Scryer alternative Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb, giving a clear overall advantage to Aldor. However, those who went Scryer early on will not see any major disadvantages for doing so and staying with the Blood Elf faction.

Burning Crusade Classic Protection Paladin Tank Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By WaltherLeopold
Updated: 2026/02/23
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Protection Paladin Tank in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Protection Paladin Tank to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 5
Hello and welcome to the fifth and final phase of TBC Classic. We don’t see any outstanding changes to Protection in this phase, but that’s not to say that there are some very interesting things going on in Sunwell.

To start things off, everything in Sunwell has a hidden buff called Sunwell Radiance. The buff will reduce the monster’s chance to roll a miss and dodge on you by 5% and 20%, respectively. In the past phases, we have relied on our avoidance to be crush immune and, secondarily, crit immune, which means that going into Sunwell, we will have to reconsider a few things.

But there’s some good (or bad) news; crush is largely a product of the past in Sunwell, only Lady Sacrolash can crush. This means we do not have to gear for crush immunity anymore; it also means that Block Rating as a stat will go up quite a bit in value in terms of threat and mitigation that it produces. It also means we can no longer reliably shortcut crit immunity through avoidance.

Another change from p3 gear is that Block Value will see a return to form. We can reach significant levels of block value, which will see a lot of use not only on trash but also on bosses. Sets weighted towards Mitigation can see upwards of and beyond 1k+ Block Value, which will net you a fairly critical amount of extra damage reduction.

Sunwell gear also carries a substantially higher ilevel over past phases. We will see a drastic increase in EHP, armor, and a higher potential TPS (but we’ll get into that). Thing to keep in mind, though, is that for the most part, our mitigation and tanking value goes up, our threat generally stays stagnant, and might even go down for some people. The reason our threat stagnates is that a lot of the fights in Sunwell are on the surface very mitigation-heavy, bosses generally hit for much harder than T6 bosses, and few fights favor the Protection Paladin playstyle. This is unfortunate because some DPS see major upgrades in Sunwell, and with it, also a lower potential threat ceiling.

Because the fights are challenging to gear for, it’s more pertinent than ever to consider your options on a per-boss/assignment basis, as we are facing quite a few tank gear checks in Sunwell. This is not at all to discourage you from tanking bosses in P5 as a Protection Paladin, because we certainly can, and with Sunwell gear, we are slated to be the most mitigation-focused tank out of all 3 options. You can expect to take less damage than both druids and warriors by late-game P5 if you choose to gear for it.
To summarize, our balanced sets will see a large growth in EHP and mitigation, but generally stay stagnant or even lose threat on single & multi-target, while our TPS sets see a good potential growth in threat and a decent amount of EHP. Ultimately, our mitigation-focused sets see a massive growth in EHP and mitigation while barely going up in threat.

The way gear is acquired in Sunwell is also unique; besides the addition of 3 new tier 6 pieces in the boots, belt, and wrist slots, a lot of gear in itself will act as currency tokens. Sunwell gear can be swapped at Yrma at the cost of Sunmote Sunmote and the corresponding item. The conversion is not universal, and items purchasable this way have a specific corresponding item. Below is a chart of any relevant Protection Paladin obtainable through this system.

Now more than ever, all gear is valuable, and we strongly recommend that you do not throw out new acquisitions without mindfulness. We also strongly recommend that you download Tankadin to help you get a good oversight of your gear in p5. We are also heartily welcoming you to visit us at the Light Club for any questions or join in on the deep analysis and discussion of anything related to Paladins in TBC Classic.

Aldor vs. Scryer for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 5
By now, in Phase 5, most established paladins have already chosen their Shattrath reputation, but there are a few things to keep in mind as it stands. For new players, Scryer offers more but less valuable Avoidance, worse off-spec/threat shoulder enchants, a good starting-out ring, and a great threat trinket for anything except Sunwell. Aldor has the best shoulder enchants for Avoidance, threat, and off-specs, but also offers very little to a new Paladin other than a pretty mediocre chest at this point.

Shattered Sun Necklaces
What is more relevant to already raiding and geared Protection Paladins in p5 are the Shattered Sun Necklaces. These are generic tank/caster necks that have a significant boon to them, a proc that will depend on what Shattrath faction you are exalted with. The necks are reputation locked behind Exalted with Shattered Sun Offensive, which is the new faction introduced in P5 that is tied to Sunwell and Magister’s Terrace. You’ll acquire rep with them mostly through dailies on the Isle of Quel’Danas.

Shattered Sun Pendant of Resolve Shattered Sun Pendant of Resolve being the dedicated tanking neck will give you +100 dodge rating proc (5.28% dodge) if you're Aldor and +100 expertise rating proc (-6.25% dodge/parry) if you're Scryer.
Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen Shattered Sun Pendant of Acumen is meant to be the dedicated caster DPS neck, but it has potential for us. The Aldor proc will award you +120 spell power, and the Scryer proc will shoot a bolt at your target for 333-367 damage (arcane).

We have continuously used strong proc based items throughout TBC with items such as Band of the Eternal Sage Band of the Eternal Sage, Tome of Fiery Redemption Tome of Fiery Redemption due to the way how these effects will be amplified on trash pulls given how often there is a lull between packs and since we are often tanking 90%+ of any given trash pack in a raid or dungeon scenario these effects will ramp significantly with either amount of incoming attacks or amount of targets around you.

In terms of single target, these necks are very uninteresting to us Protection Paladins, and we will opt for better necks for either threat or survivability, but you will see these potentially shine in large massed pulls where the neck will proc fairly early into a pull, giving us either a pretty good survivability boon, or a great threat proc.

Best in Slot Ring Jewelry for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 5
The 'Rings' section has been divided into two different categories - Mitigation and Threat- to provide a more organized list of recommended rings suited to a Paladin tank's gearing style.
Mitigation Rings
The new pair of Ring of Hardened Resolve, Ring of the Stalwart Protector go very well together for any mitigation purposes with very impressive EHP. Block Value in general takes a twist this phase with such high iLevels, the relative EHP value on Hardened Resolve makes it a very beefy ring, while Stalwart Protector's solid avoidance and great EHP makes it an almost linear upgrade from older hard avoidance rings such as Ring of Sundered Souls.

Otherwise, it's more of the same, the avoidance rings such as Seventh Ring of the Tirisfalen are still very good for the amount of block that they provide, but are also somewhat lackluster since crush is not much of a thing in Sunwell. Band of the Eternal Defender is still a ring with a drawback, even though it's pretty decent with the bigger emphasis on block value, but you have to detract value when you account for how the proc is unreliable, and it removes the buff from Scroll of Protection V Scroll of Protection V.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 4
We don't have many universally good items in these slots, we have very powerful but mostly circumstantial trinkets, and we recommend for users to be advised to change trinkets frequently based on the incoming pull. As such, there is no real shortcut to trinkets, and outside of stamina, one size rarely fits all.
Stamina Trinkets
Stamina trinkets are primarily recommended for maximizing HP only after Defense Cap (490 Defense) and Uncrushability (102.4% Avoidance + Block) have been reached. Commendation of Kael'thas is the recommended BiS trinket for Stamina; it has the highest stamina of any trinket, in tandem with the proc makes for a potentially very strong trinket on sets with higher EHP.

Darkmoon Card: Vengeance is still a great stamina trinket for what it is, but Sunwell has less trash, fewer dual-wielding bosses, and less threat issues (where you might want to use this), making it somewhat obsolete. That said, the differences are negligible as usual.

Best in Slot Librams for Protection Paladin Tank in Phase 5
We don’t get a new libram this phase, but the increased viability of Shield Specialization will give you additional value on Tome of the Lightbringer as well as Libram of Repentance. All in all, what remained good in Phase 4 is still pretty good in Phase 5, but the dichotomy has shifted slightly. We recommend that you keep an open mind with Librams because you can go in a lot of different directions and none of them are immediately better than the other.
