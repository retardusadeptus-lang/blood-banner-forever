Rogue DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Simonize
Updated: 2026/01/27
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Rogue DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Rogue DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Rogue DPS Best in Slot Hub
Pre-Raid Best In Slot Gear for Rogue DPS in Phase 1
There are a lot of great gear options for rogues before entering your first raid. With a plethora of quest rewards, crafted items, and dungeon drops, you shouldn't have too much trouble getting your rogue ready to go. I suggest you focus on trying to pick up whatever pieces of Wastewalker Armor that you can. This is a very powerful item set, and you will still be using two pieces of it in your Phase 1 Best In Slot setup!

Rogues looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Rogue DPS Gems & Enchants
Should Rogue DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer TBC Classic Phase 1?
For rogues, choosing your Shattrath faction either The Aldor or The Scryers doesn't make that big of a difference in terms of raid performance. The main longstanding benefit will be the shoulder enchants from these factions. Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance from the former is very slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from the latter, but it is such a minuscule difference that you are unlikely to ever notice it. You will do just fine choosing whichever faction you prefer.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for PvE damage dealing.
    Aldor offers a decent sword at Exalted, Vindicator's Brand Vindicator's Brand. This is good for starters, but it is already outclassed by other phase 1 weapons.
    Scryers offer Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt which can make leveling Blacksmithing Blacksmithing much cheaper.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

Overall, picking Aldor will benefit you more, however, in a Pre-Raid setting.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Rogues, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

We have created many different best-in-slot lists, including a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Rogue DPS Best in Slot Guides
Best In Slot Gear for Rogue DPS in Phase 1
In Phase 1, there are a couple of different gear setups that are very good. Most slots are still just one best item, but a few have alternatives that won't meaningfully change your damage output. You are going to want to use two or three pieces of the Netherblade set and two pieces of the Wastewalker Armor set. The recommended pieces are listed below, but as long as you stick to two pieces per set, you won't be too far behind. Be sure to read on to know what you need to get on your Rogue, and what is a little more flexible.

Below is an example gear set that doesn't use items from world bosses. The handful of items from world bosses are only very, very slightly better than this setup - you'll do just fine if you skip those items.

Rogue DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Simonize
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Rogue DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Rogue DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Rogue DPS Best in Slot HubRogue DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot
Best In Slot Gear for Rogue DPS in Phase 2
In Phase 2, you will want to use the four-piece bonus of the rogue tier set, Deathmantle. This is undoubtedly a very powerful bonus, and randomly making your finishing moves free makes your rotation a little more dynamic and varied - very fun if you ask me! There are two good offset pieces to choose from to complement the tier set, either Bloodsea Brigand's Vest or Shoulderpads of the Stranger.

The Shoulderpads of the Stranger setup is a bit starved for hit rating, but makes up for it with other stats. If you choose Shoulderpads of the Stranger, you'll want to switch most of your Glinting Noble Topaz Glinting Noble Topaz to Rigid Dawnstone Rigid Dawnstone. The gems shown here are for the recommended Bloodsea Brigand's Vest setup.

Below is an example sgear set that uses Bloodsea Brigand's Vest. I recommend this choice because it is more flexible on gem slots and hit rating while gearing up compared to the Shoulderpads of the Stranger setup. There is no noticeable difference in performance between the two offset piece choices.

For more information on Rogue Enchants and Gems for their BiS gear, take a look at our guide below:

Should Rogue Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Just like in Phase 1, choosing your Shattrath faction either The Aldor or The Scryers doesn't make that big of a difference in terms of raid performance. The main longstanding benefit will be the shoulder enchants from these factions. Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance from The Aldor is very slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from The Scryer's, but it is such a minuscule difference that you are unlikely to ever notice it. You will do just fine choosing whichever faction you prefer.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for PvE damage dealing.
    Aldor offers a decent sword at Exalted, Vindicator's Brand Vindicator's Brand. This is a great starting weapon for fresh rogues.
    Scryers offer Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt, which can make leveling Blacksmithing Blacksmithing much cheaper.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

Burning Crusade Classic Rogue DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Simonize
Updated: 2026/02/14
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 3 Black Temple and Mount Hyjal for Rogue DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Rogue DPS to acquire while they progress during the third phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Rogue DPS in Phase 3
In Phase 3, Rogues see a pretty substantial power boost thanks to another very well itemized tier set, the Slayer's Armor. Both bonuses on this set are very good, with the two-piece being even more powerful than the four-piece bonus. All pieces of the set are strong individual items as well, and Cursed Vision of Sargeras Cursed Vision of Sargeras is the only non-set item that is better than the set item equivalent in that slot.

Some lucky few of us Rogues may even be able to obtain the exceptionally powerful Legendary weapons, The Twin Blades of Azzinoth. These are the best weapons available for Rogues for the remainder of the expansion, and upgrading to these weapons is about as much damage gain as upgrading every single piece of armor from Phase 2 best in slot to Phase 3 best in slot combined.

Rogues looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Rogue Gems & Enchants
Should Rogue DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Just like in Phases 1 and 2, choosing your Shattrath faction, either The Aldor or The Scryers, doesn't make that big of a difference in terms of raid performance. The main longstanding benefit will be the shoulder enchants from these factions. Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance from the Aldor is very slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from the Scryers, but it is such a minuscule difference that you are unlikely ever to notice it. You will do just fine choosing whichever faction you prefer.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for PvE damage dealing.
    Aldor offers a decent sword at Exalted, Vindicator's Brand Vindicator's Brand. This is a great starting weapon for fresh Rogues.
    Scryers offer Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt which can make leveling Blacksmithing much cheaper.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Rogues, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Weapons for Rogue DPS in TBC Classic Phase 3
If you're lucky, you may be able to get one of the most coveted Legendary weapons in all of World of Warcraft, the Warglaive of Azzinoth. A set of The Twin Blades of Azzinoth is as much of a damage upgrade for a Rogue as it is to upgrade every single piece of BiS armor from Phase 2 to the BiS option in Phase 3 combined. Burning Crusade Classic Rogue DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Simonize
Updated: 2026/02/14
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Rogue DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Rogue DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Rogue DPS in Phase 4
In Phase 4, Rogues do not have many changes in their best in slot gearing compared to Phase 3. Zul'Aman and the new Badge of Justice Badge of Justice gear bring a total of one definite upgrade and three situational items or close alternatives. The main upgrade piece is the Signet of Primal Wrath Signet of Primal Wrath, while the close alternatives are Nyn'jah's Tabi Boots Nyn'jah's Tabi Boots, Berserker's Call Berserker's Call, and Cloak of Fiends Cloak of Fiends.

Rogues who do not have the full Phase 3 BiS may find several other upgrades that they can get before reaching best in slot, depending on which they already have.

Rogues looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Rogue Gems & Enchants
Should Rogue DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Just like in previous phases, choosing your Shattrath faction, either The Aldor or The Scryers, doesn't make that big of a difference in terms of raid performance. The main longstanding benefit will be the shoulder enchants from these factions. Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance from the Aldor is very slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from the Scryers, but it is such a minuscule difference that you are unlikely ever to notice it. You will do just fine choosing whichever faction you prefer.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for PvE damage dealing.
    Aldor offers a decent sword at Exalted, Vindicator's Brand Vindicator's Brand. This is a great starting weapon for fresh Rogues.
    Scryers offer Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt which can make leveling Blacksmithing much cheaper.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Rogues, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Rogue DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Simonize
Updated: 2026/02/24
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Rogue DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Rogue DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.
Best In Slot Gear for Rogue DPS in Phase 5
Phase 5 brings the absolute most powerful items in all of Burning Crusade. The challenge of the Sunwell Plateau raid is appropriate for the power of the items - these bosses won't fall easily. The Slayer's Bracers Slayer's Bracers, Slayer's Boots Slayer's Boots, and/or Slayer's Belt Slayer's Belt are so powerful that you will even use two of them during Wrath of the Lich King classic!

Much of the leather gear from Sunwell is not only best in slot for rogues, but also for other physical DPS like Enhancement Shamans, Hunters, and Warriors. For the most part, these classes have mail or plate alternatives that aren't quite as good as the leather option, but still big upgrades over what they had in phase four. A raid trying to optimize their loot distribution for overall raid performance should think about sending the leather options to rogues, mail options to hunters and shamans, and plate options to warriors first. The sooner you give the leather options to the non-leather classes, the sooner your raid loot starts turning into Void Crystal Void Crystals instead of being upgrades for your players.

Rogues looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Rogue Gems & Enchants

Should Rogue DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Just like in Phases 1, 2, 3, and 4, choosing your Shattrath faction either The Aldor or The Scryers doesn't make that big of a difference in terms of raid performance. The main longstanding benefit will be the shoulder enchants from these factions. Greater Inscription of Vengeance Greater Inscription of Vengeance from The Aldor is very slightly better than Greater Inscription of the Blade Greater Inscription of the Blade from The Scryers, but it is such a minuscule difference that you are unlikely to ever notice it. You will do just fine choosing whichever faction you prefer.

    Aldor offer a very slightly superior shoulder enchant for PvE damage dealing.
    Aldor offers a decent sword at Exalted, Vindicator's Brand Vindicator's Brand. This is a great starting weapon for fresh rogues.
    Scryers offer Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt Plans: Enchanted Adamantite Belt which can make leveling Blacksmithing Blacksmithing much cheaper.
    Scryers are usually the less popular faction, making their reputation turn-in items often cheaper than Aldor equivalents.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Rogues, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Rogue DPS in TBC Classic Phase 5
Dragonspine Trophy and Blackened Naaru Sliver are your two best trinkets for boss damage, and they aren't too bad for trash damage either. These are both very strong trinkets that are quite far ahead of the third and fourth options.

As you gear up in Sunwell, you may still be using Warp-Spring Coil. With all the available passive armor penetration on your equipment, you may reach a point where part of the Warp-Spring's proc is wasted, and then it becomes much weaker. When you reach about 1000 passive armor penetration on your gear, Warp-Spring Coil, Shard of Contempt, and Berserker's Call are all about equal, and as you gain even more, Warp-Spring falls behind.

If you want to use Shard of Contempt, you will need to be careful about going over your expertise cap. Expertise rating beyond the cap provides no damage benefit for your character and generally makes expertise items poor choices. Being exactly at the cap isn't very important, but it is important not to be over the cap to ensure you aren't wasting stats from your items. The expertise rating cap for human rogues is 44, and for non-human rogues is 63.

Badge of the Swarmguard from WoW Classic remains as the strongest second trinket in the game for short combat engagements, outperforming even Dragonspine Trophy. DST catches up around 60 seconds of continuous combat and is slightly better the longer the fight goes beyond that. If you've got time to wait for the 30-second equip cooldown, Badge of the Swarmguard is definitely worth equipping for short combats even if you have the best trinkets at the top of this list.
