Shadow Priest DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Passion
Updated: 2026/07/14
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Shadow Priest DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Shadow Priest DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Shadow Priest DPS in Pre-Raid
The gear below is the best you can possibly obtain before entering a Raid, but a lot of it can be replaced very easily in Karazhan, which, after Phase 1, is effectively pre-raid content. Most of the gear comes from Tailoring. Even if you don't intend to keep Tailoring in future Phases, it is vital to have it before entering 25-man raids.

A lot of this gear is also ...of Shadow Wrath. These pieces offer no white stats, but their entire budget is spent on Shadow Damage, meaning that in most cases, they will have significantly more Spell Power than other pieces in the slot. Playing with a lot of ...of Shadow Wrath items will mean you are very squishy, but with smart play, this shouldn't be an issue until Phase 2 Raids, by then you can replace most of them. For most of your gearing until Phase 3, you will be using 3 pieces ...of Shadow Wrath, in your Wrist, Wand, and Back slots.

You will unlikely get some of the items at this point, but they are listed because they are the best option. Items like Ring of the Fallen God Ring of the Fallen God and Band of the Inevitable Band of the Inevitable are from Classic raids. If you have them already, fantastic, you can wear them for a long time. If you don't, I wouldn't worry about getting them; other alternatives aren't quite as good but are significantly easier to get.

Shadow Priest Gems & Enchants
Should Shadow Priest DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
All of the gear from Aldor or Scryers is entirely negligible and will be replaced as soon as possible, meaning that the important parts are the Shoulder Enchants and the Recipes. Each faction gives a recipe for a Tailoring Leg Enchant: Aldor gives healing, and Scryers gives damage. The actual enchant can be freely traded, and they both tend to sell for around the same price, so this should not impact most players' decisions.

The biggest part of Aldor vs Scryer is the Shoulder Enchant. There are a few Shoulder Enchants that casters consider, and the best one depends entirely on class and spec. For Shadow Priest, the answer is simple, as Aldor provides a significant amount more Spell Power than Scryers and is by far the best Shoulder Enchant for Shadow. If you decide against going with the Aldor, your best option will be Zandalar Signet of Mojo Zandalar Signet of Mojo, but let's be real, no one likes going back to Zul'Gurub, at least not in TBC.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Shadow Priests, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Shadow Priest in TBC Classic Pre-Raid
Icon of the Silver Crescent is bought with Badges of Justice and should be bought as soon as you possibly can. This trinket will likely last you until Phase 4, and having on-demand Spell Power is incredible for Shadow Priests.

If you got Mark of the Champion during Classic, you're in luck. This trinket will be the Best in Slot choice against Undead and Demons for the majority of TBC. Unlike the other Classic items listed here, this item could absolutely be worth farming for you. It is a guaranteed drop from Kel'Thuzad, so if you can manage to soft reserve or even hard reserve it, it is absolutely worth the effort.

Quagmirran's Eye will likely be your go-to trinket for a while. The haste proc can feel kind of volatile at times, and losing most of a proc to Mind Flay always feels bad, but if the proc hits right, it is a fantastic DPS boost.

Shadow Priest DPS Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Passion
Updated: 2026/02/19
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Shadow Priest DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Shadow Priest DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Shadow Priest DPS in Phase 1
The absolute BiS set uses ...of Shadow Wrath in 6 slots, ending up with just over 5000 Health and Mana. Even ignoring that this set is likely to get you killed because of this, the set is also almost impossible to actually achieve in-game, using all 3 ...of Shadow Wrath pieces from Karazhan, when most people will never even see one drop.

Luckily, Shadow Priests can move a lot of gear around to deal with these issues. You absolutely do not need to wear any of the ...of Shadow Wrath pieces, and they all have solid alternatives.

Shadow Priests looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Shadow Priest Gems & Enchants
Should Shadow Priest DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
All of the gear from Aldor or Scryers is entirely negligible and will be replaced as soon as possible, meaning that the important parts are the Shoulder Enchants and the Recipes. Each faction gives a recipe for a Tailoring Leg Enchant: Aldor gives healing, and Scryers gives damage. The actual enchant can be freely traded, and they both tend to sell for around the same price, so this should not impact most players' decisions.

The biggest part of Aldor vs Scryer is the Shoulder Enchant. There are a few Shoulder Enchants that casters consider, and the best one depends entirely on class and spec. For Shadow Priest, the answer is simple, as Aldor provides a significant amount more Spell Power than Scryers and is by far the best Shoulder Enchant for Shadow. If you decide against going with the Aldor, your best option will be Zandalar Signet of Mojo Zandalar Signet of Mojo, but let's be real, no one likes going back to Zul'Gurub, at least not in TBC.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Shadow Priests, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Passion
Updated: 2026/05/12
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Shadow Priest DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Shadow Priest DPS to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot HubShadow Priest DPS Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Shadow Priest DPS in Phase 2
Shadow Priest gearing is fairly straightforward. Your goal in the early Phases is to get as much Spell Power as you possibly can.

    Spell Power - This is your main stat for almost all of The Burning Crusade, and outvalues almost every other stat available.
    Spell Critical Strike Rating - Critical rating is mostly wasted on Shadow Priests, as only two of your five spells can crit. You can typically assume that 6 Spell Critical is worth one Spell Power.
    Spell Hit Rating - Spell Hit has a few breakpoints that are relevant. The first one is 76 rating, putting you at 6% bonus chance to Hit with spells, enough to be Hit capped against all enemies when combined with 5 points in Shadow Focus. As you get more gear, you may end up over the Hit cap. If you are 2% over the Hit cap, you can remove a point in this talent and put it elsewhere.
    Spell Penetration - This stat is almost entirely wasted in PvE content, as Curse of the Elements will, in almost every situation, reduce the enemies' Shadow Resistance to 0.
    Stamina - A mostly incidental stat. Most gear has Stamina, and there are very few situations where you would need to prioritize more.
    Intellect - Governs your mana pool and gives a very small amount of Spell Critical. Rarely relevant for deciding gear pieces, but it can be beneficial.
    Spirit and Mana Per 5 Seconds - These stats give mana regeneration, which substantially affects your longevity in combat. Spirit is more valuable for Shadow Priests because of Spirit Tap, but neither is a deciding factor when choosing gear. The exception to this is if you will be raiding with a Priest specced into Improved Divine Spirit. In this case, roughly 10 Spirit is equivalent to one Spell Power.

    Shadow Priests looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

    Shadow Priest Gems & Enchants
    Should Shadow Priest DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
    By Phase 2, you will likely have replaced all Aldor or Scryers' gear, so the biggest part of Aldor vs Scryer is the Shoulder Enchant. There are a few Shoulder Enchants that casters consider, and the best one depends entirely on class and spec.

    For Shadow Priests, though, the answer is simple. Aldor provides a significant amount more Spell Power than Scryers and is by far the best Shoulder Enchant for Shadow. If you decide against going Aldor, your best option will be Zandalar Signet of Mojo Zandalar Signet of Mojo, but let's be real, no one likes going back to Zul'Gurub, at least not in TBC.

    If you are Undead Undead, then Cowl of the Grand Engineer is slightly better than Hood of the Avatar, if you can manage without the hit. The reason for this is that Devouring Plague means you benefit slightly more from flat Spell Power than the Focus proc from your meta gem.

    Even with all these options, you will likely want to keep wearing Spellstrike Hood until you can break the Spellstrike Infusion set bonus in both slots at once.

So for phase 1 and phase 2 the best chest enchant has been stated to be Major Spirit if you have the improved divine spirit buff and Exceptional stats otherwise. IMO the 6mp5 restore mana prime enchant should be added as second preference and the exceptional stats as 3rd preference. It should at least be mentioned though, as it's definitely one to consider.

Major Spirit = Equivalent to 3mp5 (while casting) and also 1.5 SP if you have the improved spirit buff.
Exceptional Stats = 6 intellect (0.9mp5), 6 spirit (1.8mp5) which together is equivalent to 2.7mp5 while casting and 0.075% crit. You also get alittle more initial mana that you can convert to mp5 depending on fight length.
Restore Mana Prime = 6mp5 (while casting).

To me, assuming you don't value stamina at all (which you won't if min-maxing) and the extra initial mana from the intellect (90 mana) will be more than gained back from the extra 3.3mp5 you in any fight over a couple of minutes, such that it's better than the 0.075 crit you also get from the intellect. It seems to me that Restore Mana prime on chest is a marginal second place winner or at least should be mentioned as a third preference.

Burning Crusade Classic Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Passion
Updated: 2026/02/28
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Shadow Priest DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Shadow Priest DPS to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Shadow Priest DPS in Phase 3
Shadow Priest gearing is fairly straightforward. Your goal is mainly to get as much spell power as you possibly can, and when available, grab spell haste as your second priority.

    Spell Power - This is your main stat for almost all of The Burning Crusade, and outvalues almost every other stat available.
    Spell Critical Strike Rating - Critical rating is mostly wasted on Shadow Priests, as only two of your five spells can crit. You can typically assume that 6 spell critical is worth one spell power.
    Spell Hit Rating - Spell Hit has a few relevant breakpoints. The first one is at 76 rating, putting you at 6% bonus chance to hit with spells, enough to be hit capped against all enemies when combined with 5 points in Shadow Focus. As you get more gear, you may end up over the hit cap. If you are 2% over the hit cap, you can remove a point in this talent and put it elsewhere.
    Spell Penetration - This stat is almost entirely wasted in PvE content, as Curse of the Elements will, in almost every situation, reduce the enemies' Shadow Resistance to 0.
    Spell Haste Rating - A new stat introduced in TBC, which isn't readily available until Phase 3. This decreases your cast times and global cooldown. It is an incredible stat for Shadow Priests and outweighs every other stat except spell power. Your DoT effects do not benefit from this, but the primary gain is faster Mind Flay casts. Mind Flay always hits 3 times over its duration, but haste reduces its duration, meaning you get each tick sooner. Because of this, you can potentially fit more ticks between your other casts, and Mind Flay casts do make up the bulk of your casts during a fight.
    Stamina - A mostly incidental stat. Most gear has Stamina, and there are very few situations where you would need to prioritize more.
    Intellect - Governs your mana pool and gives a very small amount of spell critical. Rarely relevant for deciding gear pieces, but it can be beneficial.
    Spirit and Mana Per 5 Seconds - These stats give mana regeneration, which substantially affects your longevity in combat. Spirit is more valuable for Shadow Priests because of Spirit Tap, but neither is a deciding factor when choosing gear. The exception to this is if you will be raiding with a Priest specced into Improved Divine Spirit. In this case, roughly 10 Spirit is equivalent to one spell power.

    Should Shadow Priest DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
    By Phase 3, you will likely have replaced all Aldor or Scryers' gear, so the biggest part of Aldor vs Scryer is the Shoulder Enchant. There are a few shoulder enchants that casters consider, and the best one depends entirely on class and spec.

    For Shadow Priests, though, the answer is simple. Aldor provides a significant amount more spell power than Scryers and is by far the best shoulder enchant for Shadow. If you decide against going Aldor, your best option will be Zandalar Signet of Mojo Zandalar Signet of Mojo, but let's be real, no one likes going back to Zul'Gurub, at least not in TBC.

    If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Shadow Priests, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Passion
Updated: 2026/02/28
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Shadow Priest DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Shadow Priest DPS to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Shadow Priest DPS in Phase 4
Shadow Priest gearing is fairly straightforward. Your goal is mainly to get as much spell power as you possibly can, and when available, grab spell haste as your second priority.

    Spell Power - This is your main stat for almost all of The Burning Crusade, and outvalues almost every other stat available.
    Spell Critical Strike Rating - Critical rating is mostly wasted on Shadow Priests, as only two of your five spells can crit. You can typically assume that 6 spell critical is worth one spell power.
    Spell Hit Rating - Spell Hit has a few relevant breakpoints. The first one is at 76 rating, putting you at 6% bonus chance to hit with spells, enough to be hit capped against all enemies when combined with 5 points in Shadow Focus. As you get more gear, you may end up over the hit cap. If you are 2% over the hit cap, you can remove a point in this talent and put it elsewhere.
    Spell Penetration - This stat is almost entirely wasted in PvE content, as Curse of the Elements will, in almost every situation, reduce the enemies' Shadow Resistance to 0.
    Spell Haste Rating - A new stat introduced in TBC, which isn't readily available until Phase 3. This decreases your cast times and global cooldown. It is an incredible stat for Shadow Priests and outweighs every other stat except spell power. Your DoT effects do not benefit from this, but the primary gain is faster Mind Flay casts. Mind Flay always hits 3 times over its duration, but haste reduces its duration, meaning you get each tick sooner. Because of this, you can potentially fit more ticks between your other casts, and Mind Flay casts do make up the bulk of your casts during a fight.
    Stamina - A mostly incidental stat. Most gear has Stamina, and there are very few situations where you would need to prioritize more.
    Intellect - Governs your mana pool and gives a very small amount of spell critical. Rarely relevant for deciding gear pieces, but it can be beneficial.
    Spirit and Mana Per 5 Seconds - These stats give mana regeneration, which substantially affects your longevity in combat. Spirit is more valuable for Shadow Priests because of Spirit Tap, but neither is a deciding factor when choosing gear. The exception to this is if you will be raiding with a Priest specced into Improved Divine Spirit. In this case, roughly 10 Spirit is equivalent to one spell power.

    Zul'Aman is more of a catch-up raid than anything else; most of the items exist to help new characters get raid-ready as opposed to upgrading current raiders. Despite this, there are still some interesting items for sure. For example, Hex Shrunken Head Hex Shrunken Head is very good and will replace whatever you are using. Because Zul'Aman is a 10-man raid with a 3-day lockout, you are much more likely to get everything you'll want soon enough.

    Shadow Priests looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

    Shadow Priest Gems & Enchants
    Should Shadow Priest DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
    By Phase 4, you will likely have replaced all Aldor or Scryers' gear, so the biggest part of Aldor vs Scryer is the shoulder enchant. There are a few shoulder enchants that casters consider, and the best one depends entirely on class and spec.

    For Shadow Priests, though, the answer is simple. The Aldor enchant, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, provides a significant amount more spell power than the Scryers, Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb, does, and is by far the best shoulder enchant for Shadow. If you decide against going Aldor, your best option will be Zandalar Signet of Mojo Zandalar Signet of Mojo, but let's be real, no one likes going back to Zul'Gurub, at least not in TBC.

    If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Shadow Priests, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Burning Crusade Classic Shadow Priest DPS Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Passion
Updated: 2026/02/28
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Shadow Priest DPS in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Shadow Priest DPS to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Shadow Priest DPS in Phase 5
Shadow Priest gearing is fairly straightforward. Your goal is mainly to get as much spell power as you possibly can, and when available, grab spell haste as your second priority.

    Spell Power - This is your main stat for almost all of The Burning Crusade, and outvalues almost every other stat available.
    Spell Critical Strike Rating - Critical rating is mostly wasted on Shadow Priests, as only two of your five spells can crit. You can typically assume that 6 spell critical is worth one spell power.
    Spell Hit Rating - Spell Hit has a few relevant breakpoints. The first one is at 76 rating, putting you at 6% bonus chance to hit with spells, enough to be hit capped against all enemies when combined with 5 points in Shadow Focus. As you get more gear, you may end up over the hit cap. If you are 2% over the hit cap, you can remove a point in this talent and put it elsewhere.
    Spell Penetration - This stat is almost entirely wasted in PvE content, as Curse of the Elements will, in almost every situation, reduce the enemies' Shadow Resistance to 0.
    Spell Haste Rating - A new stat introduced in TBC, which isn't readily available until Phase 3. This decreases your cast times and global cooldown. It is an incredible stat for Shadow Priests and outweighs every other stat except spell power. Your DoT effects do not benefit from this, but the primary gain is faster Mind Flay casts. Mind Flay always hits 3 times over its duration, but haste reduces its duration, meaning you get each tick sooner. Because of this, you can potentially fit more ticks between your other casts, and Mind Flay casts do make up the bulk of your casts during a fight.
    Stamina - A mostly incidental stat. Most gear has Stamina, and there are very few situations where you would need to prioritize more.
    Intellect - Governs your mana pool and gives a very small amount of spell critical. Rarely relevant for deciding gear pieces, but it can be beneficial.
    Spirit and Mana Per 5 Seconds - These stats give mana regeneration, which substantially affects your longevity in combat. Spirit is more valuable for Shadow Priests because of Spirit Tap, but neither is a deciding factor when choosing gear. The exception to this is if you will be raiding with a Priest specced into Improved Divine Spirit. In this case, roughly 10 Spirit is equivalent to one spell power.

    This is it, the final raid of The Burning Crusade. Sunwell Plateau is in a very special situation; the leap in gear quality is massive, and because of this, you will replace almost every piece of gear, no matter what you had before Phase 5.

    We will still aim to pick up the 4-piece bonus from Absolution Regalia, but due to new Tier 6 pieces in Phase 6, the gear slots used will change. Ideally, you will use Shoulderpads of Absolution Shoulderpads of Absolution from Phase 3 together with Bracers of Absolution Bracers of Absolution, Treads of Absolution Treads of Absolution, and Cord of Absolution Cord of Absolution from Phase 5. The new Tier 6 pieces are absolutely outstanding, and you would most likely want to use them even without the set bonus, so getting 10% Mind Blast damage is just a cherry on top.

    Should Shadow Priest DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
    By Phase 5, you will likely have replaced all Aldor or Scryers' gear, so the biggest part of Aldor vs Scryer is the shoulder enchant. There are a few shoulder enchants that casters consider, and the best one depends entirely on class and spec.

    For Shadow Priests, though, the answer is simple. The Aldor enchant, Greater Inscription of Discipline Greater Inscription of Discipline, provides a significant amount more spell power than the Scryers, Greater Inscription of the Orb Greater Inscription of the Orb, does, and is by far the best shoulder enchant for Shadow. If you decide against going Aldor, your best option will be Zandalar Signet of Mojo Zandalar Signet of Mojo, but let's be real, no one likes going back to Zul'Gurub, at least not in TBC.

    If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Shadow Priests, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Great list. A couple minor things i wanted to comment on/mildly disagree with:

Trinket:

Timbals should be just above Darkmoon crusade and Mark of the Champion. It sims 2/3 dps higher than crusade on https://wowsims.github.io/tbc/shadow_priest/. The sim also plays to crusade cards stengths (tank and spank with no swapping/chance of stacks dropping) and inflates it's value above it's real world value and it still loses. It's only a fraction of a dps better than mark of the champion though, but edges out in most sim iterations.

Given mark of the champions/crusade cards difficulty/time required to acquire, this could prevent a lot of spriests putting in a lost of wasted effort trying to get them.

Neck:

Guardians pendant of subjugation should be higher up in the list as it becomes better than loop of cursed bones once you've got a good chunk haste elsewhere (it sims just above loop of cursed bones for me in near p4 bis), or a note should be added for it to explain that. Obviously this assumes you put an epic gem in it.

Weapons:

Vengeful glad gavel/spellblade should be above above scryers blade of focus. You get slightly less stam+int (and a tiny bit of crit) but get spell hit instead. While you lose a small amount of survivability, It is obviously better in terms of raw dps and with hit becoming more of a concern in sunwell than in p3/p4 that isn't worthless at all. It's certainly better than the tiny bit of crit the extra int will give you. I'm not sure it's better than the sunwell staff though, so potentially scryers blade should be moved down rather than vengeful spellblade moved up.

This is mostly based on simming (https://wowsims.github.io/tbc/shadow_priest/) and it's totally possible that the sim is wrong, but if this list was made just totalling coefficients, i'd argue it's more likely the sim is accurate.
