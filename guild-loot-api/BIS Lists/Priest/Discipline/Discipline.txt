Priest Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/17
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Priest Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Priest Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Priest Healing Best in Slot Hub

Best In Slot Gear for Priest Healing DPS in Pre-Raid
Healer gearing always heavily depends on playstyle, situation, and raid environment, so treat the BiS list below as a guideline rather than a gospel. Trinkets, for example, are always situational, so you should have several and swap them based on the encounter.

Priest Healings looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Priest Healing Gems & Enchants
Should Priest Healing DPS Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Pre-Raid?
Although the Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith enchant is moderately better than Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle, the choice between The Aldor and The Scryers is fairly unimportant for healers, as both Zandalar Signet of Serenity Zandalar Signet of Serenity and Resilience of the Scourge Resilience of the Scourge are virtually identical to the Aldor enchant in terms of value.

The choice technically also affects whether Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration will proc a healing power buff or a direct heal on your target in Phase 5, but as you'll likely already have Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy by then, this is also not really impactful.

In conclusion, if you care about the slight stat advantage, you should opt for the Aldor, but if you'd like to choose the Scryers, you should go for it, as the shoulder enchant won't ever make a noticeable difference.

If you need to grind reputation with The Aldor or The Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Priest Healings, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Pre-Raid
Essence of the Martyr is both pre-raid and raid BiS in Phase 1. It's definitely the best way to spend your first batch of badges!

Scarab Brooch is a trinket hailing from Classic that has a scaling bonus, so in TBC it remains as strong as it was in Classic, with the absorb effect adjusting to updated output standards. Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon sees no changes in TBC and remains just as useful, with the bonus being percentage-based. Obviously, it should only be used if you run short on mana throughout the given encounter.

Priest Healing Phase 1 Best in Slot (BiS) - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/03/09
In this Pre-Raid Best-in-Slot Gear List for Priest Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic, we will list the recommended gear for Priest Healing to aim for when gearing up to raids in The Burning Crusade Classic, including gear sourced from Normal and Heroic Dungeons, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.


Priest Healing Best in Slot Hub
Best In Slot Gear for Priest Healing in Phase 1
Healer gearing always heavily depends on playstyle, situation, and raid environment, so treat the BiS list below as a guideline rather than a gospel. Trinkets, for example, are always situational, so you should have several and swap them based on the encounter.

Although Eye of Gruul Eye of Gruul isn't worth using on most Phase 1 encounters, it was included in the BiS list, since picking it up is important for the later phases. Phase 1 Priest BiS consists of two different sets, depending on whether or not you decided to go for the Primal Mooncloth set. The tailor set is by far the better one, but tends to be quite expensive to craft.

Healing Priests looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Healing Priest Gems & Enchants
Should Priest Healer Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 1?
Although the Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith enchant is moderately better than Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle, the choice between The Aldor and The Scryers is fairly unimportant for healers. It's worth mentioning that Karja's Medallion Karja's Medallion, which is a solid pre-bis option, is only available to Aldor players.

The choice technically also affects whether Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration will proc a healing power buff or a direct heal on your target in Phase 5, but as you'll likely already have Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy by then, this is also not really impactful.

In conclusion, if you care about the slight stat advantage, you should opt for the Aldor, but if you'd like to choose the Scryers, you should go for it, as the shoulder enchant won't ever make a noticeable difference.

Best in Slot Head Armor for Healing Priest in TBC Classic Phase 1
Light-Collar of the Incarnate is definitely the best choice in this slot, due to its great stats as well as the meta socket that allows you to gem Insightful Earthstorm Diamond.

If you do get unlucky on the tier token and have a lot of leftover badges, Cowl of Naaru Blessings is a decent alternative.
Whitemend Hood technically has the highest healing power, but due to the lack of a meta socket, it's generally considered worse than the previous two options. The main benefit is that it can be crafted before stepping into any raid without having to rely on the RNG of any sort.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Healing Priest in TBC Classic Phase 1
Trinkets depend on your playstyle and needs. For example, you might find you need better mana management or more healing output.

Essence of the Martyr is absolutely the best option for throughput. It has 133.5 average healing power, assuming the encounter is over right as the cooldown ends, meaning realistically, they can be worth even more than that on a given fight. It should always be your first BoJ purchase.

Eye of Gruul is a good option for any situation in which you are mainly casting Circle of Healing as the 2% proc chance is per target hit, not per cast. Void Reaver is a great example, as the trinket can typically be worth up to 130mp5 on average on this encounter, but it's also solid for most of the T5 trash and arguably even Leotheras and Morogrim. While that value is fairly unrealistic for most other fights, around 30 to 40% CoH usage is when EoG will generally provide more value than Blue Dragon, though you can also combine them, of course.

Other Trinket Recommendations

Priest Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 2 Gear Guide - TBC Classic
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/05/13
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Priest Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Priest Healing to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Priest Healing Best in Slot HubPriest Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Priest Healing in Phase 2
Healer gearing always heavily depends on playstyle, situation, and raid environment, so treat the BiS list below as a guideline rather than a gospel. Trinkets, for example, are always situational, so you should have several and swap them based on the encounter.

Other than that, gearing in Phase 2 is fairly straightforward as the majority of new items are direct stat upgrades over the Phase 1 options. The only thing you need to be aware of during your gear progression is that you should usually only break the Primal Mooncloth set bonus once you have acquired four pieces of Avatar Raiment.

Priest Healers looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Priest Healing Gems & Enchants
Should Priest Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 2?
Although the Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith enchant is moderately better than Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle, the choice between The Aldor and The Scryers is fairly unimportant for healers, as both Zandalar Signet of Serenity Zandalar Signet of Serenity and Resilience of the Scourge Resilience of the Scourge are virtually identical to the Aldor enchant in terms of value.

The choice technically also affects whether Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration will proc a healing power buff or a direct heal on your target in Phase 5, but as you'll likely already have Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy by then, this is also not really impactful.

In conclusion, if you care about the slight stat advantage, you should opt for the Aldor, but if you'd like to choose the Scryers, you should go for it, as the shoulder enchant won't ever make a noticeable difference.

Reputations Overview TBC Classic
Best in Slot Head Armor for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 2
Cowl of the Avatar is a very strongly itemized piece of gear that is both an improvement in sustain and in throughput compared to Light-Collar of the Incarnate. The gem bonus, which pairs well with Insightful Earthstorm Diamond Insightful Earthstorm Diamond, is just the cherry on top.

Powerheal 4000 Lens is somewhat worse than the Avatar Raiment alternative but is still worth crafting if you've already got Engineering, as it might take a while before it's your turn to receive tier tokens.

Crown of the Sun has a large amount of stats, but as it doesn't have a meta socket, it's not much more than a catch-up item.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 2

Burning Crusade Classic Priest Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 3 Gear Guide
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/14
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern for Priest Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Priest Healing to acquire while they progress during the second phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Tempest Keep and Serpentshrine Cavern, plus other pieces from previous phases like Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, Magtheridon's Lair, heroic dungeons, early PvP grinding, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

We have created many different best in slot lists, including a TBCC pre-patch list and a pre-raid list, containing gear you can obtain without doing any raid content.


Priest Healing Best in Slot HubPriest Healing Pre-Raid Best in Slot

Best In Slot Gear for Priest Healing in Phase 3
Healer gearing always heavily depends on playstyle, situation, and raid environment, so treat the BiS list below as a guideline rather than a gospel. Trinkets, for example, are always situational, so you should have several and swap them based on the encounter.

Phase 3 Priest BiS consists of two different sets. The difference between the sets is fairly small, so if you don't feel like swapping around often, you can just choose one and stick to it.

The T5 4P set will be the go-to for the majority of Phase 3, as T6 tokens are fairly contested, but both set bonuses being great makes this a super competitive option. Even once you have access to T6, it could potentially still be worth it to swap to this set on renew-heavy encounters since it's not unrealistic for the set bonuses to outperform the stat gains from the other set.

The T6 4P set has higher healing power and regen stats than T5 4P but significantly worse set bonuses. Depending on how much the Phase 3 fights favour Renew, either this set or the T5 4P will be the set of choice for encounters that primarily consist of free casting.

If you still have Tailoring, Swiftheal Mantle Swiftheal Mantle can be worth using on encounters in which you are chain-casting most of the time. This can be done with both sets, though it's better with T6 once you have access to it.

Priest Healers looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Should Priest Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 3?
Although the Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith enchant is moderately better than Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle, the choice between The Aldor and The Scryers is fairly unimportant for healers, as both Zandalar Signet of Serenity Zandalar Signet of Serenity and Resilience of the Scourge Resilience of the Scourge are virtually identical to the Aldor enchant in terms of value.

The choice technically also affects whether Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration will proc a healing power buff or a direct heal on your target in Phase 5, but as you'll likely already have Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy by then, this is also not really impactful.

In conclusion, if you care about the slight stat advantage, you should opt for the Aldor, but if you'd like to choose the Scryers, you should go for it, as the shoulder enchant won't ever make a noticeable difference.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Priest Healing, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

As it still provides roughly the same value as Direbrew Hops when you're having 4 Renews up at all times during an encounter, you'd need to consistently keep up even more than that for it to be worth using. If you average about 4.5/5/5.5 Renews rolling, the estimated value would be around 157/183/200 healing power on average, respectively. Realistically, this will only be the case if you are not specced into Circle of Healing.

Best in Slot Weapons for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 3
Crystal Spire of Karabor is by far the best Phase 3 weapon and should generally be your #1 priority if you're a CoH priest, since the only items you could potentially want to replace it with are the Kil'jaeden weapons.

The third equip effect can proc on any of the targets healed by multi-target healing spells such as Circle of Healing or Chain Heal, which is particularly great for priests since the CoH coefficient is fairly low. This means if you heal a party in which everyone is under 50% HP, on average, it'll be as if you had an additional 936 healing power for that cast, though, of course, that's a somewhat rare scenario.

While this effect provides slightly more value for CoH than for Chain Heal, it's best to simply give this weapon to your best shaman or priest first, and then the remaining ones. As it cannot proc off Heal over Time effects and isn't as useful on tank healers, Restoration Druids and Holy Paladins should ideally have lower priority on the weapon.

This proc is also affected by the talents Spiritual Healing, Gift of Nature, and Purification, which make it 198-242 for those three classes.

Apart from the Crystal Spire, Phase 3 adds a load of new weapons that are all fairly great in case you haven't gotten Lightfathom Scepter in the previous phase. Hammer of Atonement, Apostle of Argus, and Staff of Immaculate Recovery in particular should all be relatively uncontested.

Burning Crusade Classic Priest Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 4 Gear Guide
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/26
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 4 Zul'Aman for Priest Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Priest Healing to acquire while they progress during the fourth phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, and contains gear sourced from Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Priest Healing in Phase 4
Healer gearing always heavily depends on playstyle, situation, and raid environment, so treat the BiS list below as a guideline rather than a gospel. Trinkets, for example, are always situational, so you should have several and swap them based on the encounter.

Phase 4 Priest BiS consists of two different sets. The difference between the sets is fairly small, so if you don't feel like swapping around often, you can just choose one and stick to it.

The Tier 5 Avatar Raiment set is still the go-to for most Priests as the 4-piece set bonus amounts to a decent amount of additional healing and frees up global cooldowns, with Renew being one of our main filler casts. This set, of course, is also much easier to obtain than the alternative due to T6 tokens being heavily sought after.

The Tier 6 Vestments of Absolution set has higher healing power and regen stats than T5 4P but significantly worse set bonuses. Depending on how well you utilize Renew, these stat gains can realistically be outshadowed by the T5 bonuses, but this set is still a perfectly viable option. However, it's worth mentioning that, unlike the other set, you can swap to Swiftheal Mantle Swiftheal Mantle for higher haste without missing out on anything important, which is a big perk of the T6 setup.

Priest Healers looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Priest Healing Gems & Enchants
Should Priest Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 4?
Although the Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith enchant is moderately better than Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle, the choice between The Aldor and The Scryers is fairly unimportant for healers, as both Zandalar Signet of Serenity Zandalar Signet of Serenity and Resilience of the Scourge Resilience of the Scourge are virtually identical to the Aldor enchant in terms of value.

The choice technically also affects whether Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration will proc a healing power buff or a direct heal on your target in Phase 5, but as you'll likely already have Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy by then, this is also not really impactful.

In conclusion, if you care about the slight stat advantage, you should opt for the Aldor, but if you'd like to choose the Scryers, you should go for it, as the shoulder enchant won't ever make a noticeable difference.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Priest Healing, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 4
Essence of the Martyr remains the best option for throughput, and can be combined with Direbrew Hops on bosses for which you don't need any mana regeneration trinkets. They both have 133.5 average healing power, assuming the encounter is over right as the cooldown ends, meaning realistically they can be worth even more than that on a given fight.

Memento of Tyrande has 118 static healing power and 15.2/16.3/17.5 average MP5 at 24/30/40 cpm, respectively. Due to Direbrew Hops being available earlier than expected, it's a rather unimportant upgrade unless you missed out on the Brewfest. While it's certainly better in terms of value, the main use case for throughput trinkets is encounters on which you aren't struggling with mana at all, or at least not enough to warrant using a regeneration trinket, so having an additional 15.2 to 17.5 MP5 isn't really spectacular.

Eye of Gruul is a good option for any situation in which you are mainly casting Circle of Healing as the 2% proc chance is per target hit, not per cast. This means on encounters that primarily consist of CoH spam, the trinket can technically be worth up to 140-145 MP5 on average, therefore it's solid for the majority of the T6 trash, and also most T6 boss encounters. While that value is fairly unrealistic for most fights, around 30 to 40% CoH usage is when EoG will generally provide more value than Blue Dragon, though you can also combine them, of course.

Best in Slot Weapons for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 4
Crystal Spire of Karabor is still by far the best weapon in Phase 4, and should generally be your #1 priority if you're a CoH priest, since the only items you could potentially want to replace it with are the Kil'jaeden weapons.

The third equip effect can proc on any of the targets healed by multi-target healing spells such as Circle of Healing or Chain Heal, which is particularly great for priests since the CoH coefficient is fairly low. This means if you heal a party in which everyone is under 50% HP, on average, it'll be as if you had an additional 936 healing power for that cast, though, of course, that's a somewhat rare scenario.

While this effect provides slightly more value for CoH than for Chain Heal, it's best to simply give this weapon to your best shaman or priest first, and then the remaining ones. As it cannot proc off Heal over Time effects and isn't as useful on tank healers, Restoration Druids and Holy Paladins should ideally have lower priority on the weapon. This proc is also affected by the talents Spiritual Healing, Gift of Nature, and Purification, which make it 198-242 for those three classes.

Dark Blessing is easy to obtain, provides a decent amount of spell haste rating, and is your best alternative if you do not yet have the Crystal Spire.

Burning Crusade Classic Priest Healing Best in Slot (BiS) Phase 5 Gear Guide
By Wowhead
Updated: 2026/02/26
Welcome to Wowhead's Best in Slot Gear list from Phase 5 Sunwell Plateau for Priest Healing in The Burning Crusade Classic. This guide will list the recommended gear for Priest Healing to acquire while they progress during the fifth and final phase of The Burning Crusade Classic, which contains gear sourced from Sunwell Plateau, Zul'Aman, Black Temple, and Mount Hyjal, plus other pieces from previous phases like Serpentshrine Cavern, Tempest Keep, heroic dungeons, arena PvP Gear, professions, BoE World Drops, and reputations.

Best In Slot Gear for Priest Healing in Phase 5
Healer gearing always heavily depends on playstyle, situation, and raid environment, so treat the BiS list below as a guideline rather than a gospel.

Unlike the previous phases, the only slots that are really up for debate in Phase 5 are the profession-related items. While Enchanting and Engineering are still solid choices, Alchemy and Tailoring have some new crafted options that can make those two professions worth considering.

As the actual BiS is fairly straightforward, the only thing that's really arguable is how to gem it. Although there are many different ways to gem in Phase 5, opting for a +healing setup and downranking properly (CoH 4, Renew 10, FH8) will likely be the way to go for most players in Sunwell Plateau, as it provides more sustain than spirit-heavy gemming when used correctly and also allows for upranking when needed to match the throughput of haste-heavy gemming. However, if you're certain mana is absolutely not an issue, you can choose to primarily gem Quick Lionseye Quick Lionseye while activating your metagem through two purple gems.

The same concept holds true for trinket choices in Phase 5. Unlike the other healer classes, Priests have access to several incredibly strong sustain trinkets, such as Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon, Eye of Gruul Eye of Gruul, and potentially even Redeemer's Alchemist Stone Redeemer's Alchemist Stone, which is why it's going to be hard to justify using anything other than two regeneration trinkets until the content is on farm, and most likely even afterwards.

However, if you're playing Discipline, it's actually viable to keep using your four-piece Avatar Raiment in Sunwell. While you do miss out on a decent amount of haste and have lower healing on all other spells, the remaining stats are all fairly close to the ones of the regular set, and you gain 273/346/368 HPS on Renew 8/10/12, which is pretty significant as it's your most used spell. Another benefit is the fact that it's a lot easier to assemble, as most priests have the full T5 set by now, and the Sunwell cowl, shawl, and robes are all fairly close in value, so you can pick any of them as your off-piece.

Priest Healers looking to add Enchants and Gems to their BiS gear should take a look at our guide below:

Priest Healing Gems & Enchants
Should Priest Healing Choose Aldor or Scryer for BiS in TBC Classic Phase 5?
Although the Greater Inscription of Faith Greater Inscription of Faith enchant is moderately better than Greater Inscription of the Oracle Greater Inscription of the Oracle, the choice between The Aldor and The Scryers is fairly unimportant for healers, as both Zandalar Signet of Serenity Zandalar Signet of Serenity and Resilience of the Scourge Resilience of the Scourge are virtually identical to the Aldor enchant in terms of value.

The choice technically also affects whether Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration Shattered Sun Pendant of Restoration will proc a healing power buff (35 to 41 HEP) or a direct heal (11 to 13 raw HPS) on your target in Phase 5, but as you likely already have Brooch of Nature's Mercy Brooch of Nature's Mercy by now, this is also not really impactful.

In conclusion, if you care about the slight stat advantage, you should opt for the Aldor, but if you'd like to choose the Scryers, you should go for it, as the shoulder enchant won't ever make a noticeable difference.

If you need to grind reputation with the Aldor or the Scryers, or any of the other factions that offer BiS gear for Priest Healing, take a look at our guides to learn the fastest way to reach the rank you'll need.

Unless you've been lucky enough to already have two Blessed Bands at this point, you'll want to craft or buy a Ring of Flowing Life, since it's just as good as the Blessed Band and also BoE.

Signet of the Quiet Forest and Band of Lucent Beams are slightly worse versions of the Blessed Band, so they're decent placeholders.

Best in Slot Trinkets for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 5
Essence of the Martyr remains the best option for throughput, and can be combined with Direbrew Hops on bosses for which you don't need any mana regeneration trinkets. They both have 133.5 average healing power, assuming the encounter is over right as the cooldown ends, meaning realistically they can be worth even more than that on a given fight.

Memento of Tyrande has 118 static healing power and 15.2/16.3/17.5 average MP5 at 24/30/40 CPM, respectively. Due to Direbrew Hops being available earlier than expected, it's a rather unimportant upgrade unless you missed out on the Brewfest. While it's certainly better in terms of value, the main use case for throughput trinkets is encounters on which you aren't struggling with mana at all, or at least not enough to warrant using a regeneration trinket, so having an additional 15.2 to 17.5 MP5 isn't really spectacular.

Eye of Gruul is a good option for any situation in which you are mainly casting Circle of Healing as the 2% proc chance is per target hit, not per cast. This means on encounters that primarily consist of CoH spam, the trinket can technically be worth up to 140-145 MP5 on average, therefore it's solid for the majority of the T6 trash, and also most T6 boss encounters. While that value is fairly unrealistic for most fights, around 30 to 40% CoH usage is when EoG will generally provide more value than Blue Dragon, though you can also combine them, of course.

Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon is not quite as good as Eye of Gruul for CoH-heavy bosses, but still provides a decent amount of MP5 even when you are mainly casting single-target heals. With T6 BiS gear (assuming T5 4P set), it's worth 54/66/86 MP5 on average with 24/30/40 casts per minute, respectively, regardless of what spells you are casting specifically.

Redeemer's Alchemist Stone has 119 static healing power and averages out to about 40 MP5 if you're using potions on cooldown. While that's still not quite as much as BD or EoG regenerate, the fact that you don't have to rely on procs for the extra sustain, as well as the extra healing power on this trinket make it another great option in Phase 5.

Best in Slot Weapons for Priest Healing in TBC Classic Phase 5
Like in the previous phases, the new mainhand Hammer of Sanctification reigns supreme when combined with the Sunwell Plateau offhand drop. Keep in mind that it'll be heavily contested by all healers in your raid due to being a mace.

Golden Staff of the Sin'dorei is only slightly worse (~20 HEP) than the Kil'jaeden mace combination, and given it's a staff, it's most likely the Sunwell weapon we'll end up with. Despite that, it's still an amazing weapon and technically even better than the Hammer of Sanctification if you do not have the new offhand as well.

While Crystal Spire of Karabor is inferior to the two previously listed weapons when it comes to overall value, the fact that the proc happens precisely when the extra healing is needed makes it a much more competitive option than simple HEP comparisons make it out to be. This doesn't necessarily mean you shouldn't go for a Phase 5 weapon, but it's simply something to keep in mind when comparing the three options.
